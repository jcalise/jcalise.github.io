    Characters

    In your travels, you've met a number of interesting characters throughout the world. Some of them are detailed below.

    Elaydren Vown d'Cannith

    You first meet the woman who becomes your patron at the Broken Anvil Inn, a House Gallanda run establishment in the Callestan district of Lower Dura, Sharn. She has set you on the path of recovering a number of important Cannith relics for her. At first she claims to work for Baron Jorlanna d'Cannith of Cannith West, based in Aundair. Eventually she reveals to S.A.M., in order to secure his allegiance, that she is in fact a secret operative for Merrix d'Cannith, the Baron of Cannith South.

    She bears the Mark of Making is a spellcaster. Most times you've seen her she has work a dark-blue cloak.

    Elaydren has a townhouse, #19, located in Parlan Tower, Middle Central, Sharn. This was the last place you saw her before she sent you to Xen'drik to hunt down Garrow and return the items he stole after attacking the townhouse.

    Lucan and Grilsha Stellos

    Were central figures in a mission for Elaydren that took you to Zilargo and Thrane to prevent Garrow from obtaining a powerful relic, the Vampire Blade. Lucan was turned into a vampire by Calderus, the arch-vampire of Sharn, shortly after he was outed as one of their thralls by agents of the Emerald Claw. He fled the city with Grilsha providing cover and him traveling primarily in her Bag of Holding.

    In Zilargo, he tried to work with an old friend and rival to secure transport to Karrnath - where he was promised a new life by a secret operative of the Emerald Claw. His contact was playing both side and while she did arrange his papers she also reported him to her handler in the Royal Eyes of Aundair and laid a trap for him. Grilsha and Lucan managed to escape and get on the Lightning Rail bound for Thrane.

    Eventually, you were able to convince Lucan to give up the blade peacefully and he and Grilsha disappeared without telling you where they were going. Cligs believes there is a future love interest with Grilsha when they eventually reconnect.

    Brother Garrow

    This priest of the Blood of Vol and commander of the Order of the Emerald Claw has been a recurring antagonist. He is also seeking the schema that Elaydren has you looking for - he has even recovered one that she didn't know the location of. He goes about in the guise of a vampire and uses his magic to enhance this image of himself. You know that he is not a vampire but is in fact a Changeling and his shapeshifting abilities have allowed him to trick you on several occasions.

    Currently he is the one responsible for the attack on Elaydren's Town House and has stolen her schema, creation pattern, and notes and taken them to Xen'drik to hunt for the final piece of the puzzle. Exactly why he wants the schema is unclear but it is certain that the Emerald Claw acquiring such power can not happen and he must be stopped.

    The Lord of Blades

    The players have not encountered him directly but have run into several of his agents as they have been seeking the same objects and information. They have found and eliminated:

    Cutter, Waforged Ravager

    • She murdered Bonal Geldem and was attempting to recover the journal he was carrying that had information on the hidden schema.
    • The party faced and defeated her atop a skybridge connecting Dalannan Tower and the Kelsa Spire, in Upper Menthis just outside Morgrave University.
    • Her final messenger was destroyed

    Glaive

    • Glaive had been following Elaydren and then followed the party. His shifter allies heard that they were going into the sewers and so he set up an ambush for them there.
    • The party defeated him and his two feral shifter allies in the sewers below Sharn.
    • His final messenger was captured and read by S.A.M. revealing that another agent, Saber, was nearby.

    Saber

    • He went looking for Glaive having not heard back from him and found the entrance to the sewer and followed the trail left by the players into the Ruins of Dorasharn Tower.
    • The party faced him outside the ancient foundry and eliminated him.
    • His final messenger escaped

    Scimitar

    • You have not had direct contact with this agent but you know that they are on Garrow's expedition to Xen'drik.
