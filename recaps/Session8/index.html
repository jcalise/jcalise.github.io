    Session 8: 1-5 Olarune

    1 Olarune

    After successfully repelling the skeletons, the group heads to bed. Shortly after falling asleep Cassian begins to dream of a vast dark space and sees a large sack. He goes to investigate and upon opening the sack he is pulled in and begins to fall into a vast wilderness bathed in twilight. As he approaches the ground a castle appears below him and he descends into the courtyard, looking back to the sky it appears that the hole he fell through is now shining moonlight down upon the castle. Cassian makes his way through the courtyard towards a large set of doors at the other end, as he does he passes items that appear to be stacked all over the space. Some it appears are given places of prominence but others are stacked in piles; the items range from mundane toys and articles of clothing to weapons and armor of all sorts.

    Upon approaching the doors they open in on their own and Cassian is faced with a large throne room with a single individual sitting on a throne being bathed in light from an opening in the roof above. Kneeling before the figure, he calls out in a deep otherworldly voice and says "Welcome, Cassian, to the Castle of Lost Things." He introduces himself as the Forgotten Prince and tells Cassian that there is great potential in him and he can help unlock that power, if Cassian will help him find things he wants. Cassian agrees and the Prince teaches him a ritual that he can use once per day to send items that he wants to him in Thelanis.

    2 Olarune

    Land ho

    In the morning, the group awakens and has breakfast. The ship begins to make its way into the port of Rhukaan Draal around mid-morning. Before departing, the group meet with Eltarra once more to receive their reward. She gives them each 50gp and gives S.A.M. a letter of recommendation from House Lyrandar.

    Caelin decides to make their own investigations of the city and heads out with the Sovereign Host priest to check in at the local temple and do some digging.

    The Bloody Market

    The rest of the group make their way to the Bloody Market, a location similar to the Bazaar in Sharn but also completely different. Cornelius and Cassian make some purchases and the group searches for Failin, asking several of the merchants if they know of him or where he can be found. They are unsuccessful at the first few and start to head towards another when they hear from one of the stands a goblin woman shout "Cligsabethhhhhhhhhhh!" and then she comes running over to the group and grabs Cligs in a deep hug. S.A.M. tells Cligs' mom that they are traveling entertainment group and are passing through. Cligs' me-maw, Chulti, tells them that there is a tavern located just outside the Market that caters to non-goblinoids and that might be a good place to look for their associated, Failin. The group emphatically agrees, and Clig's reluctantly so, to join Chulti and Kenen, Cligs' pa-paw, at their home for dinner that evening.

    The Clenched Fist

    The group makes their way to the tavern that Chulti directed them to, the Clenched Fist, and S.A.M. speaks to the barkeep, Darna, and asks about a room for the evening for the group. She is able to offer them one room and the group pays her for this. S.A.M. then gives her an extra tip and asks about an ex-Orien individual they are supposed to be meeting for business but that they didn't know their name. She directs him to someone sitting alone in a dark corner drinking and says that his name is Failin.

    S.A.M. approaches the man, with Cornelius in tow, while Cligs and Cassian go to a table to drink and eavesdrop. S.A.M explains they want to go to Rose Quarry and Failin tells him he can take the group right now for 60gp per person, 40 up front. S.A.M. asks him to push their departure to morning and will add 10gp per person, which Failin agrees to. S.A.M. gives him 50gp to ensure that he shows up at dawn and then leaves and takes up a position outside to watch, Cornelius does the same. After about an hour, Failin leaves the tavern and heads off - neither follows him.

    Bugbear Business

    Shortly thereafter, three large and angry bugbears make their way into the tavern. They approach Darna and demand to know where Failin is. At first she says she doesn't know where he is but that he was here earlier and left. They get handsy and demand to know where Failin is and who he spoke to. Cligs attempts to attack them by casting Phantasmal Force and yelling "Fireball!" Perhaps luckily for her, this doesn't work and the bugbears dismiss her as a tiny goblin idiot. Darna finally says that Failin had spoken with a warforged, an uncommon sight in Darguun, and then left afterwards. Finally, they leave and on their way out spot S.A.M. still waiting across the way.

    The trio approach him and ask to know where Failin is, saying that he cheated them in a previous deal out of 50gp. S.A.M. is able to convince them that Failin owes him 1,000gp and has plans to meet him at the tavern tomorrow afternoon. He offers the bugbears 10gp to come and back him up at the meeting and collect their money - they agree.

    Awkarddddd

    The group heads out to meet Cligs' parents for dinner. Chulti is just as happy and open as she was in the market. Kenen seems distant, dismissive, and rude, especially to the two humans. The dinner starts off with the group attempting to embarrass Cligs with tales of their "traveling group" and the wonderful things Cligs can do, but the dinner quickly becomes awkward when Kenan starts talking about how "Darguun for Goblins" and then Chulti brings up it'll be the ten year anniversary soon of Cligs' sister's, Soozi's, death. S.A.M. begins to play a sad tune to avoid having to converse. The parents couldn't afford the magical healing that would have saved her life from House Jorasco and so she died. Chulti seems to have accepted this as the unfortunate way the world works and is trying to honor her daughters memory and support Cligs in her desires to learn. Kenen seems to have turned his anguish into hatred for all non-goblinoids, attending meetings about pushing to expel all the other common races out of their country. He has a special hatred for dragonmarked individuals. Cligs explains her own hatred for the dragonmarked and how the houses do not provide their services for free, but does not out Cornelius. Eventually, Kenen can not stand to be among the group and goes out to his forge to work (and drink). The rest of the dinner passes by awkwardly but without major incident. As they say goodbye, Cligs gives her mother 100gp and insists she keep it safe in case they need it in the future.

    Back at the tavern, Cligs drowns her feelings in ale, Cornelius heads to a corner to read, S.A.M. is trying to be supportive in a very S.A.M. like way, and Cassian has snuck up to their room to play with a figurine he bought in the Market. He attempts to use the ritual the Forgotten Prince taught him to send the elvenesque toy to him as an offering. He does the ritual as instructed and when he is done he feels a coldness in his hand and a swirl of frost encircle the figure but nothing else happens, it remains in his hand.

    3 Olarune

    Dawn arrives as does Caelin and Failin. Before heading out, S.A.M. gives Darna a letter for the bugbears for when they show up later that day. It tells them that he went chasing Failin to Korranberg. Failin guides them to a stone outcropping not far from the tavern and presses his dragonmark to the stone, after he does so his elemental land cart emerges from the stone and you all board.

    The first day of travel to Rose Quarry passes by without incident. Failin gives you all the information he knows about the old village, which isn't much:

    • It used be a Cyran village before and during the war but now is in Darguul territory
    • It was a House Cannith processing facility for red marble found there
    • The primary residents were dwarves brough in from the Mror Holds to work the mine
    • It used to provide most of the Cannith marble used across Khorvaire

    The first night also passes without incident. Cornelius creates a Homunculus Servant to aid in keeping watch, a mechanical snail he calls Snailor Swift.

    4 Olarune

    Again, the day largely passes by without incident or much new information being learned. Cornelius tries to befriend Failin by showing him his dragonmark but this doesn't seem to have the desired effect. Failin also shares more about his business, which is largely hunting for relics of the Dhakaani Empire and selling them (or knockoffs).

    5 Olarune

    Cornelius asks Cligs about Cassian's magic, since he was sick for most of the journey to Darguun and didn't know much about it. Cligs shares what she saw and knows about Cassian's magic but more importantly, she shares her concerns that his magic is coming from an evil entity and that likely Cassian will kill them all at some point.

    S.A.M. makes further conversation with Failin and convinces the man to give him the names of some House Cannith contacts he has had dealings with in the past.

    Looks like meats back on the menu...

    On the third evening, Cassian attempts the ritual to send items to Thelanis once again, this time trying to send a pretty rock he found. As before, the ritural complets and he feels coldness in his hands and sees a swirl of frost around the rock - but it remains.

    The group sets their usual wards: Cligs sets an Alarm spell and Cornelius has Snailor Swift keeping watch. In the middle of the night Snailor tries to wake Cornelius after it spots something by making him feel a wet trail across his face but he remains asleep. Eventually the creature triggers Cligs' spell and she is awoken to the sight of a giant boar. The cloud klaxon also wakes the rest of the group. Cligs attempts to put the boar to sleep so that they can deal with the situation peacefully but Cornelius has other ideas in mind - meaty ideas. The artificer attacks the board as it's under Cligs Sleep influence which interrupts the spell and angers the boar. Eventually, the group has no choice but to finish the animal off.
