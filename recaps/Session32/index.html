    Session 32: 2-4 Lharvion

    2 Lharvion

    In the four weeks since returning from Xen'drik, the crew accomplished:

    • Cligs spent time learning Primordial and can now successfully or rat skewers in the elemental language (4 of 7 weeks).
    • Cornelius took Evine on dates and enlisted the aid of House Cannith artificers to create an Amulet of Health.
    • Cassian put 350gp into learning more about the Forgotten Prince, finding out about the Prince's connection with the Kenku and discovering a number of instances where wars were lost and cities had fallen due to actions of his agents. Cassian also made two contacts in Cannith South.

    During their regular meeting at the Gray Dragon, on a particularly hot and humid night, a trio of guards enter wearing House Cannith livery and approach. The guards inform them that they are summoned to the Cannith Citadel in Dragon Towers to meet with Elaydren, at once. They, somewhat begrudgingly, agree and follow the guards, who have arranged a skycoach to take them to the Middle Central district.

    Upon reaching the Citadel, they are escorted to a lift that takes them up and up, to the very top of the tower. When they arrive, they see a large room that is both a meeting room and also seems to be a workshop. At the large stone table are three people:

    • Lady Elaydren,
    • A strange, pale looking gnome, and
    • Merrix d'Cannith

    Introductions are made and the group finds out that this is Auric, a Sivis wizard that Elaydren has recruited to aid the party with S.A.M. and Caelin off on vision quests. Merrix cuts off the pleasantries and wants to get down to business, he snaps his fingers and the letter they recovered from Garrow appears floating on the table, with green emanations coming from portions of it. Together, Elaydren and Merrix cover that over the last month they have spent time cracking the code of this letter and have learned some information from it:

    • Zorlan d'Cannith, Merrix's cousin and rival Baron of Cannith East has seemingly allied himself with the Order of the Emerald Claw.
    • They believe that Zorlan has uncovered Red Knight, a rumored forgehold that no one knew the location of, believed to be the private workshop of Starrin, the former head of a unified Cannith who died during the Day of Mourning.
    • The letter indicates that they are using Zorlan's expertise, Emerald Claw necromancy, and whatever they have uncovered at Red Knight to create some sort of new weapon.

    Merrix shares that he cannot move openly against Zorlan as it could lead to a Cannith civil war and bring The Twelve down upon them. He has asked the team to find Red Knight, infiltrate it, find evidence to prove what Zorlan is doing, and to disrupt whatever he working on. When the party agrees, with the promise of being adequately rewarded, he offers them 2,500gp to purchase supplies, the use of his private airship for the duration of the mission, and three magic items to aid them: a ring of spell storing, an artifice dagger, and an aegis of the creation forge.

    Leaving the meeting, Cligs and Cassian head to the tavern to get a head start on drinking. Auric and Cornelius go to the Cannith Library and check-out some books that they believe will be helpful on the Emerald Claw and Cannith research facilities. When they reconnect at the tavern they discuss their plan and supplies that they need for the journey. They settle on heading to Korth and going to Redhearth, a secret location they learned about at Rose Quarry. Auric and Cligs head to Olladra's Arms to seek a Cleric or Paladin to teach them the spell Spirit Shroud. Cornelius and Cassian go to purchase the supplies they talked about.

    3 Lharvion

    The party makes their way to Lyrandar Tower to connect with the captain of Merrix's ship, The Esther. She is a khoravar (half-elf) veteran of the Last War named Ressa d'Lyrandar. When they board, she introduces them to her first mate, a human named Dorren. During their first day of travel the party gets up to:

    • Cligs spends time trying to woo the captain (badly) and observing how she and the crew pilot the ship.
    • Cassian has a conversation with Dorren, who claims to have served Cannith for 30 years but has never met Merrix before. He is very pro-Cannith.
    • Auric is excited by being out in the world and spends most of the day unpacking all of their books and scrolls.
    • Cornelius smokes his pipe and studies the designs for his armor he is making.

    The captain explains that they must stop each evening to allow the elemental to recharge/calm down. They stop for the first night in Wroat, the capitol of Breland. They make their way a chain tavern that Cassian knows about, called "The Brass Wolf." They order the staples of the establishment. Cligs makes an illusory S.A.M. who performs with Cassian to overwhelming applause and tips. They pay for their food and leave the rest of the money they raised for the staff.

    4 Lharvion

    On their second day of travel, initially they are doing:

    • Auric is trying to understand and practice navigation, looking at the maps and comparing it to the surroundings - and being confused.
    • Cornelius is again studying
    • Cassian is taking in the sites but around midday notices that they seem to be off course from where the captain had indicated they were heading, Cragwar. They seem to be heading directly toward The Blackcaps instead of between them and The Greenhaunt.

    Cligs is trying and fails again to woo the captain but then starts investigating her. She observes what appears to be a tense conversation between her and another khoravar crew member animatedly pointing at maps. Unfortunately, they are speaking in khoravese and Cligs doesn't understand. She goes to grab Auric and has him use Comprehend Languages and eavesdrop on the conversation. Auric learns, and passes on to Cligs, that they seem to be off course and it is unclear how this happened. Cligs and Auric go to find the others and they chat in one of their cabins about the information.

    Cligs suspects that one of the humans may be working with Cannith East, and sends Lizzard McGuire into the Dorren's cabin to snoop about for information. While there, she finds a letter that had been sealed with a Cannith signet ring. Lizzard manages to get the letter out and sees that it is a personal letter from Merrix to Dorren, entrusting him with some sort of secret cargo. Lizzard is unable to get the letter back in the envelope, so Cligs has her bring it to her and destroys it.

    While Cligs is entranced dealing with Lizzard, a knock on the door draws the attention of the rest of the group. Another crew member, a human named Petrin, has been sent by the captain to retrieve them. Petrin gives off ex-military vibes and seem unbothered by Cornelius' questioning but doesn't reveal much. He leads them to the captain and then leaves, she is accompanied by the khoravar that Auric and Cligs saw before and another human they have not seen previously.

    The captain explains to the party that they are off course but doesn't give an explanation as to why. She asks their permission to alter course so as not to tax the elemental or the crew and instead continue on this course and stop at Drum Keep for the evening. They will be able to rest the elemental and crew but the party will need to remain on board. This will add about a half day to their journey and require a stop in Passage. The party agrees and when they turn to go the three people begin speaking in khoravese, since Auric still has Comprehend Languages up he is able to interpret the conversation. The human is asking the captain and the navigator why they didn't tell the party about the sabotage and the captain responds that they don't need to know that, only that they are off course.

    Auric turns to challenge them and ask what they said. The captain is able to brush off their concern and Auric doesn't press the issue for the moment or reveal that they understood what she she said. Back in the cabin, the bring Cligs up to speed and decide to act. Cassian and Cornelius decide to go question the human, who ultimately turns out to be Corwyn d'Cannith, the elemental binder of the crew. They good cop/bad cop him into revealing the nature of the sabotage and that their navigation equipment was subtly damaged to cause them to drift off course. He ensures them that no one but him and his assistant are the only ones who have been in the elemental engine since they have left Sharn.

    Auric and Cligs set out to go explore the the cargo hold and see if they can find the secret cargo mentioned in the letter to Dorren. They hunt through the cargo hold and find three candidates: a locked chest with a Cannith seal painted on it, a barrel that is wrapped in metal bands, and an unadorned metal box. Cligs believes they should start with the metal box and casts Dispel Magic on it, when she does this a recessed Cannith seal, small enough that a signet ring could be pressed into it, appears but she can still not open it. She has an idea! Auric casts Silence to cover their immediate area in magical quietness and then, using her new Artifice Dagger, Cligs casts Knock on the chest causing it to pop open.
