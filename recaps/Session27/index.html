    25 Dravago

    Having decided on a rough plan to approach the site of the air-skiff from the north and scout out, the group lands from The Marlow about 1 mile downriver and they set out on foot. Approaching to within 100 ft of the air-skiff, Caelin can see that there appears to only be a handful of camp rolls setup but can't see any actual people. Cligs ritual casts a new Familiar, Meerkat the Macaw, and has her circle the area above the vessel returning to show Cligs that there are 2 Emerald Claw sentries polishing their swords up in the skiff and footsteps leading off further south and a third armored sentry about 200 ft upriver.

    The group decides that Caelin and Cligs will sneak up into the air-skiff, while S.A.M. climbs into the stone giant hand to provide cover, Cornelius will hold under the air-skiff to provide support, and Cassian will be ready to Misty Step up to the air-skiff. Surprisingly, this all goes off without a hitch. They eliminate two of the sentries and capture a third for questioning. The sentry reveals that the Lyrandar Airship is hidden in the jungle some ways away and that only House Lyrandar crew remain about. Approximately 50 soldiers of the Emerald Claw, plus a warforged, left from here to head into the jungle some time ago (no one asked how much head start). The sentry didn't know exactly what the mission was but that Garrow, his vampire commander, has several important relics in his possession and is seeking another.

    They press the sentry to reveal the location of the airship, but after hearing several threats to his life decides his best bet of staying alive is to not tell them and only show them. Caelin decides they are better off without this loose end and executes the prisoner. They feel a sudden chill, almost as if Aureon is making their displeasure at such an action known.

    Cligs gets her wish....for now

    Overjoyed with what seems like a free air-skiff, Cligs lays claim to the vessel and wants to make sure the group don't destroy it or do anything to ruin her prize. She is able to successfully land the air-skiff in the sand and by guiding Cornelius they are able to remove and secure the Khyber dragonshard that powers this vessel. Cligs hasn't thought through how she will get this home or what House Lyrandar will do when they realize their vessel is captured. That's a tomorrow problem.

    Into the jungle

    They can see the path that Garrow's party made through the jungle and decide to follow this. They travel for about an hour when Caelin notices a skull stuck into the ground that appears to have the Drow markings that Quire had shown them indicate that this area is claimed by Chief Amoxtli - they proceed with caution. After not much longer they break into a clearing in the jungle that is dominated by a massive stone structure rising some 400 feet into the air and appearing to be made of solid stone.

    Approaching the structure they can see two sets of stairs, one massive that would have been too large even for the ancient giants and a narrower set. The narrow set of steps show the aftermath of a recent battle that shows to corpses of many Drow and a smaller number of Emerald Claw soldiers. Cornelius sends Snailor Swift on a scouting mission around the outside of the structure, she is able to show Cornelius that on the upper levels of the structure there appear to be 4-armed creatures roaming the outside ledges. She also shows him that there are a number of Drow hiding on the stairs that lead deeper into the structure.

    S.A.M. decides to play a mournful version of the drums that he had heard on their way here in an effort to mollify their potential ambushers. After a few minutes of playing, a sole Drow descends the stairs with his weapons drawn and begins to try to communicate with them in a broken but intelligible version of Common. The Drow, who is named Balmatzoc, explains how the Emerald Claw attacked them and destroyed their homes with fire. He questions them about their intentions and the group manages to convince him that they are here to destroy the Emerald Claw and not to interfere with the Drow. Balmatzoc agrees not to kill them and accepts their help in dealing with the intruders. He provides them some information about the structure and the Emerald Claw, who have all gone to the upper levels.

    Into Darkness

    The party ascends a staircase to the second level of the structure. When they get closer to the top they can see torches flickering and can hear the voices of several people working to open a door at the top of the stairs with some sort of machine. The party gets the drop on a squad of Emerald Claw soldiers and manages to eliminate them. Cornelius figures out how to work the winch that the soldiers had brought and with Muroni's help is able to operate it to pry the doors open. Unfortunately, this unleashes a torrent of waters that sends several of the party tumbling down the stairs. S.A.M is able to save Cornelius from taking too much damage but Snailor Swift is lost in the water.

    They begin to explore this space which is completely obscured in darkness without their own light sources. The room is filled with water up to 2 ft high that is constantly running and flowing out down the stairs now that the doors are opened. There are three main structures that the group can see, two large columns on either side and a massive ziggurat ahead of them. The column on the right side features a portal that when they look through it shows a massive expanse of water as far as they can see as well as what looks like water elementals in the distance - Cornelius is able to throw an object through this that appears to splash in the water on the other side. The opposite column has a similar portal showing the expanse of water but is where the water is flowing in from.

    They turn their attention to the ziggurat and find that almost every surface of it is inscribed with runes of some sort that no one can seem to make out. The only face that isn't is one side of the very top level of the ziggurat where each of the other three sides only have one rune. Caelin and Phineas are able to make out that they are some sort of Giant language but neither appears able to read them - not because of a language issue but something is obscuring their ability to read the runes as if they are magically encrypted. S.A.M. explores the rest of the cavernous room and in talking with Phineas they determine that this room was setup for some form of experiments but something has damaged the second portal that prevents the water from leaving.

    The group reconvenes at the base of the ziggurat and is going to set off for another section of the structure when they all feel as though the air becomes thick and wet and a feeling of drowning overcomes them, even though the water still on reaches their ankles.
