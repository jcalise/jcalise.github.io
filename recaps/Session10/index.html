    Session 10: 6-7 Olarune

    Olarune 6

    Having "escaped" from Garrow and the Emerald Claw soldiers, the group is weighing their options for reaching Whitehearth now that they have it's location. Remembering some details about traveling the Mournland from their reading, Cornelius relays to the group how dangerous it could be to travel there on foot. The group decides to wait the 19 hours until Failin returns for their meetup and than convince him to take them there in the land cart.

    S.A.M. suggests hiding in the abandoned quarry for shelter. Cligs shares with the group that there is little presence of civilization out this far, so it's possible that there could be dangerous creatures or even roving bands of unfriendly goblinoids, so they should be careful and set a watch.

    The group travels down one of the access ramps into the quarry and finds the first open work tunnel. They do a quick check and find the remnants of people that fled quickly and left the work that they were doing. The place otherwise seems untouched since the disaster that took out the town four years ago.

    They decide to take two sets of long rests to ensure everyone is rested but that a watch is maintained. Caelin and Cornelius took up the first watch, with S.A.M. semi-resting and being on alert as well. During the watch there is a giant spider that approaches the group but Caelin is able to talk to it and convince it to go away. S.A.M. takes over towards the end of the watch and before the second watch comes on, he doesn't see anything interesting and there are no incidents. Cligs, Cassian, and SAM take the second watch and the time passes by without issue.

    Olarune 7

    One way or another, we're getting on that land cart

    Before heading out to meet with Failin, S.A.M. tells the group if they can't convince Failin to take them into the Mournland with money than they may need to force him to take them. Some of the group seem resistant to this but ultimately it's decided that at this point this is their only option to get there before Garrow and safely.

    The conversation with Failin doesn't go well at first. He is adamant that it's time to retun to Rhukaan Draal and doesn't want to continue. S.A.M. tries to convince him and ultimately tries to threaten him, but Failin believes he holds the advantage because they can't make the cart work without him. Caelin takes over the negotiation in Thieves Cant and is eventually able to convince Failin to take them, for 10 pp per rider. He also tells them that at the first sign of trouble for him or his cart, he will turn back. Cornelius offers Failin the Olladra's Chalice as a downpayment and for a potential discount. He doesn't negotiate well but Failin gives them a 1 pp discount off the deal but still charges them 9pp per person, payment upfront.

    On the way there Cligs tries to apologize to Failin. SAM takes this opportunity to send messages to the others about what needs to happen when they get to Whitehearth. He is adamant that Failin can not be left alone and unsecured while they venture in or he will leave them. They need to ensure he is there to take them back. The others seem to agree.

    Bad, worse, and worst

    After about 30 minutes of travel, they make it to the wall of mist that completely encompasses what once was the jewel of Galifar. Entering the mists they notice that sound feels muffled and distant and a feeling of isolation and dread builds in all of the non-Warforged party members. Luckily, they are on the land cart so they are able to quickly traverse the danger and make it through.

    Traveling a short distance into the Mournland, they encounter swirling black mists that seem to descend from the sky and cover a large area - impossible to avoid. They make their way into the Doldrum Storm and they begin to hear hissing whispers and feel lethargic and listless. S.A.M., hearing the whispers but unaffected by it, decides to play music - a riot of noises to drown out the whispers around them. He does this until they pass through the Storm and once on the other side of it almost everyone is able to quickly shake the feeling. Cligs and Failin aren't so lucky and suffer from exhaustion as a result.

    Continuing on their way, they enter an area of the Mournland that seems like the ground is constantly moving and shaking, as if a constant earthquake is happening. Luckily, again, the land cart protects them from the effects of this and they begin to pass through the region. Eventually they see masses of large worms poking out of the land, trying to probe the cart and see what is in it's land. Cornelius tosses some of his boar meat out a distance to see what happens and notices that it is quickly swallowed up by a mass of worms. As he does it and is reaching over, the mound of worms below forms into a makeshift hand and grabs him up from the cart as it's giant body, fully made of tens of thousands of worms, rises out of the ground. The group is able to free Cornelius and decides the best way to get away from this danger is to toss it the rest of the boar meat, giving it ample food to distract it as they get away.

    Further down the "road" they are traveling, the come upon a curious site. They see several corpses strewn about that don't seem to be decayed at all. They also see two green bubbles floating around in what clearly seems like they are playing. At one point one of them does a dive and seems to spit out a human head, completely cleaned of flesh. Cligs studies these creatures and determines that they seem to be a living version of a Acid Splash. She also tests a theory by making an illusion close to them, which they quickly attack. They don't seem to notice the part though, and seeing what they can do the group decides it's best to leave them be.

    Awhile later, the group rides into the remains of a massive battle. Like the others, they appear freshly killed, despite clearly being much older—they can easily identify Brelish and Cyran soldiers who were locked in battle. Among the sea of dead soldiers is a pile of other corpses—halflings, elves, and their mounts. While there is no smell of decay, many of the bodies are mutilated: missing limbs, heads, or sliced in half. The group decides to spend a half hour searching here and casting about with Detect Magic to see what can be found amongst the dead. They recover a number of mundane item, but also several magic ones and money. As they are returning to the land cart, they are ambushed by a giant crab that seems to be made up of dead bodies and equipment. They are able to defeat this creature and get back on the road.

    Finally, they approach a series of low hills and see what looks like at first glance to be an abandoned mine shift. It's a tunnel into the hill that is framed with timbers. Upon closer inspection, they see the symbol of House Cannith emblazoned on the crossbeam - Whiteheath.
