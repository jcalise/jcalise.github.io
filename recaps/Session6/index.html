    Session 6: 5-27 Zarantyr

    5 Zarantyr

    Having stopped the ritual and saved Drevan, the group is ready wrap up this case - maybe. Drevan offers them a reward for their service and they escort him home to collect and ensure he gets there safely. Upon returning to his manor in Upper Northedge they find that Nora, his wife, is gone. S.A.M. and Caelin do some investigation and it seems like some of her clothing is missing but the servant who saw her leave said she wasn't carrying any luggage but was wearing a traveling cloak.

    Having felt they have exhausted their options with Drevan at the moment, they accept their rewards, a letter of recommendation and 250gp, and head out to hunt down Max for more information. They encourage him to seek out Sgt. Germaine Vilroy to take on additional protection for now.

    Where is Max?

    Not knowing where Max lives, or if he has an office, they head back to the Black Dryad to see if he is there or if anyone has seen him. He isn't there when they arrive and questioning people they find out the following:

    • The bartender said he was here drinking for a bit and then left a few hours ago
    • They question a couple at another table and are able to discern that the lady seems to know more about him then she lets on but they aren't able to figure out what.
    • No one knows where he lives or if he has an office.

    They decide to see if they can find him by asking the City Watch for his information. S.A.M. approaches a nearby guard and asks about the location of Max Murlock. Coincidentally, the guard tell them that they have recently taken Max into custody. He sends them to the station that has Max, which is the one they know Vilroy works at.

    Vilory to the Rescue

    They head to meet up with Vilroy who tells them that she received their message about the crime and had been looking into it. Apparently Max had already been on their radar and when we indicated we were working with him she went to find him. He was found, somewhat drunk and teary, outside of a Kundarak Bank. They ask her permission to interview him and to recover their fees, which she agrees to.

    Max tells them that he had received a message from Nora to meet one of her servants who had her luggage and to meet her at the bank. He did so and waited while she went inside and was gone for a bit. When she came out she said that she had what she needed and was going to flee the city. Max thought he was going with her but she turned him down, paid him for his service, and left him there.

    They recovered their fee, and Caelin's new hat, and went about their business. The group agreed to meet up once a week to check for messages at the House Sivis station in Barmin Tower.

    6 - 26 Zarantyr

    During this time span each group member completes some tasks:

    Caelin:

    • Finds a crossbow expert to train them in the hand crossbow, gaining proficiency with that weapon for a cost of 25gp per week.
    • Studies the schema and journal that they had retained from Bonal and is able to learn more about it, discovering that like what they recovered for Elaydren this is part of a larger creation pattern. They are unable to discern what the pattern might be for without more pieces of it.

    Cligs:

    • She heads back to work at the Gray Dragon, earning her daily rate of 5sp per day.
    • She also spends her evenings working on mastering the art of lock picking, breaking a number of lock picks along the way to earning proficiency and costing her 125gp.
    • She also spends 100gp to imbue the obsidian dagger as a +1

    Cassian:

    • Sends a message to his parents updating them on all that has happened since arriving in Sharn.
    • Also goes to Nibbur's shop in Middle Dura to finalize his suit which Nibbur sells to him for $100gp.

    S.A.M.:

    • Modifies Cassian's message to ensure the narrative doesn't worry his parents.
    • Purchases a trumpet and a lyre for 100gp.

    Cornelius:

    • Having a new found bloodlust and appreciation for weapons and armor dives headfirst in to the world of House Cannith making and crafts a set of splint armor for themselves.

    27 Zarantyr

    The group meets up several weeks later for their regular check in at the message station. It's once again raining in Sharn. Approaching the Message Station they notice that the door is hanging open in it's frame, closer inspection shows that the station has been ransacked and the gnome working there appears to have been knocked out.

    Examining the station, and talking to Jarina, they are able to uncover the following:

    • They identify a set of warforged footprints on a piece of parchment
    • They see several pairs of clawed footprints in the spilled ink that appear to belong to kobolds
    • There are sling stones scattered about the room
    • A message had come from Lady Elaydren, but Jarina didn't know what it contained.

    That's a big bird

    Heading back outside and trying to make their way to the Cannith enclave to find Elaydren, the group is approached by a giant owl that is carrying a satchel. It circles them for a moment and then drops a scroll case near them. Caelin grabs it, breaks open the unmarked seal and finds the following message:

    "The normal means of communication  seems to have been compromised, so I have  sent this special messenger to personally  ﬁnd  you  and  deliver  this  letter.
You served me once before, and now I need your help again. Time is short. I feel  that our enemies have discovered me and  are  closing  in.
Meet  me  at  the  Broken  Anvil  tavern,  where we ﬁrst discussed heirlooms and job  opportunities.  Act  quickly,  for  I  believe  that we are all in terrible danger."

    They head out to meet up with Elaydren at the inn.

    Fancy meeting you here

    Heading back to the Broken Anvil once again, they enter to find that it appears there is only one customer. As they walk in the person, dressed in dirty clothes and looking disheveled, raises their hands as if to cast a spell. Pausing as they seem to recognize them, they pull their hood back and it is Elaydren.

    She hurries them over and tells them that they have no time to chat. She puts a backpack on the table and tells them that everything they need is in the backpack, including instructions in a letter in the left-hand pocket and to take it and go. As she tries to encourage them to leave, the door bursts open and another agent of the Lord of Blades, this one named Cutlass, enters with 5 kobold goons in tow.

    The enemies attempt to make their way to Elaydren but the party positions themselves, mostly, between her and them. They are able to quickly dispatch the kobolds and eventually eliminate Cutlass, also managing to intercept his Final Messenger.

    Once they are safe, Elaydren again conveys that time is of the essence and that they must leave immediately before more assassins show up. She doesn't wait for any more questions and leaves. S.A.M. follows her lead and begins to leave, urging the others to leave at once and not delay. They start heading for the Lightning Rail station at Terminus while Cligs investigates the pack and reads the letter.

    One if by land, two if by sea

    Reading the letter they realize that they need to head into Darguun to an old Cannith outpost called Rose Quarry, to find the hidden location of a secret Cannith Workshop in the Mournland and schema. Knowing that they need to head to her homeland, Cligs tells the group that they can either take an overland route that would take over a week to get there or they can travel by ship in about 4 days. The group quickly settles on the sea route and makes for the ports at the base of the Sharn cliffs so that they can make their way to Rhukaan Draal on a House Lyrandar galleon.
