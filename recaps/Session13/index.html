    Session 14: 11 - 13 Eyre

    11 Eyre

    As Cassian falls asleep, he once again finds himself feeling the now familiar sensation of falling into the courtyard of the Castle of Lost Things. As he makes his way to the throne room he sees many new items strewn about, but the one that catches his eye is Olladra's Chalice on a podium bathed in moonlight. Meeting with the Prince this time, Cassian is more confident. The Prince thanks him for his service and hopes that they can continue their partnership. As promised, he gives Cassian more power, touching his shoulder he can feel coldness radiating through his body and then, suddenly, tendrils of ice flare out in all directions. The Prince says "Use it well," and as he lifts his hand Cassian finds himself back in a dream about Arcanix where he is giving a presentation but he has no pants on and his teeth are falling out.

    An intervention...but a bad one

    Cligs decides it's time to lay everyone else's cards on the table and so invites Cassian to dinner at the tavern she works at and also send messages to S.A.M. and Caelin telling them that Vilroy has a job for them and to meet at the same tavern, slightly later. When Cassian arrives Cligs presses him that they need to tell S.A.M. about his nature and what they uncovered in the scroll. She also confronts him about his "demon" that he seems to be listening to more and more and how that could be dangerous. Cassian is more open about the Forgotten Prince but doesn't reveal all. He also reluctantly agrees to talk to S.A.M. about what they found out.

    When the others arrive, Cligs informs them that this is an intervention for both S.A.M. and Cassian. She dives into spilling the information she learned about S.A.M and what she believes about Cassian. A lengthy, awkward, conversation ensues in which S.A.M. states that he is aware of jos nature but isn't bothered by it and doesn't understand why the others would be concerned. Caelin reveals the nature of Cassian's patron as an Archfey. Everyone agrees that they want to work towards more self-determination for the Warforged and the possibility of creating more.

    As they conversation dies down, a strange figure throws open the doors of the tavern. Taking up most of the frame is a large creature that appears made of stone, with massive wings. As it enters the tavern, most patrons pay it no mind - gargoyles, while not common, are not unknown in the city. It approaches the table and in a voice that sounds like rocks smashing together says "S.A.M.?" When S.AM. affirms his identity, the creature hands him a letter and then departs. It is from Elaydren and simply states:

    "My friends,

    Meet me at Ertur's Shield, Sword Point, Middle Central tomorrow. The time to strike back is at hand.

    Your patron, Lady E"

    On the walk back to their quarters, Cassian and S.A.M. have a tense exchange around the information that was revealed at the table. In no uncertain terms, S.A.M. threatens Cassian that if he tells his family about S.A.M.s nature and loyalties, S.A.M. will reveal knowledge to the family that will outcast Cassian. Cassian asks him to stay elsewhere that night.

    12 Eyre

    When they meet at the Ghallanda-run tavern the next day, a young halfling server escorts them back to a private room that has Elaydren and a mysterious Elven woman sharing a bottle of wine. The server takes their orders and leaves, returning a few minutes later with their drinks and an array of food. Elaydren waits until he leaves to begin speaking.

    She tells them that since they returned she has been preparing to strike back at the Emerald Claw and that her House Thuranni associate has collected information on Garrow's plans and now is the time to strike. She asks them if they will take the mission before proceeding further. When they finally agree, she tells them that Garrow has been seeking magic artifacts from deep under Sharn, one of these was a sword in the possession of an ancient vampire that has since seem to gone missing. She asks the Elf to explain more. Elaydren refuses to reveal her identity, and she doesn't offer one, so S.A.M. suggests giving her the codename "Nightingale". She reveals the following information - after the first she is able to produce a lifelike image of them using illusion magic:

    • Calderus. Ancient vampire. Based in the undercity of Sharn. Has agents throughout the city.
    • Lucan Stellos. Citadel agent in Sharn. Eight days ago multiple anonymous letters were sent to various officials. They revealed Stellos to be under the control of Calderus. Apparently, unwillingly.
    • Edvin Garm. Emerald Claw agent in Sharn. Eight days ago met with Stellos and passed him a letter. This occurred approximately an hour before Lucan Stellos was outed. Likely responsible for outing Stellos, but that is unconfirmed. Stellos disappeared within hours.
    • Garrow. Goes by “Brother” Garrow. Vampire. Not ancient. Priest of the Blood of Vol. Agent of the Emerald Claw. Was spotted in Korranberg six days ago. Nights, rather. Some Emerald Claw activity still going on there.
    • Grilsha Stellos. Lucan’s sister. Raised him. Very close. Protective. Lives in Ivy Towers. Magically talented.
    • Lucan reappeared the night before last. Seen at the residence of his sister. He had a magic sword in his possession. It matched the description of the one Garrow was studying. The vampire blade. Both Lucan and Grilsha disappeared that night. Last night, both of their residences were searched by agents of Calderus.

    Elaydren will fill in the rest that they think Lucan and his sister are alive and on the run, likely to meet Garrow in Korranberg. She wants to part to head to the Zil city and disrupt his plans and, if possible, secure the vampire blade and bring it to her. She wants them to take the next airship to Korranberg, departing tomorrow around noon. She provide them with the following before they part ways:

    • 3 flasks of holy water
    • a wand of light bearing the mark of a House Cannith artificer
    • a pouch containing 45 pp and 50 gp
    • a letter of credit with House Ghallanda covering a week’s worth of room and board at the Korranberg Gold Dragon Inn.
    • Tickets for passage to Korranberg on the Cloud’s Destiny, a House Lyrandar airship

    13 Eyre

    The next morning, Cassian receives a sending from Cornelius, telling him that the Artificer has been called on for urgent assignment and will meet the group in Korranberg. Everyone else makes their way to Lyrandar Tower for their departure. The tower is very busy, with two airships docked and many people coming and going. In the crowd Caelin and Cassian spot the halfling server from the previous day, who seems to be waiting. Caelin goes to meet with the young man but as they do, the halfling takes off at a run. Caelin chooses not to pursue him and the group boards the vessel.

    They are shown to their rooms, but S.A.M. and Caelin remain on deck to observe the other passengers. The vast majority are gnomes, there is also one dwarven woman. Caelin takes note of one gnome in particular who appears to also be watching the crowds and taking feverish notes in a notepad. They approach this gnome and attempt to question them but it appears the gnome wants to question them instead. They do learn that the man is apparently a reporter for the Korranberg Chronicle and after their conversation decides that they will write a story about warrior priests invading Zilargo.

    "Gertrude", a gnome that definitely isn't just Cligs in disguise, also appoaches this gnome and tries to get information from him. She doesn't learn much more except that in them time she he spoke to Caelin he has become fascinated with S.A.M. Cligs also decides to spy on the dwarven woman, who is the only passenger who has not come out of their cabin. Using Find Familiar, she has a lizard sneak out the porthole in her room and look into the dwarven woman's. It seems like nothing peculiar is happening in here, except that she does seem to be talking to a bag.

    It's a bird? No, definitely halflings and warforged coming to kill us

    Not long into their flight, as they are flying over the King's Forest, passengers begin to notice strange "birds" that seems to be following along their path. Eventually these are close enough to realize that they are halflings mounted on some sort of creature and a group of warforged riding hippogriffs. The captain orders passengers to their quarters and crew to their stations. Obviously, our group ignores that and heads up on deck. Six of the halfling riders are circling the ship, shooting at the crew and party. The four warforged land on the deck and immediately seek out the party, once again paying particular attention to S.A.M.

    The dwarven woman also comes up on deck to face the boarders. Eventually, the illusion this woman has been maintaining drops and the party can see that this is Grilsha, the sister of Lucan that they learned about yesterday.

    The party, crew, and Grilsha all work together to defeat the warforged and most of the halflings, the remainder who flee. As combat is ending but before the group has a chance to connect again and discuss what happened, a strange mist of ice begins to swirl around Cassian. Everyone can hear a voice that is unknown to them, except for Cassian, say "I need your help" and a moment later Cassian is gone.
