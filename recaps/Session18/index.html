    18 Eyre

    Having returned to the inn to recover from the battle at the Ball, morning dawns the group ready to get back on the hunt for Lucan and the vampire blade. S.A.M. has no interest in waiting for the rest of the group as they decide to wear disguises and so heads out to the station with the letter of credit to secure tickets. They decide that he will leave the rest of the groups tickets at the counter under the name "Mr. Underhill." The rest of the party decides to work on disguises:

    • Cligs disguises self as a gnome woman, Philipina Crinklebottom, perhaps?
    • Caelin disguises self as a half-elf woman with resting bitch face
    • Cassian has Caelin attempt to use their disguise kit on him, only for it to go awry and he ends up with bright red hair, a drawn on scar and instead of giving him defined cheekbones he is wearing bright red blush
    • Cornelius realizes that Grilsha and Lucan have never seen him, so he only is going to change the form of his armor. Cligs thinks this isn't enough and attempts to shave his long, flowing, grey locks - which causes Cornelius to punch her after he stops her.

    At the station, S.A.M is told that there is space on the train for the group either in two sleeper cars, separated in standard class, or all together in steerage. He decides on one sleeper car for himself and gets the rest of the group steerage class tickets. When they arrive and pickup their tickets that are not happy to find out about where their seats are but given the train is about to leave they don't have a choice but to get on and deal. The train pulls out and starts its journey to Zolanberg and once they are on the move the group decides to explore:

    • S.A.M. heads to the dining car to sit and observe people as they move about. He notices an odd pairing in a Cleric of the Silver Flame and a Gnome that seem to be traveling and spending most of their time together.
    • Cornelius also makes their way to the dining car but seems mostly interested in sitting and reading a book
    • Cassian makes their way back to the cargo cars to take a look. In the first he finds mostly goods marked as supplies for the journey and some crates that identified only with an ID number. As he attempts to go into the second car he finds this one under guard by 4 House Orien guards. He, somehow, manages to convince them to let him take a look as he claims to be a vampire hunter and there is a rumor of vampires aboard. As he is escorted around he sees that there is mainly secure luggage that seems to belong to more well-off passengers as well as cargo that is only identified with a number but also locked. Curiously, there is a large empty space in the middle that seems like its waiting for something.
    • Caelin goes to seek out a conductor about getting an upgrade, they are eventually able to find one and secure an upgrade to a sleeper room - at almost double the cost it would have been at the ticket counter. On their way they pass through the galley and find two interesting passengers: one is a gnome that seems to be going on and on about Sharn and how excited they are for the journey; the other is human woman who seems to look remarkably like Neya, Caelin approaches this woman and she claims to be a cousin but that they aren't close and she wasn't at the Ball.
    • Cligs goes to make their way to the sleeper cars so that she can have Lizzard McGuire do some scouting. They cross paths with S.A.M. who lets them use the sleeper room to setup while Lizzard is scouting. Cligs has her do a loop around the sleeper cars and report back. She finds the one next door to have the window blocked, the next two are empty, and the last on on this side of the train has a pair of gnomes getting busy. On the opposite side the first room is empty, the next has a sleeping gnome, the next a half-elf woman that seems to be reading a book on the book of the history of the Brelish monarchy and the last two have the gnome and cleric S.A.M. met earlier.

    At lunch time, Cornelius makes the acquaintance of the gnome that Caelin had met earlier and finds that her name is Evine d'Sivis and the two chat for bit. The others share what information they have gathered so far.

    In the afternoon, the train arrives at Zolanberg, the third largest city in Zilargo, for an extended stop. While there, Cornelius is able to get an upgrade to standard class. Cassian decides to become a man of the common people and goes to smoke with some of the other steerage passengers. Caelin uses their expertise with mimicking hand writing to create two fake first class tickets with varying success.

    S.A.M. hangs out on the platform observing and notices two strange, likely related, things. First, he sees cargo being loaded at the back of the train but, curiously, they seem to be taking grate care to keep whatever they are loading hidden by backing a covered wagon directly up to the side of the cargo car. Second, he sees a number of House Orien stewards loading baggage into the first class car but no passenger. He manages to convince one of these stewards to share some information, but unfortunately he doesn't know who the mystery passenger is but that they were told to load this cargo and that the passenger would join just before departure time. After being rebuffed from getting into first class by a train attendant, Cligs sets up Lizzard McGuire to be able to do a scout of the first class car and then spy to see who will be boarding. The group decides that the most likely place to find Grilsha is in the first class car and so plan to ambush her after the next stop in Sterngate, when they are in Breland and she may be sleeping. Just as the train is starting to power up and head out two figures board the train, first a strange man that boards the first class car. They do not recognize him but he seems dangerous, then a second person boards with a cloak pulled tightly up and around their face.

    What a coincidence

    At dinner time, Cligs, S.A.M., and Cassian sit together and observe the dinning car where many passengers have come to eat and socialize. Cornelius asks Evine to dinner and escorts her there and sits at a table with her, separate from the group. Caelin remains in their room. As they are sitting, a somewhat familiar face walks into the dinning car and claims an empty table in the far corner of the car - it looks a lot like Brother Garrow, minus the vampiriness. A few minutes later, another familiar face appears in the car - Lucan Stellos. He looks anxious as he heads to sit with Garrow and talk.

    Cligs takes this chance to see what Lizzard saw and she shows the mystery man appear to board and sweep the first class car and then go into room closer to the front of the car. She then sees Brother Garrow appear and enter the same room. A few minutes later she sees Garrow slip a note under the other first class door that appears to be glowing slightly.

    Back in the present, S.A.M. seems ready to engage and sends a message to the cleric he met earlier and tells her that two of the vampires he told her about earlier are sitting behind her talking. She responds by reminding him that they are many innocents around and protecting them needs to be their first priority. He then messages Caelin and lets them know that Garrow and Lucan are in the dinning car. Cligs sees what is happening and decides it would be best to go to Grilsha and try and get her to help stop a fight from breaking out and she sprints off for the first class car.

    Cligs uses one of the fake first class tickets to get into first class and talk to Grilsha, who lets the goblin in and the two talk. Grilsha is adamant that neither she or her brother knew Garrow was Emerald Claw and tells Cligs that there is no way her brother would ever agree to work with Garrow or give him the blade and if he found out Garrow was Emerald Claw things might go badly. She also tells Cligs that if she helps prevent a fight tonight Grilsha will try to convince her brother to give her party the blade, but that it wont be easy as it has started to corrupt his mind as it is sentient and has it's own will. Cligs agrees and heads back to the group trying to send messages letting them know not to fight.

    Caelin approaches the dining car stealthily and takes a moment to look at the scene before entering. They notice that it seems like the conversation isn't going well. Lucan appears to be angry and Garrow appears frustrated, neither seems to be getting what they thought the would from the conversation. They join the group at the table. S.A.M. decides it's time to do something and goes to sit with the two. As he does, Lucan gets up to leave. S.A.M. attempts to convince/threaten him into staying to talk but he refuses and keeps heading out. Caelin attempts to Command him to stay, but he is able to resist. S.A.M. then turns to Garrow and tells him he knows what the man wants.
