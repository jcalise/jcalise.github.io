    7 Olarune

    Having arrived at the entrance to Whitehearth, the group discusses a marching order for exploring and also assign Cornelius the task of staying with Failin and "keeping him safe." As the enter the cavern, once Cligs is the last one exposed, they hear the rustling of feathers and are ambushed by the same Zombie Vulture that they had previously seen at the Emerald Claw camp in Rose Quarry. The bird seems intent on taking out Cligs, but is no match for the group. While this was nothing more than an inconvenience, it does signal the possibility that others might be not far behind.

    One hatch, two hatch, three hatch

    Entering the cavern, it at first seems like just an empty cave that used to be in use. They can't find any signs of recent activity. Eventually, they find a place where the cavern branches off into 3 different tunnels. Exploring the first one, they find that it dead ends but that there is a hatch embedded in the ground with a curious blue enameled recess in the center. Cligs puts the blue keycharm that they received from Elaydren into the slot and they hear a metalic click and hiss; however, when they actually open the hatch it appears to lead to solid rock. Careful examination doesn't reveal any secret mechanism or way through, so they decide to explore the other two tunnels.

    One tunnel comes to an end at stone face that again they don't find any hidden mechanism at. Backtracking, the other tunnel seems to branch again and both branches have a metal hatch embedded in the floor at the end. They check the west tunnel first and inspecting the hatch find that it is trapped, it also doesn't seem to show any significant signs of wear. The other hatch, the southern one, is also trapped but they can see slight scratches in the enamel around the key hole. Cassian uses Mage Hand to insert the keycharm, pop the hatch open, and see if there is a clear path.

    Descending down the ladder, the group enters a room that is perfect metal sphere, with the only opening the one they just came through. In the center of the room is an octagonal panel that has 9 enameled slots, a blue one in the center and then one on each side that are purple (1), blue (2), blue (3), green (4), purple (5), green (6), blue (7), and blue (8).

    Exploring the facility

    Cligs inserts the blue keycharm into slot 3, the room begins to vibrate and then rotate around opening onto a new hallway. Exploring this new area they come upon a large room that is filled with shelves and shelves of clean bedsheets, towels, blankets, and clothing. The room is completely spotless and a pleasant smell fills the air. They find a washbasin on the far wall that appears filled with clear water. Taking a drink out of it, Caelin find it is the best tasting water they have ever drank. They decide to scoop up a flask of it and then the group goes back to the rotator room.

    Next, they try slot 7. The room again comes to life and the entryway rotates to a small hallway that leads to what appears to be an administrative office with shelves filled with books and scrolls, it also has a dead woman laying on the desk on top of a scroll. Cligs examines the desk and the dead clerk, while Caelin, Cassian, and S.A.M. each take a section of the bookshelves and they spend 10 minutes searching. The find:

    • Cligs finds a scroll of protection from energy and a House Cannith signet ring. The desk is filled with mundane office supplies (papers, ink, etc.)
    • Caelin and S.A.M. find that most of their books and scrolls deal with records of shipments of goods to the facility, ledgers on projects, etc.
    • Cassian finds much the same as the other two, but he also finds a special section detailing projects from other Cannith Enclaves. One item in particular stands out, it's called "Special Autonomous Model Project". Cassian puts this scroll in his pocket and says nothing about it.

    Woof woof

    Heading back to the rotator room, they now try slot 8. Entering the hallway they notice streaks of blood down the hallway leading to the center of the three rooms at the end of the hall. They also hear the unmistakeable sound of wolves at play coming from the left room. The third room appears locked. Cligs cast an illusion to obscure them from the wolves while Caelin scouts the room. As they are sneaking past the two wolves, a third wolf comes out of the room. Caelin says "Who's a good boy?" using Speak with Beast and she responds back, in Common, "Thanks, but it's good girl." This is Rorsa, she explains how she "awoke" four years ago and how she learned how to use the keycharms and make her way around the facility. She takes care of the two younger pups, that she calls her children. She tells them about a dangerous moving fire in the NE wing of the facility (slot 2) and also asks the party to help her free the other wolves trapped in the kennels, being kept there by a hideous beast. She offers a green keycharm and a special magic item she found if they help her.

    After agreeing to help her, she leads them to the kennels. They go back to the rotator room and she puts a green keycharm into slot 6. This leads them down a hall and to a second rotator room. This one has a metal square with 4 enameled slots : yellow, blue, orange, green. She leads them further on and they can hear the howling and barking of a number of wolves. As they approach the kennels they can see that 11 kennels line the walls, 7 of which are still occupied with angry wolves. There is also what appears to be a wolf made of stone standing in the center of the room, this is the monster Rorsa warned them about. The group is able to dispatch the wolf and Rorsa takes charge of the wolves and says she will care for them. She gives them a green keycharm and a pearl of power. In the kennels they also find a pile of debris the stone wolf was laying on, in it they find an orange keycharm, a strange metal disc, a House Cannith signet ring, and a mithral-and-bloodstone brooch. She also tells them that the third room in her hallway she has never been in as it's locked with a purple keycharm. Finally, she says that the slot 4 key in the first rotator room leads to a third rotator, but she never had the right keycharms for that room and that at least one of them is red.

    "I'll never let go, Jack"

    The group heads back to rotator room two and decides to try their orange keycharm, which leads them to a door that is sealed with a yellow keycharm. The group decides to check out the third rotator room first, heading there they find another square with 5 enameled slots: red in the center, then green, orange, yellow, brown. They try the orange keycharm, immediately the room is plunged into darkness and the floor drops out from under them. Cassian and Cligs are able to grab onto the podium and save themselves. Caelin and S.A.M. plunge into a well of freezing water, Caelin can feel the cold immediately starting to seep into their skin. With Cligs and Cassian's help they are both able to climb out of the water and use the green keycharm to go back to the hallway.

    The group heads back to Rorsa's den and decide to try the third room. They don't have the purple keycharm, but S.A.M. tries to use Knock to get in. They let out a tremendous gong - scaring the two wolves - which shatters the enamel lock and the door pops open. This room appears to be a well appointed with nice furnishings and well made weapons and armor adorning the walls. Searching the room carefully they find:

    • a greatsword, a longsword, and a shortsword. These are a matching set, exquisitely crafted by a Cannith artisan and inlaid with mithral tracery.
    • A chainmail shirt armor of gleaming
    • A shield baring the Cannith seal
    • 25 pp and a silver ring set with a bloodstone
    • A Darkweave cloak (+1 to stealth checks)
    • A potion of hill giant strength and a vial of oil of sharpness
    • A yellow keycharm

    That's bright, yo

    Taking the yellow keycharm, the group heads back to that locked door they found. Using the keycharm on the door, they find that the room beyond is filled with mirrored walls and there is a massive ball of bright light. Stepping into the room the group finds that all sound is blocked out, which goes away when they step out. The living color spray is busy bouncing around the mirrors and doesn't take notice of the group at first. They see a door beyond the room and realize they need to deal with this threat. S.A.M. destroys several of the mirrors which ends the deafening effect.

    Eventually, after defeating the living spell, they enter the room beyond. They face a room that appears to be filled with extreme heat and fire, but they don't feel any heat or smell any smoke. They quickly realize that this is an illusion, clearly showing somewhere else in the facility. They also notice a clear chest that seems to hold the diamond-shaped schema they are looking for. A final inspection also shows them that the only thing in the room that is real is a red keycharm hanging on the wall.

    H-O-T-T-O-G-O

    The group heads back to the third rotator room. Cligs' thinks of putting the red keycharm in first, which when they do they hear a loud click. When they then use the orange keycharm again the room comes to life and rotates to a new hallway this time. Heading down the hallway they see a set of double doors and can see and feel intense heat radiating off of the doors. They decide to have Caelin stealthily sneak in and check things out and, if possible, grab the chest. Opening the doors they find two fire elementals roaming the corners of the room, in the center is a large crucible filled with bubbling molten glass radiates terrible heat. Long, snaking heads made of iron and brass rise up from the molten pool like some metal hydra. The crystalline chest is past this. Caelin also notices that the room appears to have a domed ceiling. They attempt to life the chest but find that it is far too heavy to do so. Instead, they pick the lock and remove the contents to the bag of holding:

    • 2 copies of the schema and the creation pattern they belong too
    • 2 potions of fire resistance
    • 2 potions of greater healing
    • a spell scroll of protection from evil and good

    Having had to stop and stay to open the lock, Caelin's invisibility drops and the elementals immediately notice them and charge. The elementals pursue Caelin as they attempt to flee to the rotator room. The group is eventually all able to get into the rotator room and and activate the green keycharm to close out the elementals.

    At this point, the team decides it's time to go. They make their way back to the main rotator room and use the center blue slot to access the ladder. They climb out and S.A.M. send a message to Failin to ask if they are still there. He responds yes, so they decide to head out. As they exit the cavern they hear from a familiar voice “Well done, adventurers.” Arrayed before them is six Emerald Claw soldiers armed with crossbows, four skeletons armed with scythes - one of whom is holding Failin and one Cornelius, and a female necromancer. The vampire is nowhere to be seen, but you can hear his voice.

    “It is futile to fight us,” the vampire says. “Give me the third schema, and I will let you live. Oppose us, and I will take it from your corpses, and then raise your bodies to serve me.”
