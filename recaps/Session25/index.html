    Session 25: 20-22 Dravago

    20 Dravago

    The group wraps up the day by spending time talking to Muroni in the tavern, asking her questions about the prophesy and Vuulaytherus. She doesn't have much more to add about the prophesy but tells them about growing up amongst the dragons of Argonnessen.

    21 Dravago

    In the morning, the group meets in the common room to eat and discuss their plans. S.A.M. chats with Marbur, the hobgoblin barkeep, who provides him with the names of 4 potential riverboat captains that will take varying amounts of time and have different sized crews and vessels. Ultimately, S.A.M. advises the group to go with Chinxero since they can get an in through Channa and it is the fastest option.

    On cue, Channa arrives to the tavern with the letter from Elaydren and refers to the group as "Elaydren's Angels," clearly in a belittling tone, who has evidently filled in her cousin on how helpful the group has been to her. She tries to imply that she knows more than she does and to get the group to speak more openly, but they don't give her much. She agrees to cash in her favor with Chinxero to help the group on the condition that they check-in with her on their return journey.

    Definitely a bastard

    S.A.M., Cornelius, and Cassian head out to secure Chinxero's services for the journey south. He is, as has been promised, a difficult person to deal with. He doesn't want to commit to so much time and so long of a journey, even with the Cannith favor being called in. Ultimately, the trio is able to negotiate a deal to get him to agree to the trip in exchange for 2,000gp. He tells them that The Marlow will be ready to depart in 3 hours.

    Getting underway

    Back at the tavern, Muroni arrives and is greeted by Caelin and Cligs. They fill her in on where the others are and she offers to contribute to the cost of the travel expense. She gives the duo 200gp, which they pocket. When the others return S.A.M. informs them that the cost was much higher and Muroni offers to add more gold, but the Bard tells her it's all covered and not to worry. With that, they all make their way to the river dock to head out on the Rachi River.

    Most of that first day of travel is spent leaving Stormreach and the small communities surrounding it. After a few hours they are in the proper thick of the jungle but still a ways from the more dangerous part of the deeper jungle. During their travels Cligs again tries to learn what she can about controlling elemental bindings without a dragonmark; Caelin converts 2 crew mates to the glory of Aureon (Raoul and Sidney); S.A.M. begins teaching Cassian the fiddle; and Cornelius takes up fishing, providing the ship with several fresh caught rainbow trout for their evening meal.

    Close encounter of the undead kind

    After their shared meal of grilled fish, hardtack for Caelin, and conversation most of the ship heads to bed. S.A.M., Snailor Swift, and Raoul are all on night watch. A couple hours into the evening S.A.M. notices a pair of lights that seem to be enticing Raoul and eventually it appears they are hurting him - S.A.M. raises the alarm and the party comes to help. A second pair of lights, which are identified by Caelin and Cligs as Will-o'-Wisps, are approaching from the east as well. They manage to save Raoul from having his life drained and kill 2 of the Wisps, the others are able to escape.

    22 Dravago

    The Marlow sets out again at first light and the party occupies their time during the day. Cligs again spends time with Chinxero, Quire (the first mate), and just generally watching how the ship works. Caelin makes three more converts: Bernard, Chud, and Brandy. S.A.M. and Cassian continue to practice and Cassian is able to play his first tune. Cornelius continues fishing and bringing in fresh food for the ship.

    Sorry to interrupt

    After lunch time, the boat comes upon an interesting scene. An ettin is standing in the water, close to the banks of the river and seems to be talking to an ogre that it sitting on the shore. They are far enough away and not paying enough attention to notice the boat right away. Phineas gives S.A.M. a heads up that they appear to be speaking Giant and it seems that the ettin is hitting on the ogre, trying to impress him. Phineas encourages S.A.M. that the situation can probably be dealt with without violence.

    Cligs also notices what is happening, but not speaking Giant is concerned. She makes an illusion of an "pretty ogre lady head" splashing in the water. This gets the attention of the ettin but not in a good way. Cligs then centers a Sleep spell on the duo which seems to take effect - hoping that they remain asleep long enough for the boat to pass by. Unfortunately, it appears the ettin is immune to this effect and seeing it's friend passed out and the flopping ogre head, starts to move in on the boat. Cassian uses Phantasmal Force to make the ettin believe that the sun is falling down on it, the ettin dives under water and begins to swim away. The Marlow is able to continue on without facing further incident and Chinxero is appreciative of their help, but is wondering if they might be a magnet for trouble.
