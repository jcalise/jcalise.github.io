    Session 23

    18 Dravago

    "Morning," such as it is under the waves, arrives and the party finds the crew about the business of keeping the Sea Dart running. S.A.M. once again takes up playing for the crew as they get their morning meal and is able to make a few more of them friendly towards the group. Caelin decides to proselytize to the gnomes and converts Zander, Tallian, and Talia to the word of Aureon and the Sovereign Host. Cassian takes a rest day, having spent much of the previous day working out. Cornelius spends most of the morning reading up and preparing to continue to work on his armor when he returns to Sharn. Cligs tries to get back on to the bridge, only to find that the captain has given orders that she not be allowed on the bridge any more. She casts Invisibility on Lizzard McGuire and manages to sneak her onto the bridge and has her spy.

    Da-dum, da-dum, da-dum

    Around noon, the captain calls the party to the observation blister. There he shows them an underwater view of Shargon's Teeth - massive stone columns that shoot up to the surface and between them are fields of phosphorescent kelp and bright fish of all sizes darting about. As they are watching S.A.M. is the only one who manages to notice the massive shark bearing down on them and is able to brace before the shark smashes into the ship. The crew and the rest of the party are knocked off balance by the impact. Shouts from the crew start to rise up about damage to the elemental binding and the ship taking on water. The captain orders the ship to the surface for repairs.

    As they rise to the surface the ship gets caught on something, presumably the kelp has gotten caught up. Four of the crew plus S.A.M., Cligs, Cassian, and Caelin exit the ship in order to work on freeing the ship. Cornelius remains inside to help with repairs. They find that what the ship is actually caught up in appears to be a massive net stretched between several of the stone columns and trapping the ship in four points - they begin to work on freeing the ship.

    While they are working on this, S.A.M. and Cligs get a quick glimpse of a humanoid with a tail swimming off away from the scene. Struggling with the rope nets eventually out of the darkness the shark returns and attempts to gobble up one of the crew. It continues to harass them while they work. They eventually are able to drive it off and free the ship. The crew returns inside but the party remains outside for the ascent to the surface.

    Parley

    Breaching the surface, the crew secure the ship to a nearby relatively flat top of a column and get to work on repairs. S.A.M. posts up in the ship scanning the water below with two Everbright Lanterns to keep watch. Cligs summons a new Familiar, Eelijah Wood, to swim through the waters and help keep watch. Caelin communes with the kelp forests and asks them to give them a sign if anything bigger than a fish approaches the ship. Cassian chills. Cornelius aids in repairs.

    S.A.M. catches sight of the shark that seems to be keeping it's distance just at the edge of the Lantern light (120 feet). Caelin notices that the kelp forest glow has started pulsing in the rough direction of where the shark was reported by S.A.M. Cligs sees creatures rising through the water and alerts the captain, who identifies them as Sahuagin warriors. He offers to try to parley on behalf of the party, since he speaks their language, but tells them to quickly figure out what they are willing to offer as tribute.

    Byam and the lead Sahuagin have a brief conversation and then Byam asks for what the party is offering. They decide to offer Cligs' Wand of Firebolt, an Everbright Lantern from the part stash, and S.A.M. offers a Potion of Growth. The captain explains these items to the Sahuagin and is able to negotiate for safe passage through the rest of the Teeth.

    19-20 Dravago

    With safe passage having been been negotiated by Byam, the rest of their journey to Stormreach is uneventful. S.A.M. and Cligs have a heart-to-heart about her desire to learn how elemental ships work and to be able to pilot one. S.A.M. offers to write a letter to Elaydren on Cligs' behalf about this and try to help her achieve this goal.

    Stormreach

    Late on the second day following the attack of the magebred shark, the captain brings the Sea Dart to the surface just outside of Stormreach. The city stands out as a lone beacon of civilization along a coast completely dominated by jungles. He docks the ship and hides it among the jungle flora about a mile down the coast, giving the party instructions to enter the city through the docks. He gives them travel papers that indicate they arrived on the House Lyrandar galleon Strong Wind as part of an expedition from Morgrave University. He also gives them a letter of credit but instructs them that it can only be used at the House Cannith enclave and only to arrange transportation into the jungle. With that, they set out for the city and discuss their plans, deciding to split up when they reach the city to try and do some recon before nightfall.

    They arrive in the city and split up: - Cassian and S.A.M. converse with dock workers and sailors in the Harbor district and eventually make their way to the Wavecrest Tavern to arrange rooms for the night and try and get a lead on transport downriver. - Cornelius heads out to the Cannith Enclave to inquire there about using the letter of credit for transport. - Caelin and Cligs start out together going to the Market, looking for maps and other information about the area. They eventually also split up, with Cligs heading toward Falconer's Spire, the House Lyrandar airship docking tower, to inquire about recent arrivals and Caelin heading to the Blood of Vol temple.

    Their exploits turn up some results: - Cassian "Man of the People" ir'Bellona manages to gather some intel about possible places they can ask about riverboat travel. - S.A.M.'s generous tips and subtle threats secure them a set of rooms at the Tavern and the Hobgoblin barkeep agreed to provide them with a contact in the morning who can discreetly take them downriver. - Cligs learns, after learning herself that being cheap doesn't open people's lips, that an airship recently docked from Sharn that had a number of armored warriors, a heavily armed warforged, and a strange undead creature being kept below decks. They appeared to have been led by a Half-Elf named Rarwog. They airship then headed off to follow the Rachi River. - Caelin spies on the Blood of Vol temple and identifies the head priestess. They approach and out loud ask about the faith but put a message in the priestess' head about Brother Garrow and the Emerald Claw. She quickly escorts them into her office. There she tells them that Brother Garrow, a terrifying vampire, and a monstrous undead creature came to visit her and ask about the jungle and the ruins it contains. She had directed them to Provost Baris Kaven, leader of the Morgrave Expedition who is the most knowledgable about the ruins around Stormreach.
