    20 Dravago

    Cligs and Caelin meet up outside of the Blood of Vol temple and start heading back to the Tavern, sharing what they have learned on the way. They also stop in at Curra's Curiosities, a Drow-owned shop with a collection of wares that also advertises selling maps. Curra shows them the maps she has of the area south of the city and encourages them to purchase a Drow-made map in order to not fall victim to the Traveler's Curse. They end up paying for a Drow map which shows about 700 miles of the Rachi River that appears to have been a hunter's map, showing various dangers and hunting locations. It also shows the location of a giant ruin, indicated by a hand sticking up out of the ground.

    Traveler's Curse

    The Traveler’s Curse is one of Xen’drik’s most famous anomalies. Explorers may find their journey accelerated to a fraction of the time needed, or slowed down by months, but perceive the journey as completely normal. Two groups traveling the same path can arrive at the same destination at different times - or even different destinations altogether. The Curse is unpredictable.

    Back at the Wavecrest, Cassian tries to have a conversation with S.A.M. about the source of his power and his fears around that. S.A.M. encourages him to trust his judgement and that so far it seems that the added power has been only beneficial. S.A.M. then discusses with Cassian about the need to prioritize the mission, returning the schemas and other items to Elaydren, over the party and their plans. Cassian seems to agree.

    After they leave Curra's Caelin talks to Cligs about the fact that they are worried they are losing their way and straying from Aureon's teachings. Cligs tries to reassure them. The two then move on to plotting how they may deal with S.A.M. and Elaydren when it comes to returning the schemas, creation pattern, and documents.

    Returning to the Tavern, Cligs checks in with the Hobgoblin barkeep (who is named Marbur, even though no one asked) about their rooms. Cligs, Caelin and Marbur have a brief conversation in Goblin before the two party members head upstairs. Reuniting with the others, they share their information - it is then that they realize that Cornelius hasn't returned from the Cannith enclave. The group decide that they probably need a name....but also to split up again, with Cassian and Caelin heading to talk to the Provost at the Morgrave Expedition and Cligs and S.A.M. heading to the Cannith enclave to find Cornelius.

    Morgrave, more like Sadgrave

    Caelin attempts to disguise Cassian again, this time as an experienced researcher and writer but instead gives him a cool looking gray streak in his hair. They head to the boarding house and find the students, but it seems like the Provost is out for the evening. The two try to endear themselves to the students but it doesn't go so well. Eventually, they are able to learn that the Provost tends to drink at the Lusty Mermaid a few blocks away. They head over and talk to the barkeep, slipping her some coin to point out the Provost and what his drink of choice is. She points to a sad looking man sitting alone in the corner drinking a Mermaid Juice, the house drink. The two go over and use the cover of wanting to write about giant ruins and Caelin being an illustrator to get information from him.

    He confirms that Garrow and a warforged named Scimitar came to visit him and he provided them some information. He relays the same information that the safest way to travel is to follow the River and avoid cutting through the jungle as it is far too dangerous. He also gave them some rough ideas of the ruins along the river and what they might find. Finally, he suggests that Chinxero, a grizzled riverboat captain, would be their best bet to get them there quickly as he is in possession of the only elemental-powered riverboat in Stormreach.

    Cannith Cassanova

    S.A.M. and Cligs make their way to the Citadel of the Twelve, where House Cannith has a series of offices as opposed to their own standalone enclave here. Arriving their they make their way to their space in the basement, the interior has been converted into a large workroom with a variety of different equipment setups. There is someone who seems to be in charge sitting at the front desk who appears to have very similar features to Elaydren. They also see Cornelius in the back, seemingly helping a Cannith artisan at the forge. They check-in with the woman at the desk and then go to talk to Cornelius. He is working with a young, attractive, blacksmith named Leila. S.A.M. covertly relays to Cornelius that they are staying at the Wavecrest and that they have picked up some information and that Cornelius should come there when done. He also passes off the letter of credit to S.A.M. who quickly examines it and notices that the handwriting is Elaydren's and it seems to be written in a very personal way directed to the woman at the front desk.

    They talk to her on their way out, her name is Channa Vown d'Cannith and S.A.M. relays to her what they are looking for - transportation down the Rachi River, and gives her the letter. She takes a quick glance and they can see she clearly recognizes the writing. S.A.M. asks her to stop by the Tavern the next day.

    Obviously a Trap

    The four meet up again, Cornelius still occupied with Leila, and head back to the Wavecrest. As they approach they find that there is a small girl standing outside seemingly waiting for someone, clutching a note in her hand. When she sees them she goes running up and practically begs them to the take the note and let her go home. The note appears to be scrawled in blood and says "I answer asks for gold. I Surthain. Meet now, Xaponath Lane, Old Quarter.” The girl, Samna, confirms that she was given the note on Xaponath Lane, but that it is in Southwatch, there is no Old Quarter in the town.

    S.A.M. is, unsurprisingly hesitant to get involved, but Cligs and Caelin are adamant that they help the girl and Cassian is able to be swayed, they all head out. Arriving at the place indicated, it appears to be a dead-ended alley mostly obscured by building and few lights. The party investigates and finds a lone figure standing at the end with a cloak pulled up over their head. S.A.M. tries to talk to the figure but it is intent on violence. It attacks and when it has drawn several of the party into the alley it's allies, a pair of ghouls, move in to try and trap the party. During the fighting a mysterious Elven woman joins the battle and it is to help the party.

    Prophesy-schmophesy

    After helping to eliminate the undead, the Elf reveals her name is Muroni and that the group was spoken about in the Draconic Prophesy and that she was sent here by Vuulaytherus, a silver dragon who studies the prophesy and found the verses that speak about the party. She tells them that is predicted that a a group of adventures tied to House Cannith would arrive to Xen'Drik from under the sea and she saw them arrive on the Sea Dart. She asks to accompany them on the journey to observe and that she can also help them along the way. S.A.M. doesn't really buy into the Prophesy thing but both him and Cassian are mostly concerned that she might pull them off their mission in service of the prophesy - she assures them that she is just here to help. Finally, the group agree to bring her in and they all return to the Wavecrest Tavern.
