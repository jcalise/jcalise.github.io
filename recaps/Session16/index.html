    Session 16: 16-17 Eyre

    16 Eyre

    Having secured three invitations and left Lizard McGuire behind as a spy, Cassian and "Philip" head back to meet up with Caelin and Cornelius, who seems to be thoroughly enjoying the Aundairian pastries. As they arrive, S.A.M. also turns up and the they all fill each other in on what they were up to while they were apart. Cligs is pretty sure that, with help from Caelin, they can magically forge another 2 invitations if they need them. S.A.M. decides that it might be best for him to try and secure another real one, in order to limit things that can go wrong. The group splits up again, with S.A.M. heading to the embassy and the rest of the group going to look for costumes and masks for the upcoming ball.

    A vision of home

    S.A.M. presents himself to the guards and tells them that he is looking to offer his services at the ball as an entertainer. They tell him that the embassy has already booked "The Marionettes" for the ball, but after a brief conversation he is able to convince them to let him speak with Jayres about other opportunities. S.A.M. is led to the majordomo's office and he again offers his services for the event. Jayres humors him and allows him to audition. The traditional Aundairian piece that S.A.M. plays is performed so well that it moves the man to tears and he agrees to pay S.A.M 10 gp and gives him the hour before the official ball start time, when guests are arriving, to play in the garden space. He also gives him an invitation in order to attend the party after performing.

    Queue the montage

    Meanwhile, the rest of the group searches Old Town for an appropriate shop to buy costumes and masks for the event, eventually choosing "The Dapper Road", a high-end boutique that is specifically advertising attire for the masquerade ball. Inside the shop there are numerous outfits on display and a whole section devoted to attire fit for the ball. After browsing, they each decide on an outfit and mask:

    • Caelin:
    • Chose a flowing color-shifting purple [[common-glamerweave-erlw|Glammerweave]] gown adorned with embroidery in order to cover their armor and a plain black mask.
    • Cassian:
    • Decided he would wear the full glammerweave suit he already owns and purchased an eagle mask.
    • He also chose for S.A.M. an over-the-top. peacock mask adorned with feathers and a matching vibrant cloak.
    • Cligs
    • Thinking she will go to the ball as Philip Crinklebottom, she purchased a nice but relatively plain tunic and pants in House ir'Bellona colors and a bugbear mask.
    • Cornelius
    • Insists that he will go in his armor and helmet and didn't buy anything

    Purchases done, the group heads back to their tavern to meet up with S.A.M. and discuss the plan for tomorrow.

    A quick stop

    After leaving their purchases in the tavern, Cassian and Caelin leave for one more quick errand and seek out an alchemist. They head to the nearby Central Market and are able to find a suitable shop, called "What Ails You" and ask the shopkeep for both a sleep potion, because Cassian can't sleep, and a poison, because Caelin has a rat problem. They head to the back and eventually return with two bottles: one marked with a skull and the other a question mark. The shopkeep says the question mark one is a sleep potion but that he can't guarantee how long it would work - hence the question mark. Cassian takes a whiff and it smells strongly of valerian root and other sleepy-time things. They pay and return to the tavern.

    17 Eyre

    Having most of the day to kill, Cassian, Caelin and S.A.M. decide to try and gather some information about other guests and go out to check in with a variety of clothing shops around Old Town to see if they can get information on what Brother Garrow or other potential targets might be wearing. They are able to hit up 5 such shops across the Aundairian and Karrnathi areas of Old Town. They are able to convince shop owners in all the stores to share some information about recent purchases but in reviewing the information they are only able to identify one person of interest: E. Garm who purchased a white wolf mask.

    Unfortunately, Cornelius begins to suffer the consequences of over-indulgence yesterday and is confined to the privy. They group leaves him an invitation in case he is feeling better and can join them later.

    Pre-game

    S.A.M. heads out before the rest of the group for his allotted time as an entertainer and sets up in the courtyard with a good view of the entrance so he can monitor who enters. As he arrives before any guests he is able to scope out the building and identifies the following:

    • Two pairs of guards: one at the main entrance and one and the barrier between the garden and ballroom (green bases 1-5)
    • A fifth guard stationed in the ballroom
    • Various members of staff setting up and getting carts of food ready in both spaces
    • A "stage" of sorts at the opposite end of the ballroom that the Marionettes are setting up on
    • Both areas are illuminated by dancing lights and floating everbright lanterns, casting the whole space in dim lighting.
    • And Jayres roaming about checking in on everything, who dons a green stag mask (on red base 13) before guests start arriving.

    The Guests

    After a few moments guests begin to arrive and S.A.M. begins to play, making note of the guests as they come in.

    • The first pair are two Aundairian humans, a man and a woman with the woman hanging on the man's arm. They are both well dressed in expensive looking glammerweave clothing. The man wears a green dragon man (red base 7) and the woman a black cat mask (red base 9)
    • The next pair were two Zil gnomes, a man and woman, both wearing expensive looking clothing but the woman's attire significantly more showy. He is wearing a gold stag mask (red base 2) and she is wearing white swan mask (red base 6)
    • A solo male dressed in what appears by attire favored by Brelish nobility arrives and is wearing a black crow mask (red base 10)
    • A trio of Aundairian's arrive, a woman and two men. The woman is wearing nice, well made clothing but it is not overly showy and a blue dragon mask (red base 8). The two men are both wearing well fitting suits and plain black domino masks (red bases 3 and 12)
    • And finally, the rest of the party arrives

    S.A.M. wraps up playing and begins to circulate the crowd, receiving compliments and invitations to perform from several. Cligs, disguised as "Philip", takes a moment once they are in the main ballroom to swap senses with Lizzard and get the information from her. She shows her a stream of information from the last day, mostly workers and delivery people coming and going and a few visits from Jayres to Neya. She also shows Cligs a visit to Neya from a House Lyrandar envoy. Eventually Lizzard is showing her a live feed of Neya's office and sees the woman pacing around, reading from a set of cards and speaking out loud - she is practicing her speech. Eventually Neya finishes and dons a gold dragon mask (red base 4) and heads out to the ballroom. Cligs uses this opportunity to have Lizzard search her desk where she finds a closed letter with the Karrnathi Seal on it. Lizzard is able to get this open and read the contents, which in brief say that E.G. will bring the papers to the masquerade ball.

    A toast and a lot of action

    After most of the guests are in the ballroom and have had a chance to get a drink, Neya makes her entrance and heads up to the stage. She gives a brief message welcoming everyone to the ball and encouraging them to enjoy the evening.

    30 minutes after the party begins two prominent guests arrive, a man in a well fitting suit wearing an eagle mask (red base 5) - actually the same one as Cassian, and a half-elf woman wearing an owl mask (red base 11). Other guests also come and go throughout the evening.

    As the ball continues on the group notices that people are getting pretty raucous. There are several different dances and attempts at conversation - including an awkward attempt at being "Eagle Bros" from Cassian to the other guest in an eagle mask. Cassian attempts to see if he can identify any hidden weapons on the eagle man but isn't able to.

    50 minutes into the event, the man in eagle mask and Neya share a lengthy dance and what appears to be an intense conversation. S.A.M. isn't able to pick-up anything by observing but he makes note.

    60 minutes into the event, S.A.M. sees Neya speak with Jayres and decides to check in with the majordomo. While they are talking it seems that Jayres is a bit distracted, as if his mind is concentrating on something else. After a brief chat S.A.M. notices a grave look cross over Jayres' face and the man apologizes and goes to leave - S.A.M. gently encourages him to seek his help if it's needed.

    Briefly after this, blue dragon mask asks eagle mask to dance. At the same time, Cligs asks owl mask to dance and begins an intense attempt to woo the half-elf into sharing more information. During the dance Cligs uses the cover of tossing her in the air to check for concealed weapon but finds something else - a messenger-style bag hidden under her clothing. She begins to question more and eventually is convinced that owl mask is none other than Grilsha. She begins trying to let the others know her thoughts. When Cassian comes over to the pair the woman seems to recognize him and leaves to return to the garden - convincing Cligs further of her suspicions.

    75 minutes into the event, as owl mask is leaving Cligs, another guest arrives in a white wolf mask. He makes a quick round of ballroom and then goes to have a conversation with Neya. He eventually passes her what appears to be documents before heading off.

    90 minutes into the event, Neya once again addresses the crowd. She delivers a brief speech thanking everyone again for coming, extolling the virtues of the work done by the architects in designing the space, and thanking the musicians for their wonderful performance. After she leaves the stage, eagle mask approached her for another conversation. As the two are talking the two Aundairian men in black domino masks approach him from behind and each put a hand on his shoulders. They say, loud enough for several others to hear around, "you must come with us," instead the man puts his hand out and what appears there is something the party has heard described - the vampire blade. He swings at the closest of the two men - chaos ensues.
