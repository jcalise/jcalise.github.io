    25 Dravago (cont.)

    Having defeated Arkaban, the group loots the corpses of the dead recovering the mummy's Blurred Cloak which goes to Cassian. Caelin is able to determine the type of affliction that has befallen Cassian and themselves and know that they need to find a way to lift a curse in order to be rid of it. In the meantime, they also use the Codebreaker to review the glyphs on the tiers of the ziggurat. The first two tiers tells a myth of the magician-gods of the giants and a parable of the magehand and the giant’s son, both mostly inscrutable due to cultural differences. The top tier, with each side only having one glyph, the Caelin can translate these to be The Monkey’s Paw, the Unicorn’s Hoof, and Io’s Twinkle - Cornelius is able to connect these names to a sextet of constellations named by the Giants.

    The group decides to split their efforts, with Cassian, Caelin, Muroni and Meerkat the Macaw descending to the Drow to seek a cure. S.A.M., Cornelius, and Cligs go to explore more of the floor they are on. The room directly across from the antechamber they find a large flight of stairs that lead up - they decide to wait for the others to go up. They explore another set of rooms that are mostly empty, until they enter one door to find the mostly decayed corpse of one of the Cloud Apes. Cornelius notices that a pile of debris and dust seems to be moving and they decide to nope out of there.

    It's better than death

    Returning to the Drow, Caelin leads the conversations in asking about a way to remove curses as they are the only one who speaks a common language with the Drow. The scout they meet at says that their is a wise woman in their nearby camp that can potentially help and they agree to go. They find her tent is a haphazard collection of hides and upon entering it is filled with all manner of vials, containers, plants, and strange creatures.

    Caelin explains the situation to the wise woman, who isn't sure what mummy rot is but things she can make them a brew that will help - but warns that not knowing the physiology of Furbolgs and Humans it could help or kill them. She suggests trying it on Cassian first, but Caelin is willing to take it first. She goes to her mortar and starts adding ingredients: a live scorpion, some jungle dirt, various flowers and clippings, a bit of her spit, and finally she tops it off with a healthy glug of river water. She pours this into two cups and hands them out.

    Caelin downs the "fluid", which even though it looks very wet it slides down their throat like a heavy ooze. Caelin chases it with a vial of holy water and a prayer to Aureon. Luckily, it seems to have helped. Cassian can see color restore to Caelin's face and buoyed by this downs his as well. Fortunately, this seems to also work on Cassian. In payment they give her a a robe that Caelin had acquired in Khorvaire, fine fabric that is not commonly available to the Drow.

    A quick detour

    They party decides to make a quick trip down to the Dark Level to checkout the other ziggurat. They use the same method on this one as the one on the temple level and can decipher the designs on the lower two levels of the ziggurat describe a myth of the creation of a place called Xulenzen and a parable of a carver and a creek-lizard, both mostly inscrutable due to cultural differences. The glyphs that decorate three of the four sides of the uppermost step read as: The Scorpion’s Tail, the River’s End, and Garyx’s Eye - the remainder of the sextet from the daylight ziggurat.

    Enough with the zombies

    Returning to the antechamber, the two groups share their stories and begin to head towards the stairs to go up. As they enter the room with the stairs they are greeted by a trio Karnnathi Undead Soldiers. The three cause quite a bit of trouble for the group, even downing Cornelius. Eventually the group is able to eliminate the threat and recover a bit. They decide it's best to press on.

    At the top of the stairs, Cligs is able to notice that the door is trapped with a Glyph of Warding. They decide to set it off with Mage Hand which causes a huge thunderous explosion that scatters the supplies stored here and kills to Emerald Claw sentries that were guarding the other side of the door. They quickly decide to pretend that Cligs was killed in the blast and that they used her to set it off should anyone come. A pair of guards comes around the corner just as they finalize this plan and luckily it works to convince them to go help. The group uses the element of surprise to eliminate the two soldiers and press on.

    That is definitely not Garrow

    They make their way through the corridors finding another room filled with supplies, a stair case that leads up sized for elves (Meerkat goes to explore this and promptly dies), and room that neither Cligs nor Caelin is able to pick the lock to get into. Eventually they find a passageway that leads to a massive constructed gap in the ruins. In the distance they can see a giant throne, sized for a titan, sitting in the middle. There is a woman dressed as a scholar from Morgrave University working at a nearby table with their back to the group. They decide to leave her for now and cross to the other side of the ruins. As they approach they can hear a number of voices and decide it's best to get the drop on them and take them out before they can be a problem.

    Cligs uses her illusions to draw off a pair of soldiers to the ledge where, unluckily for them a air of Cloud Apes have been hanging out who grabs them and throw them over the side. The party engages the remaining 6 soldiers and Scimitar, the warforged accompanying Garrow. Through grit and some trickery they manage to eliminate them all, including sending Scimitar to meet the Cloud Apes through Command.

    As they are dealing with the aftermath of the combat, the rotund woman comes into view. Cligs and S.A.M. attempts to both question her but also keep her from seeing what happened inside the room. Eventually they tell her that they dealt with the Emerald Claw soldiers - who initially she says kidnapped her to work on this project. A confusing back and forth happens and then, finally, she says "I've had enough" and reaches out to Chill Touch them both. As she continues saying that she's sick and tired of them meddling in her plans she morphs, features melting, into the hideous visage of the vampire Garrow they've seen previously.
