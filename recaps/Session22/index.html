    Session 22

    Downtime

    Having returned from Thrane and me with Elaydren for their rewards the party has some downtime to pursue other interests. Here is what everyone got up to:

    • Caelin: Purchased a new set of magic armor (+1 studded) and offloaded some unwanted items at the Bazaar.
    • Cassian: He had a long awaited reunion with the Forgotten Prince, who officially became his Patron and bestowed upon him more power drawn from Thelanis and a powerful tool (+1 Rod of the Pact Keeper). He also shared that one of the most precious things that has been lost and he has been searching for is his true name.
    • Cligs: Lost her job because she kept disappearing on adventures. Decided to shake down Elaydren for money since she got fired. Elaydren agrees to give her 15gp per month to cover her lost wages.
    • Cornelius: Took Evine d'Sivis on several dates - but they are taking things slow. He also began working on a set of plate armor.
    • S.A.M.: Received an invitation for a private dinner with Elaydren, which he accepted and filled her in on more of the details of their last adventure and things he didn't tell the rest of the party. Elaydren rewarded his loyalty by gifting him with a Docent, a small metal sphere that he embedded in his chest. The Docent is sentient and goes by Phineas and can help S.A.M. with certain tasks.

    16 Dravago

    The group meets for their weekly dinner to catch up and check-in to see if they have more work. While they are meeting, a gargoyle messenger arrives at the tavern with a message from Elaydren inviting them to meet with her later that evening for a new job.

    Ding, dong, undead

    Arriving to Elaydren's town house things appear wrong, the door is open, papers are scattered, and several windows are smashed. A shadow passes across the door and they hear a shout from a woman come from inside. Rushing in to the scene, Cassian sees three undead soldiers have Elaydren cornered - several corpses of her household staff and guards are strewn about the ground floor and there is one dead zombie.

    Seeing the party, Elaydren begins to retreat back to further and gratefully accepts their help. More difficult than other undead they have faced, these three put up quite the fight - shouting slogans that sound like Karrnathi military slogans. Eventually they are able to overcome them and end the battle.

    Elaydren fills the group in on what happened. A jolly merchant talked his way inside the door, only to melt into the Blood of Vol priest, Brother Garrow and in came his Emerald Claw soldiers and the 4 Karrnathi zombies. They killed her staff and stole her schemas, the creation pattern, and some recently uncovered notes from Elaydren's ancestor, Kedran d'Cannith, that indicate where the final piece of the pattern is. She asks them to go after the Emerald Claw and get to the schema before they can and return the others to her.

    When they accept, Elaydren offers them 5,000gp each for the successful return of all of the schemas, the notes, and the creation pattern. She tells them she will arrange transport to Stormreach, the coastal city on the continent of Xen'drik where the final schema is located. She gives them a letter of credit for 5,000 gp to cover prep costs and tells them to return in 2 hours. They head to the Bazaar and purchase a number of items that they think will be useful for the upcoming journey, mainly healing potions.

    Under the Sea

    Returning to Elaydren, she informs them that she has arranged transport on a secret creation of House Cannith, an undersea ship that can travel under the water and get them there discreetly - and hopefully ahead of the Emerald Claw. She gives them directions to the Stone Docks, Grayflood district and to the ships captain, Byam. She tells them to hunt the Emerald Claw if they can, but if they cant then to head towards the ruins 400 miles inland from Stormreach, up the Rachi River. She gives them the clue from Kedran's journal: At the turn in the Rachi River, where the land rises and the hands of stone reach for the sky, look toward the giant’s left hand for guidance to the schema’s resting place. Finally, she gives each person a small pouch with 2,500gp worth of gems.

    They make their way to meet with Byam, who escorts them to a warehouse set back from the docks. There he shows them the Sea Dart, Cannith's underwater ship powered by water elemental. Unlike most other vessels, this one is completely sealed from the outside and the only entrance appears to be a hatch at the top. There are several gnome crew loading the vessel up, who don't seem to happy to be sharing the ship with so many large passengers.

    The captain gives them a brief tour of the ship and , once settled in, the hatch pressurizes and the ship descends into the water and takes off under the Thunder Sea toward Xen'drik. The captain does warn the party that in order to follow Elaydren's instructions to get them there as fast as possible he will be taking a route through Shargon's Teeth, territory under the control of sahuagin raiders. Going around their territory would add more than 1 and a half days to the journey. S.A.M. says the group will be ready if needed.

    17 Dravago

    Their first day of travel takes them beneath the Thunder Sea, where traveling under the waves allows them to avoid most of the dangers of pirates on the surface. Their travel day is mostly uneventful. S.A.M. plays some traditional Zil music and is able to, if not make the gnomes like him at least put them at ease with his presence. Cligs takes this opportunity to study how Byam works the ship, noticing that he does not have a Mark of Storm which is normally how elemental ships are controlled. Cligs notices that Byam speaks to the ship in Primordial as part of the control mechanism. At one point, she thinks she has understood and when the captain steps away she tries to make an adjustment to the ship - and succeeds! Byam sends her off the bridge at that point.

    Cornelius uses his time to study designs for the plate mail that he is working on back in Sharn. Cassian seems uneasy and occupies himself doing pushups and twirling his big black crystal rod. Caelin was put on guard by the captain saying they couldn't use the second berth across the hall and decides to check it out. They do so and....it is empty. They decide to steal a pillow and ultimately to sneak in and sleep in there.
