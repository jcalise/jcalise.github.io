    Session 12

    7 - 8 Olarune

    Exiting the cavern, the party is faced with the Order of the Emerald Claw before them, they can hear but not see the vampire Garrow talking to them and demanding the "third" schema. They quickly converse and S.A.M. advises that they give up one of the copies of the diamond-shaped schema that they recovered in order to get out of this alive. The group agrees and Caelin hands over one of the copies to the closest soldier. Once they have it, Garrow reveals themself and tells them they made the right choice. He, the necromancer, and several of the soldiers mount up on their horses and prepare to leave. As he rides off, he shouts out to the remaining soldiers "kill them all!" The party, and Failin, are able to fight off the remaining soldiers and skeletons and Failin is ready to get out of there.

    Luckily, the journey back through the Mournland is far less treacherous than it was coming to Whitehearth. The land cart knows the land now and is able to backtrack through the dangers the party already cleared. They see some of the same sights they passed on the way in but are able to avoid and direct conflicts. They finally break through the mists and back into Darguun at around 3am. They are able to convince Failin to take them back to Rhukaan Draal in exchange for some of the items they found and a letter of reference from Elaydren. With that, they decide it well past time for some rest.

    In the morning, Cornelius identifies the Talaen Kara and shares what he finds with the others. They also discuss what they are going to give to Failin in exchange for the travel and settle on the chainmail armor of gleaming that they recovered. Eventually they break camp and start the journey back to the capital. Failin insists on traveling most of the day to put as much distance between them and the Mournland as possible and they don't make camp until 11pm that evening. S.A.M. takes this time to give Failin the armor of gleaming and discuss asking Elaydren for the letter of reference, which seems to please Failin for now.

    9 Olarune

    The group travels most of the day and are fortunate to not run into any issues on the journey. When they break to make camp, Caelin asks Cornelius, as a fellow old, about their thoughts on the war and how Cannith contributed to it.

    10 Olarune

    The last day of travel, they should reach Rhukaan Draal late that evening and then will need to meet with Elaydren at the inn they pre-arranged with her, the Crossed Hammers. Caelin takes this morning to investigate the chest behind Failin's seat where he put Olladra's Chalice and the armor of gleaming. They notice that it doesn't appear to be locked, magically or otherwise, but that there is Abjuration magic emanating from it.

    Several hours into the journey, several of the group notice dark shapes in the sky that appear to be following them and getting closer. Eventually, they are able to make out two griffon's bearing down on them, no doubt looking to make them a meal. In the confusion of this approaching threat, Caelin takes the opportunity to steal the chalice from Failin - which triggers a blaring Alarm and immediately alerts Failin to what they did. Failin refuses to move the cart further until the chalice is returned, even with the threat of the griffons bearing down on them. Cligs is almost taken away as a snack, but Cassian is able to save them from the beasts claws and eventually S.A.M. is able to scare them away. Turning back to the issue at hand, Caelin agrees to pay Failin 10 platinum in exchange for the chalice, twice the original bargain. Failin agrees to these terms but makes it clear he wants nothing to do with the party after they part ways in the city.

    Arriving back in Rhukaan Draal, they part ways with Failin and tell him they will send the letter of reference from Elaydren, if she agrees, to the tavern where they met. They then go to meet up with her. They find her sitting in the back of the Crossed Hammers, looking more like herself but dressed in plain travel clothes. They debrief her on the situation with Garrow and give her both the schema and the creation pattern. She seems distressed about the fact that the Emerald Claw is now also in the mix of this hunt and it seems has at least one other schema, but is bolstered by the discovery of the creation pattern. She gives them the 2,000 gold reward she promised and offers them an additional 1,000 gold to help safely return her to Sharn, where she intends to go on the offensive against the Emerald Claw and Garrow.

    11-14 Olarune

    Elaydren books the group on the sea voyage home, the same route they took to get to the goblinoid city. On the journey back, several conversations take place:

    • Caelin talks to Elaydren about S.A.M. peculiar affinity for House Cannith and tries to understand why that is. She admits that she doesn't know, it's unusual but she has run into a few warforged in her time that hold the house in high regard. She doesn't know a specific reason why S.A.M. would feel this way. They seem to believe what she says.
    • Caelin also talks to Cornelius and asks for the artificer's help in deciphering the mysterious disc that they found in Whitehearth. He is able to identify it as a piece of warforged equipment, imbued with stealthy properties.
    • Cassian shows Cligs the scroll he found in Whitehearth, Special Autonomous Model Project, and asks for her help in studying it and for their discretion in keeping it to the two of them.
    • Cassian goes to Caelin and asks their permission to take the chalice and take some time to ponder his connection with the Sovereign Host and why he felt a connection to the chalice in Rose Quarry. Caelin agrees and gives Cassian the chalice.

    Olarune, Therendor, and Eyre

    After arriving back in Sharn, Elaydren again tells the group to check in regularly at a Sivis message station and she will let them know when she has more information. It takes several months for her to reach out to the whole group again. In the interim, they each tend to their own interests:

    • Caelin: Finds a master of the scimitar to give them lessons in the martial weapon. They also spend a fair amount of time searching for a magical scimitar and are able to secure one at a great price! (Proficiency with Scimitars, +1 Scimitar)
    • S.A.M.: He spends he time as he usually does, taking care of Cassian. He also asks leave to spend time with Lady Elaydren, which Cassian approves. During his time with her, the two spend a lot of time talking about House Cannith and forming a closer bond. Eventually, Elaydren shares the whole truth with him about the fractures within her house and also that she is secretly working for Merrix d'Cannith, the Barron Cannith South. Because of her honesty, S.A.M. decides to share everything with her, and gives her a full accounting of their journeys as well as all of the information on the ir'Bellonas.
    • Cornelius: He has gained a taste for adventure and wants to improve his presence as a book returner for the library. He goes on several missions with the more seasoned returners, the meanest of the bunch, and as a result has become a more Intimidating librarian (Proficiency in Intimidation).
    • Cassian: He does spend some time contemplating the Sovereign Host and sends a prayer up to Olladra....and then uses the ritual taught to him by the Forgotten Prince to attempt to send him the chalice. This time as he sits and does the ritual as instructed, after the cold and swirl of ice, the chalice vanishes. He doesn't immediately hear from the Prince, but he does feel different...stronger.He also works with Cligs to decipher the scroll he took from Whitehearth. Together, they find out that House Cannith created a series of warforged that were different than the rest, not built for combat but for infiltration. These were given as "gifts" to various noble houses and important figures across Khorvaire during the Last War. They also learn that each of these was imbued with a secret code phrase that when spoken to them would "activate" them. The scroll did not contain the code phrases, what activation means, but it did contain a list of some of the models that were created and it included S.A.M. - Special Autonomous Musician, given to House ir'Bellona.
    • Cligs: In addition to helping Cassian, she spends much of this downtime researching the nature of the elementals bound to devices and vehicles that she encountered on their journey, trying to decipher if there is a way she could have taken control of Failin's cart. In her studies she learned that it is impossible to take command of such a vessel without the appropriate dragonmark to command the elemental. Additionally, she learned that their containment is within a physical place and it is possible to release them by destroying that containment, similar to how the fire elementals were released when the crucible was destroyed.
