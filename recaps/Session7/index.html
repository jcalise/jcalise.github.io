    27-29 Zarantyr

    27 Zarantyr

    Dragon's Breath ahoy

    The group secures passage on the Dragon's Breath, an elemental powered galleon captained by Eltarra d'Lyrandar. They have two rooms with Cornelius, S.A.M., and Cassian in one and Cligs and Caelin in the other. After settling in, some of them decide to talk to some of the other passengers. Cornelius becomes almost immediately seasick and S.A.M. ends up spending most of the voyage tending to him and avoiding the investigative blunders of the rest of the group.

    The other passengers include:

    • A pair of gnome traders
    • A Valenar elf who seems particularly aloof
    • A human diplomat from Breland and her assistant.
    • A young gnome
    • Three humans in the livery of House Cannith
    • A human priest of the Sovereign Host
    • A pair of goblin merchants

    Don't be suspicious, don't be suspicious

    Cassian decides that he would like to speak with the group of Cannith workers and find out some information from them and see if they are also looking for the schema. He learns that they are traveling to Korranberg to work on a project but they are otherwise tight lipped about their work. They spend much of their time examining the workings of the elemental galleon. One of the trip catches his attention, because she is watching him, and he decides to talk more directly to her. She introduces herself as Karin d'Cannith and has much of the same story as the others. Cassian decides he needs a cover, so tells her that he is a biologist named Diego Dejoya traveling to find rare flora.

    Cligs and Caelin decide to go speak with the pair of goblins, intending to determine if they are Daask agents or not. She uses the same accept she previously used with the group in Callestan, but these don't respond the same. They tell her they are traders, hoping to make their fortune in Darguun. Cligs tells them that her name is Xerox and that Fen (Caelin) is her finance and that they have just finished their honeymoon in Sharn (yep, that makes no sense but whatev). She determines that the main thing that they deal in is weapons and it seems like they've at least sold to Daask but they don't appear to be directly involved with the group.

    Later, at dinner, the priest approaches Caelin who they saw talking to the goblins, and wearing an insignia of Aureon, and praises them on preaching to them. He shares that he is heading to Darguun to attempt to spread the faith of the Sovereign among the Darguuls. He seems extra impressed when Cligs, as Xerox, tells them that they are getting married.

    28 Zarantyr

    Burying the hatchet.....maybe

    The previous day, S.A.M. had given Caelin a note saying that he hadn't been lying to them about anything and that if Caelin wanted to ask him, or accuse him, of anything that they could meet at midnight to discuss. They have their conversation but it doesn't seem to have the positive effect that either might have hoped for.

    Uh oh

    Later that day, Caelin decide to have a conversation with the House Cannith trio but disguised as Cornelius, who is also of Cannith South, and see if they can get any more information. They have a conversation with Karin and attempt to get more information from her, instead they raise her suspicions even more by probing too much into her project, which is apparently taboo in House Cannith. Fortunately, none of the trio seems to know Cornelius directly and only by name so they are able avoid further issues with not knowing more about Cornelius.

    Korranberg

    Shortly after dinner the ship arrives in Korranberg. There is a three-hour layover while most of the passengers disembark and the crew bring on additional cargo bound for Rhukaan Draaal. A new passenger also boards, a hobgoblin diplomat that the group later learns is named Eral. Their time in port passes uneventfully.

    Fey-what!?

    Later that evening, Cassian is alone on the stern of the ship looking at the moonlight on his hands - as one does. He is startled a moment later when a coating of ice flashes over his body. It lasts for a few moments before dissipating. He finds in his hand a marble that he had played with a lot as a child but had become lost at some point. He hears the voice that had been whispering to him throughout his life more clearly and directly than ever say to him: "What is gone is not forgotten. This is but a taste of the power I can give you.....I will see you in your dreams." Cassian spends the rest of the night and much of the next day exploring potential powers, overcome with excitement about finally manifesting magic.

    29 Zarantyr

    Is it a bird? A plane? Definitely a spectral owl.

    Having departed Korranberg the previous evening, Caelin is up on deck when they notice something in the sky that appears to be tracking the boat. They go tell Cligs who comes to investigate and the two of them determine that it is some type of owl and it is definitely following them, it also appears to be spectral.

    Cligs decides to speak to the captain about this, who seems to indicate that they are aware and it has been following them for some time. She doesn't seem that concerned as she hasn't seen any suspicious vessels following them so is dismissive of Cligs' concern. Cligs does not take this well and in the ensuing conversation the captain believes Cligs has made threats, so when she leaves she has a sailor follow her.

    S.A.M. has had enough

    Cligs, instead of going back to her room, goes to speak with S.A.M. and Cassian about what they have seen. S.A.M. is frustrated that Cligs brough the sailor back to their room and put them on the captain's bad side. He goes to the sailor and convinces him to leave and that S.A.M. will take responsibility for Cligs for the remainder of their journey.

    Skelebones

    As the ship pulls in to Kraken Bay for the last leg of the journey they sail into the first poor weather of the trip, a patchy blanket of fog covers most of the Bay. Caelin is alone on deck, except for the crew, when they get an odd feeling coming from the bow. Going to investigate, they uncover a group of skeletons boarding the ship seemingly directly from the water. They are able to alert the rest of the party and the captain as even more skeletons make their way on board.

    Eventually Eltarra is able to clear the fog using her dragonmark to aide the party in seeing the enemy and also lends several of her sailors to assist the party in dealing with the threat. The group is able to defeat all of the skeletons and Eltarra promises them a reward for aiding her ship when they arrive in Rhukaan Draal.
