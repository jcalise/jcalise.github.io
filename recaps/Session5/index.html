    4 Zarantyr

    Let's ambush!

    After recovering the items from the hidden vault in the Foundry, and with Cligs having warned everyone about her Alarm spell being triggered the group sets themselves up to be the ones doing the ambushing for change. Cligs is on the roof being on lookout while the others hide just inside the doors. They are expecting that Saber will need to use fire as they did to avoid the beetle swarms.

    Eventually, instead of seeing a torch coming her way Cligs hears a thunderous crash in the distance that echoes throughout the cavern. A short while later she hears another one and quickly afterwards is able to make out a warforged just as they take aim at her with a crossbow. She is able to dodge the attack and alert her compatriots.

    Who's this weirdo?

    Battle ensues but the group also notices that someone seems to be coming up behind the warforged. At first they aren't sure what this person is up to, but they also begin attacking Saber - enemy of my enemy and all that. During the fighting Caelin is able to make out that this person bears the Mark of Marking on their left-hand.

    Let's end this

    The group makes an effort to convince Saber to give up but he will only do so in exchange for the schema, so naturally they will need to eliminate him. S.A.M. is able to incapacitate Saber for several rounds while the rest of the group attack him. Eventually they are able to kill Saber and as he dies two things happen. First, the familiar Final Messenger emerges from his chest; being in a vast cavern it has an easier time trying to get away. Caelin makes an attempt to down the Messenger but isn't able to and it unfortunately gets away. Secondly, a black band of metal that had been over Saber's eyes appears to loosen and fall away from his face.

    Back to the weirdo

    The elderly human identifies themselves as Cornelius Spindleby, a dragonmarked member of House Cannith South. He thanks them for helping him deal with the warforged who took a book from the library Cornelius Scribes at and he was trying to get it back. Cornelius requests that he recover the book from the body. Cligs won't have any of that and searches the body and put the weird visor, Saber's +1 Longsword, and the book she finds into the Bag of Holding and tells Cornelius they can discuss later when they have verified who he is.

    A vision in glamerweave

    The group decide to immediately return to Elaydren to deliver the items, collect their reward, and get some intel on the newcomer. They send a message to her via a House Sivis telegram station and head for the Broken Anvil. They setup in the backroom and wait for her to arrive. Unlike last time, when she was being very inconspicuous, Elaydren really stands out when she arrives. She is dressed in a floor-length shimmer blue glamerweave gown and is accompanied by two well-built guards in House Cannith livery. They give her the schema and she thanks them for their service and provides them with a House Kundarak Letter of Credit for 1,000gp. Ever the opportunist, Cligs shakes Elaydren down for an additional days stipend of 100gp.

    Elaydren's bodyguard is able to confirm that they have seen Cornelius at the Cannith South enclave here in Sharn. She also verifies his Mark of Making is legitimate and that the book he was seeking, Explorations of UnderSharn: An Examination of pre-Galifaran Artifacts by Bonal Geldem, is a legitimate text. She returns the book to Cornelius.

    Elaydren tells them to periodically check in at the House Sivis message station in Barmin Tower and she may have more work for them in the future. She offers to identify the properties of any magical items they may have found, they give her the three unknown items they have:

    • The weird black kink visor (Night Visor)
    • She lets them know it's an item that a warforged can attune to be able to have 60 ft of night vision.
    • The well-made chain shirt
    • Will provide an AC bonus equal to the wearer's dexterity modifier, up to +3
    • The rapier with the flame motif and topaz pommel
    • Is a +1 rapier that the wielder can use a command word to cause illusory flames to engulf the damage-dealing part of the weapon. These flames shed bright light in a 40-foot radius and dim light for an additional 40 feet.

    They chat for a for more minutes and then Elaydren goes to leave, she pauses and asks the group if they might be willing to help in an unrelated manner. An acquaintance, Nora ir'Roole's, husband Drevan has gone missing and the Watch is being the Watch - not helpful. Nora has hired a private Investigative to look into the matter but Elaydren is doubtful of his abilities. She tells them that she will arrange for them to meet with Max Murlock, the Investigative, at the Black Dryad tavern in Middle Menthis tomorrow morning. S.A.M. invites Cornelius along to help and he agrees.

    Surely I can figure this out...

    Before retiring for the evening Caelin makes another valiant attempt to decipher the original schemas that they recovered from the Old Sharn dig site and uses Bonal's notes to attempt to decipher it. Unfortunately, they are not able to discern much more than previously known that this is an ancient Cannith schema and it is coded in a way that makes it unreadable.

    5 Zarantyr

    What a fraud

    The group meets up at the Black Dryad with Cornelius and they wait for Max Murlock to show. He ends up being over an hour late and when he shows up he looks like a wreck and comes to their table and immediately grabs a drink. He gives them the same rundown that Elaydren did:

    • Drevan ir'Roole has gone missing and his wife Nora is distraught.
    • Drevan likes to drink at a Middle Dura tavern called the Half-Pint and pays to get the place to himself to drink and think.
    • Two nights ago he didn't come home.
    • She went to Watch but all they have done is secure the tavern he was drinking in and haven't assigned an Investigative to the case.

    Max informs them that he isn't really an investigative, he just "plays" one to impress the ladies. He also has run afoul of the Watch on multiple occasions and can't go anywhere near the actual investigation. He offers them 50 galifars (gp) to take over the investigation but to make sure he gets credit and looks good. Cligs (shock) bullies him into making it 75, which he agrees to and gives them 20gp upfront.

    On the case

    The group heads to Middle Dura and that Half-Pint Tavern, on the way they send a message to their old friend Sergeant Germaine Vilroy to see if she can led a hand. Arriving at the Tavern they see a half-elf man arguing with the Watch guard who is blocking the door, the half-elf storms off. They chase him down and he reveals that he is Kyle, the tavern owner. He confirms some of the information they knew and says that when he left Drevan two nights ago he locked the door on his way our and the noble was alone. He usually leaves the back door unlocked so that Drevan can leave.

    They return to the guard with Kyle in tow. Flashing their writ from Germaine they are able to convince the guard to let them in to examine the scene. S.A.M. asks Kyle to do a quick inventory to see if they can determine if anyone else joined him. Investigating the scene they find out the following:

    • Amongst the chairs and table is a large scrap of cloth that shifts in color. It has intricate stitching on the inside and the colors indicate glamerweave.
    • Someone fought hard to rip this cloth and probably damaged the entire garment.
    • What is most striking is the pungent smell of sour perfume and rot.
    • The scrap of cloth contains a maker’s mark for Davani Fine Tailoring, a high end tailor that deals with exclusive clientele in Upper Menthis.
    • No one exited out the front door and there is evidence someone was dragged out the back.
    • Mixed in with the broken shards of glass are pieces of porcelain.
    • Picking through the larger pieces, it appears to be part of a mask with red paint, patterned like a dragonmark.
    • You have seen this pattern on posters around a few parts of the city, mostly concentrated in the Middle and Lower Dura area. A group of performers wears masks with red dragonmark patterns, with sporadic shows in Callestan in Lower Dura.
    • There is a lot of blood splatter and small spots of blood, but no large pools to indicate someone died here.
    • The blood trail leads behind the counter and out the back door.
    • The injured person was still alive afterwards and hurt bad enough that they would need medical attention and healing.

    They head out back to continue their investigation and see some additional blood smeared on one of the walls, as well as a person huddled near some crates. They talk to the person, who reveals his name is Bort and, for a small fee, tells them that last night he saw 6 people come out of the bar 5 in robes and one being carried by two of the others wearing fancy clothes. One of the ones in robes was bleeding badly and missing his mask, he told the others he was heading to Clifftop for healing.

    As they are about to leave, Bort grabs Caelin's arm having recognized the symbol of Aureon on his armor. He gives him a small obsidian shield-like relic with a red gem in the middle and says that holding it makes him feel strange. Recalling their studies and similar relics they had seen drawings of Caelin identifies this as an icon of a Cult of the Dragon Below, dedicated to Overlord Val Gultesh, the Shaper of Nightmares.

    Reviewing the options

    Knowing that Drevan might still be alive but not having had enough time to wait for Germaine, the group debates their options. They can either:

    • Head to Upper Menthis to find the glammerweave tailor and possibly track the Cultists that way
    • Travel to Clifftop and look for the wounded cultist and persuade them to lead them to the rest of the group
    • Go back to Callestan where they had previously seen the posters and see if they can catch up to the cultists

    Ultimately, deciding that time is of the essence, they head down to Callestan to find the theater and, hopefully, the cultists - and Drevan.

    All the world's a stage

    The group makes their way back to Callestan and starts their search at the Bridge, a central plaza that they had previously seen the posters for. They see the posters around but they don't indicate any upcoming shows or where the theater is. They decide to ask a local and, by loosening his lips with some extra rounds at the tavern, he tells them that the troupe usually performs out of the Silvermist Theater.

    The Silvermist Theater has signs that it was once a fine establishment, but time has not been kind to it. Inside is a large space that seems even larger without a crowd. It's dimply lit with a central aisle and two smaller side aisles. On the stage they can see that an altar has been placed and an unconscious person is laying on it. A cultist in a fancy robe is holding an obsidian dagger and chanting over the altar, there are four additional cultists circling the altar.

    The group attempts to convince the cultists to let Drevan go but the leader doesn't stop chanting and the rest of the cultists move to intercept them. They are ultimately able to use Command to Silence Echo, the lead cultist, and stop the ritual. Dispatching the cultists and their leader they see that as Echo dies his skin melts away and a decaying warforged frame is underneath. A Final Messenger pops out of his chest and, unlike the others they encounter, this one delivers an audible message to them before flying away: “The Shaper of Nightmares will ensure my return and I will have my vengeance in your pain!”

    The group recovers 180gp from the cultists, a wand of entangle, and the obsidian dagger with a Khyber dragonshard in the hilt. They rescue Drevan who thanks them profusely and offers a reward if they bring him back to his manor in Upper Northedge.
