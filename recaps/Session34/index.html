    6 Lharvion

    Cligs and Cornelius complete the remaining journey to Flamekeep, when they are arriving they realize that during the fight with the Elemental Auric had been grievously wounded and the ship's medic could not save him. In addition, Cassian was gone and had left a note behind saying that he was going to stay with his patron, the Forgotten Prince, in Thelanis for a time.

    As they arrive in the city, Cornelius takes a moment to inspect the device he removed from the elemental engine. It is a simple device, essentially a Khyber dragonshard encased in arcane circuitry with a clockwork countdown device. Inspecting it further it seems that it was intended to, after a delay to allow the saboteur to escape, weaken the containment field on the engine and allow the trapped elemental to escape. He also notices that the device is inscribed with the symbol of the Emerald Claw.

    A hyena, a human, and a reverend walk onto a dock

    The Docks of Flamekeep contain a motley assortment that includes many Church of the Silver Flame officials, warforged workers, average human citizens, and standing out amongst these is a massive Gnoll who captures the attention of most as she makes her way through the crowd with a small, frail looking human trailing behind. Cligs and Cornelius' attention is focused on her as a warforged worker comes lumbering out of an alley and pushes past a reverend of the Silver Flame. As they push past, the reverend notices a crossbow bolt sticking out of the back of this warforged and turns to look in the direction of where they came from. As she does so, a second bolt comes streaking out of the alleyway and embeds in the nape of the warforged's neck, it collapses at Gnoll's feel and presses a piece of paper into her hands and says: "The mark inside. Cannith. They know I looked."

    The reverend quickly tries to take control of the scene, ordering the nearby paladins to secure the area and keep the crowd back. Cligs and Cornelius rush to the scene to see what happened and the human with the Gnoll seems to be attempting to treat the downed warforged. As Cligs tries to investigate the body the reverend stops her, after a brief conversation they all decide to move the corpse into a nearby gatehouse to continue the investigation and introduce themselves:

    • The Gnoll is Yz the Barbarian, who claims to be a hired bodyguard for the human with her
    • The human says his name is Garrick Kessler who bears a strange mixed Aundairian-Thranish accent
    • The reverend states that they are Reverend Ira Drake, official of the Church of the Silver Flame

    Cligs and Cornelius also introduce themselves. Then the investigation can begin.

    Troubling Discoveries

    Cornelius examines the two crossbow bolts and identifies that they seem to have been made specifically for the purpose of doing maximum damage to armored individuals, or warforged. They are made of solid metal with a series of razor sharp barbs at the tip. It is also stamped with the seal of House Cannith's Fabricator's Guild.

    Cligs and Garrick review the contents of the warforged's pockets which include an identification card which shows this warforged is named Shaper and that they lived and worked in the Cathedral District. They also find a Writ of Indenture which shows that Shaper is considered the legal property of the Church of the Silver Flame.

    Yz shares the scrap of paper that Shaper gave her with the group. It has the Cannith Gorgon at the top and a series of alphanumeric lines of text. On the back, hastily scribbled, is the Emerald Claw insignia, presumably added by Shaper.

    Reverend Drake begins to try and restore order and sends off a series of Sendings using the gatehouse's Sending Stone to put out inquires into the information they have discovered.

    The group decides to work together to solve this mystery. Cligs, Yz, and Garrick will go to investigate the alleyway where the attack happened while Cornelius and Ira go to investigate Shaper's workplace. Before they depart, they question the assembly warforged who had been waiting outside the gatehouse for news of Shaper. The warforged share that Shaper had been agitated for the past several days, saying that they had noticed some irregularities in shipments. They also confirmed where Shaper worked and that sometimes Shaper would bring deliveries to Holtvan Freight in the warehouse district.

    Blood trail

    Yz, Cligs, and Garrick go to the alleyway and search for clue. They find a scrap of fabric stuck to a nail in the wall and a faint trail of blood leading away. Yz is able to follow this trail while Garrick is able to discern that the fabric seems to be made of a wool that is too heavy weight for Thrane's climate and likely comes from somewhere further north.

    They follow the trail to the warehouse district before is fades away. There they run into a Deacon of the Silver Flame named Pol whom they convince they part of whatever is going on at Holtvan. He gives them the warehouse's location, informs them that tonight is the last delivery, and lets them know that there is a passcode to gain entry, but they are unable to ask what that is.

    Depressing workplace

    Cornelius and Ira inspect the construction yard that Shaper lived and worked at. There Cornelius discovers several shipping crates, a couple of which seem too light and smell strangely compared to what they claim to carry: altar stone from the Trumpet Mountains. He finds a manifest that shows that these, along with several others over previous weeks, had been rerouted to the Holtvan Freight warehouse. He also uses his Lantern of Revealing to check the area and is able to see that invisible Emerald Claw insignia are marked on the crates.

    The Reverend is disturbed by the conditions of the warforged, they had not been aware of how they had been treated until now. They question a few of them about Shaper and how they are treated. Ultimately, the warforged give similar information about Shaper and share that the overseer of this construction yard, Bishop Beref Neskus, does not seem to have much compassion for them. The reverend gives the group of warforged 40 silver.

    The warehouse

    Yz and Garrick provided a distraction that allowed Cligs to scout the outside of the building where she found an open window that had smoke pouring out of it. She used this window to allow Lizzard McGuire to scout the inside of the building, showing that there were four workers emptying crates, repackaging them, and then burning the empty ones. There was also a desk with three documents on it. In addition, there were two guards outside.

    The crew meets briefly at the Brass Wolf to share information and then heads out deal with the warehouse. Finding evidence that the workers were Emerald Claw, Cornelius and Yz opt for the hammer approach to dealing with this situation. The Gnoll eviscerates the two guards outside and smashes in the front door. Garrick, Cornelius, and Ira enter the back with Cornelius and Ira immediately engaging the enemy and Garrick swiping the documents.

    While dealing with the 6 workers, the leader of this cell arrives, Devrak, and demands the manifest in exchange for their lives....he clearly couldn't see how this battle was going. The party makes relatively quick work of everyone and Reverend Drake puts their corpses on the fire. When a patrolling Paladin comes to investigate, they all run away.

    Inspecting the documents one is partially completed letter stating: "The final Flamekeep transfer is prepared for tomorrow's wagon. The Flamekeep route should be closed after this — the worker situation was handled but drew too much attention." Additionally, there was a manifest which indicates these, and other, crates were being sent on a House Orien transit route to the Ironroot Mountains where they will be claimed by a "Mr. Thul" for final delivery. Finally, there is a telegram bearing the Mark of Scribing to Devrak talking about a device being in place and an operation proceeding, it appears to be about a day old.
