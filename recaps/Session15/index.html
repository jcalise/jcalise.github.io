    Session 15: 15-16 Eyre

    15 Eyre

    As dusk begins to fall across the city, Caelin rejoins the group at the Lewd Pirate and stats to fill them in on where the second gentleman went. As they are speaking a somewhat familiar swirl of ice forms in the air and pop Cassian reappears before them. S.A.M. quickly attempts to distract from this scene and get the group to move to their inn to continue the conversation.

    The group heads to check-in and debrief on what they learned, what happened to Cassian, and what their plans are. Cligs convinces the halfling behind the front desk of the Gold Dragon Inn to upgrade them to nicer rooms. The group discuses their options and decides to head out to scope out both Embassies.

    Old Town, Korranberg

    Both embassies are located in the Old Town district, a few blocks from each other. The first stop at the Aundairian Embassy and notice that it has scaffolding being taken down and a fair number of guards. They see signage indicating that a Masquerade Ball is being held on 17 Eyre to celebrate the opening of the embassy. Lots detect a lot of magic in and around the building. They also see a fairly well secured door set in the back wall on the opposite side of the building from the main gate.

    They continue on to the Karrnathi Embassy, it is has brutalist style, mostly solid walls and tall columns decorating the outside of it. There are many guards stationed at the entrance and several groups of guards patrolling the outside. They decide to send Cligs' familiar to attempt to go inside the embassy and see what it can find. She has Lizzard McGuire crawl up the wall and into an open window while they sit and have traditional Karrn food at a nearby cafe - meat, cheese, and a chocolate-cherry cake. Cligs loses connection with the lizard when passing through a version of Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum. She is unable to connect to her and hopes for the best.

    Cassian fills the group in on what happened to him in Thelanis. His patron needed him to deliver a message to Thelania, the Queen of Dusk, in the Moon Court. He didn't know why or what the message was and the whole experience was fast and weird. To him it was only a couple of hours, not days.

    Eventually, Lizzard McGuire returns and meets up with Cligs. She is able to replay images to Cligs of what she saw for the 30 minutes she searched. The room she snuck in to was a sort of small barracks with room for 6 guests in bunk-beds. Snooping further, Lizzard found signs of Emerald Claw activity.

    The group goes back and discuss their plans on what to do from here. They decide that their best option is to ambush Grilsha and Lucan when they arrive on the docks the next day. They create an elaborate plan where Caelin is disguised as Neya having a picnic lunch with Cornelius, Cligs is disguised as a gnome dock worker, and Cassian & S.A.M. are at a distance ready to attack. What could possibly go wrong!

    16 Eyre

    The group gets setup for their plan and head to the docks. "Neya" and Cornelius set up their picnic on the docks. Cligs, now disguised as a gnome, takes up position near where the ship will come in and starts moving boxes around. As the ship arrives and they start removing cargo and people start disembarking Cligs, nearby, notices a solitary human woman surveying the docks. She seems to notice "Neya" and then step away from the railing. A few moments later a well dressed sailor with a sword on his hip comes off the ship and approaches "Neya".

    Before he can really say much S.A.M springs their Silence spell over the area which makes the sailor run back to the ship, drawing his weapon. As soon as he breaks the barrier of the spell he starts shouting "to arms, we're under attack!" As he retreats up the gangway, continuing to call out to take up arms, sailors take up positiona on the boat with crossbows. A few moments late House Lyrandar guards come out from a guard house to support the vessel and secure the docks.

    "Neya", Cornelius, and Cassian make an escape in a carriage - the latter two making a show of "protecting the ambassador." They get a message from S.A.M. saying to head to the Aundairian embassy and start staking it out. Cligs remains for a bit to see what happens but eventually Cligs also leaves and makes her way to the embassy. S.A.M. stays to watch until the boat leaves, he sees no sign of anything suspicious and never sees anyone make a last minute escape.

    Back in Old Town

    Back at the Aundairian Embassy, the crew again takes up a position in a nearby cafe and scope out the scene. They see that the scaffolding has further been disassembled and that there is a constant stream of workers coming and going. The guards seem to check IDs and papers for everyone that comes into the facility.

    Cligs sends Lizzard over the outer wall to get more information. She sends her back images of the real Neya working in her office, seemingly alone. She sees a courtyard busy with workers setting up and guards at each entrance.

    Cassian and Cligs decide to go to try and get an audience and perhaps an invitation to the Ball. Cligs disguises as a gnome manservant, Philip Crinklebottom - basically the gnome version of S.A.M. They approach the guards and Cassian uses his family name and his time studying at Arcanix to secure a meeting with Jayres Tachiot, the majordomo of the embassy. They are not able to meet with Neya but do get some information about the Ball as well as secure 3 invitations to the even. On the way out, Cligs "fakes" a tumble and drops Lizzard McGuire on the floor on their way out.
