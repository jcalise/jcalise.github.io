    25 Dravago (cont.)

    A feeling of breathlessness overcoming most of the group and as they begin to feel as if they are drowning, S.A.M., who is unaffected as a warforged, turns to face a hideous create - something that was clearly once an Elf but has long since decayed into something horrific. It's skin seems to be hanging off of it and its clothes are merely tattered rags. Around it's neck S.A.M. can see it wears a collar that appears to be solid gold, but it has scratch marks all over it and blood oozing around the edges.

    S.A.M. approaches to try and communicate with it but its voice seems as if it hasn't used it in a long time and it can only rasp out a single word: "Freeeeeedom." It allows S.A.M to inspect the collar, who doesn't see any obvious signs of being able to opened - no seams or keyholes. While this is happening, Muroni retreats up the ziggurat and when she is far enough away seems to be able to catch her breath and yell to the others to step away from the creature as its aura is causing them to feel as if they are drowning.

    Finally, S.A.M. decide to try Knocking on the collar, warning the creature and the group that this will be loud. Sure enough, a thunderous crash fills the enclosed space as he raps on the collar. Miraculously, the collar opens. Caelin reads the creature's mind and can see that it is confused but hopeful seeing the collar in S.A.M. hands. Just then, the create takes of running towards the portal exit they determined was broken and leaps at it. As it does, Caelin gets the impression that it has tried this thousands of times over the millennia and each has failed - but this time it passes through the portal into the waiting waters beyond and sets off - free finally.

    Cligs inspects the collar and is able to determine that this was clearly a mark that denoted this as a privileged individual amongst the Giant's elven slaves and it would have given the create the ability to influence other elves. She is also able to determine that it carries a terrible curse as well.

    Continuing to the Temple Level

    The group decides to move on, on their way back to the first level they pickup a couple of the dead Emerald Claw soldiers to show the Drow that they are true to their word. They pass through the charred out remains of what was once the intricate and beautiful crafted Drow earthen homes before the Emerald Claw destroyed them. Caelin can see that several swarms of carrion beetles have taken up residence here but luckily they have enough food to ignore the party - for now.

    As they make their way back to the front of the ruins they spot a small group of Drow that are keeping watch. They give them the collar and show them the corpses of the Emerald Claw and explain what happened. The Drow seemed pleased and will pass on the information to Balmatzoc. They make their way up the stairs, rising some 200 feet into the center of the ruin.

    As they approach, Cligs scouts out and see that there are two Emerald Claw soldiers guarding a large set of Giant doors that are slightly ajar with light spilling out. She creates a questionable impression of a Drow warrior speaking what Cligs' thinks Drow sounds like. While it's a terrible impression, it does the job of distracting the guards who flee into the larger sanctuary behind and call out to others inside about an attack.

    The Sickest Backflip

    Avoiding the main room for now, they explore a side room. It's filled with dust and the decrepit remains of ancient Giant furniture and trappings. Caelin and Cassian find a door and begin exploring this side room of the side room. Caelin discerns a trap that is positioned right before another door in this room. Carefully, they work to disarm the trap and are finally able to do so. The room beyond is pristine, no dust anywhere, and there is a pillar in the center of the room with a clear lens in a golden hoop set upon it.

    As Caelin and Cassian circle the podium they are able to identify runes glowing on the floor surrounding it that glow brighter as they get closer. Not seeing a way to disarm this and not being equipped to dispel it, they decide on another plan. While they are discussing their new plan, S.A.M arrives and asks what is going on, the pair fill him in and he suggests the wait while he goes to get Cligs who can Mage Hand the lens. He returns moments later, Cligs in tow, just in time to see Cassian pull off a back hamstring back flip tuck to land precisely on the podium and avoid the runes. He secures the lens and Misty Steps back to the group.

    A Questionable Choice

    Lens in hand, the group go check on the other side of the building. On the way, Caelin sneaks a peek into the large sanctuary and sees that a similar ziggurat to the one in the Dark Level except this one has what seems like a small sun set at it's top. They can also see that there are 6 Emerald Claw operatives in the room: 4 soldiers plus 2 of unknown disposition.

    Checking the room on the other side, they find a similar scene to the previous one: mostly empty except for dust and the remnants of furniture. There is a room beyond and here they can see a semicircle of Emerald Claw soldier corpses positioned in a perfect ring from an iron door set in the back wall. Their corpses emit a faint green gas.

    Cligs sends Meerkat to tap on the door but nothing happens to her. She then summons a Mage Hand to pull the door open and again nothing happens. Inside they can see a Giant-sized tooth sitting on a podium in a completely clean room. Likely the tooth that Phineas is able to tell S.A.M. that the trap is still active but likely only has a limited lifespan left, also that the green gas is harmless. While the group is debating what to try, Caelin walks through the doorway. As they do a brilliant green light streaks out and strikes them in the chest - they immediately drop to the floor. Clearly in bad shape, Muroni steps in to Spare the Dying to stabilize them and S.A.M. and Cornelius administer potions to help them recover. Before Caelin can consider trying the tooth on for size, Cassian collects it and put it away - it has shrunken down to human sized when he grasped it.

    Ding dong, we're Emerald Claw now

    It's time to deal with the threat in the large sanctuary. Cornelius and Cassian grab Emerald Claw tabards from the dead soldiers and Caelin Disguises Self as one of the soldiers. Cligs, once again, makes a delightful impression of Drow and S.A.M. makes impressive battle sounds. With that, the trio who now appear as Emerald Claw run into the Sanctuary and ask for help. They tell them that they were on the crew working on the door downstairs and that when they opened it there was another ziggurat in that room but they were ambushed by Drow. The person in charge, the one at the top of the ziggurat, orders two of the Soldiers to go with them.

    As they exit, they notice the others waiting who get the drop on them. Combat breaks out in the anteroom and flows into the Sanctuary. Launching spells at the figure at the top seems ineffective, almost as if they just bounce off the top of the ziggurat. As combat continues, Caelin is able to get close to the figure at the top and can identify it as a mummy. When it realizes that Caelin is an enemy it strikes at them and inflicts Caelin with Mummy Rot - a potent curse.

    Eat Fireball

    As the party takes out the mummy's allies one after the other it quaffs an invisibility potion and seems to be trying to sneak out of a back door. Cassian moves to intercept the creature at that door, not being able to see but assuming it's goal. As he closes in on the door, he feels the cold touch of death strike his face and is also inflicted with Mummy Rot. Arkaban attempts to flee after this but Cligs has decided enough is enough and drops a Fireball on the undead creature - eliminating it.
