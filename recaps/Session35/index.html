    7 Lharvion

    Early in the morning the group finds themselves regrouping back at the guard house that they first examined Shaper's corpse. Cligs, recognizing that she and Cornelius can't complete their mission for Merrix on their own, decides to approach the three newcomers about coming along. She then seems a bit concerned about the fact that they all agreed to come so easily and wants to know why she should take them with her. Except for Yz, Cligs is smitten and is ready to take Yz along.

    Ira shares a bit about his skills and the connections he has that could aid the group. Garrick takes a different approach and says that if they don't take him and Yz along, he will report them to Baron Jorlana of Cannith West. In this exchange Cornelius notices that the accent Garrick is using appears forced and is not quite right. Eventually, everyone agrees that the three new comers will join. Reverend Drake go to the Argentum and their home to collect equipment while the rest return to the airship.

    Alllllll aboard

    Back on the ship, Cornelius attempts to interrogate Garrick about their fake accent and secrets. Confronted, Garrick drops all pretense of the semi-Aundarian accent and reverts to his true voice a thick rural Karrnathi accent. He also informs Cornelius that he doesn't care about House Cannith and is only interested in hurting "traitors." Cornelius seems content with this, for now.

    Separately, Cligs has taken Yz up to the deck to try and get some information and lay on her charms. Yz doesn't seem to notice the flirtations but is also much more free with information than her traveling companion. She reveals that she is from Graywall, in Droaam and that she met Garrick in Sharn after having completed a job. She also mentions that she is a bodyguard by trade.

    During their interaction, Captain Ressa comes over to speak to Cligs and question the large Gnoll currently aboard. Cligs fills her in on the new additions and the captain seems to accept this without much pushback. She also lets Cligs know that a replacement elemental binder will be arriving around 6am and they can depart then. At this time, the Ira arrives to the airship and the captain doesn't seem too pleased about the addition of a Silver Flame reverend to the crew but again doesn't protest.

    Ira gives Cligs a Scroll of Detect Thoughts and the goblin decides to inscribe it into her spell book and sleep on it so that she can be fresh to cast it multiple times tomorrow during their interrogations. The rest of the crew take a short rest.

    Where were you on the night of 5 Lharvion?

    The crew decides to wait for Cligs to awaken before beginning the interrogations, when she does they claim to the Captain that they have news of the saboteur and have her gather the senior staff for a briefing. However, instead of sharing information they begin questioning them while Cligs probes their minds. They don't get any suspicious information from Ressa, Navigator Ilyn, First Mate Dorren, and Chief Engineer Sable. They all were either doing their work during the sabotage or the things they are hiding are not sabotage related. They finally explain to the Captain that they want to interrogate the crew, she reluctantly agrees and reminds them not to destroy her ship when they find the culprit.

    They rationalize that the most likely time that the sabotage happened was when they were berthed in Passage. They remember that Thessaly was a crew mate who came out to drink with them, but she didn't return with the group. They bring her, Bram, Faela, and Big M in for a group interview. They question them, with Big M discussing how she provides boxed meals for the binders when they are on duty so they don't have to open the door to the elemental engine until their shift ends. Cligs doesn't get anything from the others, so decides to dig deeper into Thessaly's mind - revealing the Detect Thoughts spell and pulling out what she did that night: she was buying drugs from a contact in Passage that she then sold to Tobin.

    Shifting gears

    The group decides to change things up. Garrick, Yz, and Cligs decide to do a one-on-one interview with Sylas, the junior binder who was poisoned. Cornelius and Ira will go to the crew quarters and start searching the crew's lockboxes. They don't immediately use Detect Thoughts on Sylas, instead just asking him questions. He shares that he can't really remember anything since they crossed the Aundairian border, almost two days ago. Cligs asks permission to attempt to probe deep into his mind and attempt to retrieve a some of the lost information. With his consent, Cligs dives in and prods deep into the haze of Sylas' memory and eventually finds something she is able to grab onto. An image of someone handing him a flask, Sylas tipping it up to drink from it, and as he hands it back the face of the person who shared it: Petrin Voss.

    Cornelius and Ira, unaware of what is happening with Sylas, go to the crew quarters to search their belongings. The reverend has no trouble popping the locks on the crates as he searches them, with Cornelius keeping watch. In Sylas' lockbox he finds items typical of a sailor: cloths, toiletries, and other common goods. He also finds a stack of letters that seem to be from Sylas' father, Reggie. Ira next goes to Quartermaster Ilvan Cross' lockbox and finds much the same mundane sailor gear as well as a number of books - presumably logs and other details about the voyage. Finally, Ira examines Petrin's lockbox which has a surprisingly sturdy lock attached. Once inside of it there is the usual gear but also three items that stand out. There is a set of House Sivis telegrams prepaid that would allow for untraceable communication, a set of 3 papers that when read appear to be gibberish, and a small, ornate dagger that has a red glass teardrop at the pommel.

    Coming back together, the two groups share the things that they each learned. Ira reviews the gibberish letters again. Cornelius can tell that there is some sort of illusion at work that accounts for why they can't read it and determines that the letters need something added to them to make it legible. Ira inspects the papers closer and notices staining and decides to lick it. He tastes a mineral iron-like flavor. Realizing what must be done, he pricks his fingers and drops several drops of blood on the paper. After the paper has absorbed several drops, text begins to appear. That part that becomes readable is descriptions of Cligs, Cornelius, and three other people Ira has not met: a human, a Firbolg, and a warforged.

    What's in the jar?

    The group decides it's time to confront Petrin. Keeping Sylas with them for now, Yz accompanies Petrin to collect him for the interview. Cligs Messages the captain to let her know that they think they have their suspect. To Yz he seems to be acting perfectly normal as he follows her, not like a guilty person caught. When they return to the others he takes the seat, Sylas leaves and they begin questioning him. He answers questions in a way to deflect and cause doubt, not admitting to anything. Cligs begins to use Detect Thoughts to probe his mind but finds that she cannot, she can't even sense him. However, beyond her companions and Dorren at the door, she senses 6 other thinking minds in the room. She informs Cornelius of this, who sparks his Lantern of Revealing and sweeps it across the room. It doesn't show any other people, but when it runs across Petrin's cloths a small green signet pin appears on his shirt, in the shape of a clawed hand.

    Seeing he is exposed, he drops the pretense of innocence and becomes confrontational. In a quick motion, as he is speaking, he taps the signet pin on his chest and grabs a large jar from his belt pouch and tosses it casually towards the wall.
