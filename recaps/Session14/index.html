    Session 14: 13-15 Eyre

    13 Eyre

    The battle concluded, the group takes stock of their situation. No one seems too concerned about Cassian, assuming he will show up again at some point - though hopefully not in the same spot. The captain makes an announcement letting the passengers know that danger has passed, the ship will stop for an extended stay in Trolanport to be inspected for damage and offers to transfer anyone who no longer wishes to fly to a House Lyrandar galleon to complete the journey by sea, which will take longer. He then approaches the group and thanks them for their help. He offers them a reward if they will continue on with the ship after Trolanport as extra security - the group agrees.

    We know who you are

    Back below deck, they attempt to speak with Grilsha who initially won't open the door for them. Eventually they name drop her and her brother and she opens the door for them. The group attempts to get her to trust them and to give them information by revealing what they know. Grilsha is unmoved by this and is adamant that she is heading to Korranberg on vacation and that she has not seen her brother in several weeks and has no idea what he has been up to. She does remind them that he works for the King's Citadel, so it's possible he is on a mission for the king and not to get in the way.

    The group is unable to convince Grilsha or see through any deception she may be presenting them with. S.A.M. resorts to telling her he will stand outside her door until she comes to her senses. Cligs manages to sneak her familiar into Grilsha's room during this conversation. S.A.M. is true to his words and stands outside her door. Cligs periodically swaps senses with the lizard to check out what is happening but it generally is the same scene, she is keeping to herself and occasionally talking quietly out loud.

    At dinner time, a crew member comes to bring Grilsha a meal since she didn't feel comfortable leaving the cabin. On his way out, he tells S.A.M. that she would like him to leave and she is making her uncomfortable. In turn, S.A.M. implies to the crewman that he believes Grilsha was an inside operative on the attack on the ship and provides his "evidence" and encourages the crewman to take this news to the captain - he says he will.

    Gottem!

    Shortly after dinner, Cligs does another swap senses with the lizard and this time is surprised to find that a man is in the room with Grilsha. She attempts to get the lizard close enough to hear what they are saying but in doing so the man notices the familiar and snatches it up and crushes it. The sensation is jarring for Cligs but she also had a chance to see the man's face - it's Lucan Stellos. She rushes to the door and calls S.A.M. over to share the news with him and figure out how to deal with this. They decide to confront them now and go to do so; however, they find that the room is wide open and appears to be empty. S.A.M. decides it's time to bring the captain in on this and goes up to his cabin to speak to him. When he enters, he is surprised to find two armed guards and Grilsha, the captain seems quite angry. As S.A.M. begins to explain the situation the captain cuts him off. The captain says that Grilsha has explained that S.A.M. has been harassing her and making her uncomfortable. He orders S.A.M. to be confined to his quarters for the remainder of the journey and an armed guard will be stationed there to ensure that they don't bother Grilsha anymore. As the captain is signaling to his guards to take S.A.M. away he is able to discern that captain seems altered from the last time they spoke with him. S.A.M. decides to go along with this for now, he returns to the cabin and fills the others in on what happened.

    14 Eyre

    The next morning, the ship pulls into Trolanport and docks at the airship dock. The captain announces that they will be staying for most of the day, departing in the late afternoon, and that anyone who wishes to transfer to a sea vessel can do so. Many passengers disembark at this point. Cligs and Caelin observe the passengers, looking for Grilsha, Lucan, or Grilsha in disguise - they also spot a familiar face. Cornelius finished his task for the library and was able to call in a HUGE favor to make sure of teleportation from Sharn to Trolanport. They don't see anything strange amongst the passengers leaving and take Cornelius to their cabins to fill him in on everything. Cligs decides to set an alarm spell outside Grilsha's door to trigger if she leaves. There are no disturbances throughout the day. As the ship takes off for the remainder of the journey, Cligs decides to setup another familiar to take a look inside Grilsha's cabin. Maneuvering the creature up to the porthole and peaking inside she finds the cabin is empty! The personal effects and uneaten meal from the previous night remain on the desk, but no people to be seen. Cligs attempts to have the familiar sneak into the cabin's quarters but it is once again seen and destroyed.

    Detective Caelin on the Case

    Caelin heads up to the deck to where they know Grilsha was in the last 24hrs hour and uses their Clue spell to see her footsteps. They follow these down to her cabin and look around. They seem to eventually leave several hours later with a second set next to them and so Caelin follows them. The steps lead directly up to the captain's quarters and then never appear to exit. Caelin returns and shares this information with the group and they all decide it's best to leave it for now and deal with it when they arrive in Korranberg.

    My bad...

    Shortly after dinner there is a knock on the door at S.A.M. cabin, when he open the door an armed guard is there - not the one that had been in the hall previous, that guard seems to be gone. This guard, Steve Rogers, says that the captain requests S.A.M. presence in their cabin. S.A.M. agrees to go and the others come with. In the captain's quarters it is a very different scene than 24 hrs previously - the captain seems confused and apologetic about what happend with S.A.M. He says that the woman came in with a man and the two of them convinced him that S.A.M. was a problem and that they needed to keep him away from her - then the man disappeared. He also let them know that she remained in his cabin the rest of the night and then in the morning she disguised as one of the gnome passengers and left the boat. He offered them 500 gp as both an apology for the issue and as a request to keep this between them. He also agrees to give the group the information and contact of the captain of the sea vessel and also warn that captain.

    15 Eyre

    The remainder of the journey passes without issue. Upon arrival in Korranberg's industrial district the captain gives them the name of The Sovereign's Storm's captain, Thariv d'Lyrandar. They group also asks the captain if there is a place where unsavory sorts gather for jobs and information, he directs them to the Wanton Fool, a nearby inn that caters to outsiders and smugglers. He also warns them to be careful to not run afoul of The Trust.

    This drunk idiot

    The group make their way to the Wanton Fool Inn, S.A.M. decides to leave the group there and go check out the docks while they ask around inside. Cligs orders several shots and beers for the other two and tries to chat up the barkeep for information. After being unsuccessful at first, she presses further and mentions the Emerald Claw and tries to give the barkeep a 5gp bribe. At this, the barkeep tells her she should be careful with what she says and starts looking around the bar and then walks away - leaving the tip behind. Caelin notices that several nearby patrons have noticed this interaction and are watching them now.

    Meanwhile, S.A.M. deploys his forked tuning rod tongue to cast Locate Object and tries to find an icon of the Emerald Claw. As he moves around the docks he gets a few hits on this spell, one coming from the dock area and two others coming from within the district. As he makes his way back to the group he keeps checking for more sources. When the group reconvene in front of the Inn they are hit with an uncomfortable feeling of multiple messages bombarding them at once, all saying "Welcome to Korranberg. e are peaceful folk and this is a peaceful city. You, too, will be peaceful while you are here, one way or another." At this moment, Cornelius plumbs the depths of his knowledge to recall his live in Zilargo and remembers that the Trust is Zilargo's secret police and that they ensure peace by operating a massive network of spies, informants, and assassins - and that they are not one to mess around. S.A.M. decides to leave the Trust a note explaining their business and then the group heads out to investigate one of the signals S.A.M. was picking up on.

    They head out and eventually arrive at a tavern called the Lewd Pirate. It's shabbier than the Wanton Fool and rowdy on the inside, it is also very busy. There is a musical duo playing bawdy sea shanties riling up the crowd. S.A.M. asks the group if he can play a few songs and they are fine with his, he chooses several songs that include words like "Claw" and "Emerald" and the others watch the crowd to see if there is any response. They eventually think they have identified the Emerald Claw agent who is in the corner with another person. As they get up to leave, Cligs decides she should help separate them so that the others can deal with the Emerald Claw agent. She starts hitting on the other man and, when he seems uninterested in her, eventually manages to display an unnatural and horrendous ability to make herself vomit on this man - it's disgusting but effective, he leaves his friend to go to the back and the other man leaves the tavern.

    Caelin follows the Emerald Claw agent as he makes his way through the industrial district. They are able to remain stealthy as they keep a close watch on him. The agent eventually makes their way to the airship docking station and up to a carriage dispatcher. They are able to overhear that the man requested a carriage to the Karrnath Embassy.
