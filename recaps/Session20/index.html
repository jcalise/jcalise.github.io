    Session 20

    Having settled on a plan, the party separates. Cligs remains with Grilsha and Lucan; Caelin and Cornelius return to their seats; and S.A.M. and Cassian return to steerage. Back at his seat, Cornelius once again enlists Farnish in the battle against the undead and tells him to be ready. No one from the party decides to attend or eavesdrop on the meeting with Garrow, so Lucan heads to this on his own.

    About an hour after they all separate, Cornelius sees Lucan storming back to First Class through the Standard car but doesn't say anything to him. He also sees Garrow follow a few minutes later, clearly frustrated and angry about what happened at the meeting. Back in their cabin, Lucan fills Cligs in on the meeting. He says that Garrow asked him for the Vampire Blade in exchange for safety in Karrnath. Lucan refused to hand it over and refused to work with the Emerald Claw. Cligs asks Cornelius to relay the outcome of the meeting and that Lucan wasn't able to string Garrow along to the others.

    20 Eyre

    In the morning, Cligs thinks it would be best for Grilsha to leave her room and come stay in the sleeper cabin that S.A.M. had booked. She agrees and on the way out they set an Alarm spell to trigger if anyone other than Grilsha, Lucan, or the party enters the room. Passing by, Cornelius gives Cligs Sending Stones to hand out so that there are 2 paired sets: Cornelius -> Caelin and Cligs -> Cassian. The train sets out for it's next stop, Vathirond, which it should reach around 2pm.

    Around Lunch time, Cornelius heads to the dinning car with Farnish. While they are there, Garrow comes, takes a table, and orders a meal. He sees Cornelius and S.A.M. while there and makes a point of smiling and toasting them, though they make sure to give him a wide berth. Shortly after, Cligs' mental Alarm starts going off, alerting her to someone entering Grilsha's cabin. She starts notifying the rest of the group, including using her Sending Stone, telling everyone to get in position because she thinks the Emerald Claw will make their move soon. After some time, Cligs, in the guise of Phillip Crinklebottom, decides to go check out what happened. As she is crossing into the Standard car she notices 3 Hobgoblins drop off the roof of the car and head in and sit down in seats. As she is about to enter, a 4th Hobgoblin drops down - they glance at each other but don't interfere with what they are doing. This Hobgoblin heads the opposite direction. Cligs sends messages letting people know she thinks the Hobgoblins caused the alarm to trigger. Cornelis and S.A.M see the Hobgoblin enter the dining car and sit down with Garrow. They have a brief conversation, which seems to upset Garrow.

    The Hobgoblin leaves the dining car. As he passes by Caelin's cabin, which Cassian is still waiting in, Cassian decides to confront him. He accuses the hobgoblin of being Emerald Claw and breaking into the first class cabin. The hobgoblin responds, "Prove it" and starts to walk away. Caelin lunges at the creature and attempts to grapple him. Cligs sees what is happening and tries to diffuse the situation by saying they are making a ruckus, but closes herself and Grilsha into their cabin when it fails to stop the fight. Cornelius and Farnish head back to help out with what is now a real, if lopsided, combat. After a few rounds, a message plays throughout the train, letting passengers know a situation has arrisen and the train will be stopping while House Orien guards searches the train. The party starts to hear whistles from the guards coming. Cassian and Caelin render the Hobgoblin unconscious. Cassian attempts to pull him into the cabin but isn't able to lift him. Cornelius Jumps onto the roof of the car and runs away before the guards get there. When they arrive, they see Farnish in uniform and his explanation that he just arrived and they let him leave. Caelin and Cassian claim that the Hobgoblin shouted something about the Emerald Claw and attacked them. The guards decide to take everyone to the Helm to answer to the Conductor. Caelin steals the Hobgoblin's ID and ticket before the guards take him away.

    At the Helm, the Conductor listens to Cassian and Caelin's story and their claims that there are undead in the cargo and ensures them that he is aware of what is happening on the train. He tells them to return to their seats and stop making trouble and he will question the Hobgoblin when he awakens. Caelin is able to discern that the Conductor is aware of what is in the cargo hold but his duty to House Orien is to transport the cargo, as that was what the arrangement was.

    Next stop, Vathirond

    Back at their seats, Farnish no longer seems as eager to follow Cornelius into battle after seeing what happened with the Hobgoblin. The guards search the train after the fight and ultimately the train begins moving again. During the rest of journey to Vathirond, Cligs has Lizzard McGuire search Grilsha's cabin and sees that it has been completely ransacked. The train arrives in Vathirond about an hour behind schedule. Here all of the Brelish soldiers depart - including Farnish. The train only stays briefly and is quickly back on the move, trying to get back on schedule. Just before nightfall, Cligs leaves Grilsha with Lizzard McGuire to go check in with the rest of the group to decide if they are going to continue with the plan. They decide that there isn't really another option and they need to act before Garrow has a chance to launch his next plan of attack. Now that night is falling and they are in Thrane, they decide it's time, Cornelius heads to the back of the train to try and decouple the two cargo cars and the rest of the party heads to collect Grilsha and Lucan to confront Garrow.

    As he reaches the back of the steerage car, Cornelius, along with everyone else, can feel that the train is beginning to slow down - but there has not been any announcement as to why. When he gets to the coupling he can see down the car and into cargo that the guards that were posted there seem to now be fighting off the undead. Cornelius quickly acts, using his tinkers tools and knowledge to work at the coupling and is able to sever the connection between the cargo cars and the rest of the train. A few seconds later, disconnected from the magic of the bound elemental, the cargo cards drop to the ground. Cornelius makes to connect with the group, running along the top of the cars to catch up and let them know the undead are loose.

    The others start making their way forward, Cassian and Caelin on the roofs of the cars and the S.A.M. and Cligs inside. Once they reach the the end of their sleeper car, they are confronted with two worrisome events: first, a wall of magical Darkness appears to be shrouding the end of the car, completely obscuring the cabin Grilsha is in, and secondly they hear a woman's scream. Caelin and Cassian on the roof are able to see the Darkness but not hear the scream. As they are running they also see a bat fly out of the Darkness and head toward a nearby town. The train comes to a stop, still without any announcement, and the group moves to engage and find out what happened.

    Passing into the sphere, Cligs turns Invisible and barely misses getting struck by a dagger in the dark. She feels her way around the cabin and eventually is able to find Grilsha's body, laying on the floor with blood pooling out of a wound in her abdomen. She feels a very faint rise and fall of her chest; she is barely alive. Cligs is able to administer a healing potion to Grilsha to stabilize her and stop the immediate urgency of her wounds. Caelin, Cassian, and S.A.M. move in to try and deal with the threat; Caelin Jason Bournes into the room by swinging over the side of the roof and kicking the window in. As the three of them engage they are eventually able to wound the unseen assailant and cause them to drop their concentration on the Darkness - revealing that the attacker is the mysterious man they have previously seen in Garrow's cabin.

    Into the Night

    Eventually, they are able to defeat Winn, Grilsha's would-be assassin. S.A.M. convinces the group that, even though Garrow is a threat, recovering Lucan and the Vampire Blade is their mission and must be their priority. Cligs convinces Grilsha to come with them to save her brother. Cassian, S.A.M., Cornelius, Cligs, and Grilsha begin to head off the train to pursue Lucan towards the town. Caelin decides to stay and go after Garrow and separate from the party, for now.
