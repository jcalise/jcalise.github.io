    6 Olarune

    Having slaughtered the giant boar and harvested it's meat, the group re-sets their wards and heads back to bed. While the other drift off to their dreams, Cassian finds himself back in the throne room of the Forgotten Prince. He acknowledges Cassian's attempts to send him trinkets that he has found and appreciates the thought, but the ritual he taught him is intended for items that he specifically is seeking or that are special, not mundane items. With that, the Prince also gives Cassian a mission to find and send to him an item that he desires. This wasn't revealed to the rest of the group and Cassian didn't share.

    I think we need a bigger party...

    Their remaining travels to Rose Quarry are uneventful and as their sleep was interrupted all of the group decides to use this time for a long rest. As evening begins to fall, they crest a ridge and are surprised when Failin asks them "Were you expecting company?" In the distance laid out before them is the mile long quarry and the nearby village, which strangely seems to be covered in ice despite the fact that it is quite warm. What stands out from here is a bonfire burning in the southern portion of the village and what seems like a large group of people.

    The group takes time to make a more careful observation and is able to determine the following:

    • They see 4 tents, the bonfire, and two covered wagons
    • There are sixteen horses tied up nearby
    • There are 7 human shapes, wearing chainmail armor and half-faceplate as well as several skeletons in the camp
    • There is a periodic ringing sound coming from the further north side of the village, it sounds like a pick striking stone or ice.
    • They make out the emblem on the soldier's clothing, a green claw

    Checking with Failin, and searching their memories, they know that these soldiers are members of the Order of the Emerald Claw - a former elite unit of knights in the Karrnath military that has since been outlawed and branded as a terrorist organization by Karrn and most other nations. They also know that many members of the organization are also followers of the Blood of Vol, the former state religion of Karrn that focuses on the divinity within each person and advocates for the use of undead.

    Failin tells them that with the Emerald Claw involved he will go no further but will wait for them while they do what they need to do. He will give them 24 hours to return to this meeting spot and if they aren't back he will return to Rhukhaan Draal. The group agrees and then sets off to decide how to approach the situation in the village.

    They put the terror in terrorist

    The plan they ultimately decide on is for Cligs and Caelin to scout out the group ahead using stealth and darkvision while S.A.M., Cornelius, and Cassian try to pose as lost adventures asking for help/their services. In their scouting, Caelin is able to determine that at least one of the humans is a Cleric of the Blood of Vol and that their are at least four skeletons, they also discover that what they thought might have been ice before is actually a 12-inch layer of glass that was molten when it covered the village and is responsible for the destruction. Cligs is able to determine that the glass is relatively new, only about 4 years old - which means it likely happened on the Day of Mourning. They both also learn about the patrolling zombie glass dwarves.

    Despite this new information, the other group decides to continue on to approach the camp. As they do so, Cligs falls on her ass and alerts the camp to her presence. Two groups from the camp head out, each consisting of 2 soldiers and a skeleton, one to check on the disturbance made by Cligs and one to intercept the group of three coming their way. In the conversation that ensues between the soldiers and Cassian/S.A.M. it becomes clear that the Emerald Claw has no intention of letting them into the village, accepting their help, or in any way working with them. They also make it clear that they want them gone - now. Cassian is able to back them down from immediate violence but not to convince them of anything else, so they all retreat to regroup on a new plan.

    Taking the long way around

    Eventually, the group settles on skirting around the village by following the edge of the quarry and going in from the north side of town. There doesn't immediately seem to be any soldiers as they make their way into town again but they do encounter more glass zombies. Caelin attempts to sneak up on of the groups and accidentally slips on the glass, alerting them to their presence. The zombies turn and take them in, but don't seem to want to attack them. This group of zombies is quickly dealt with. To test a theory, Cligs makes an illusion that taunts another pair of zombies and they immediately attack it, but when Caelin is in their view they attack anyone but them. It seems that Caelin, disguised as a soldier of the Emerald Claw, is able to avoid their detection.

    With this new knowledge, Caelin scouts ahead towards the two relatively intact buildings. The first appears to be a large Cannith refinery, indicated as such by their gorgon and anvil symbol etched in the door keystone. The other is a temple of the Sovereign Host. Examining the refinery, they see one zombie that seems to be carting debris from the larger back room to the front two rooms and also hear the conversation of several soldiers. The rest of the group joins up with them and they do attempt to talk to the soldiers down but they won't hear anything of it and immediately attack. The leader of the group orders one of the soldiers to attempt to call for reinforcements while the rest dive into battle. The group successfully prevents the calling of reinforcements as well as defeats the soldiers.

    Finding the way

    Examining the room more carefully now, they notice three large fireplaces in the North, South, and West walls made of red, white, and black stone, respectively. They also see two statues at each of the fireplaces made of the same material. In the center of the room is a large map embossed into the ground that features central Khorvaire pre-Mourning, so it shows: Aundair, Cyre, Karrnath, Thrane, and Breland. Cities, topography, and mines are clearly shown on the map but nothing is labeled. Investigating further, they find on the back of the statues the following text:

    • Red Dragon: “Whitehearth, White Seal, NE 9.”
    • Red Lion: “Tallis, Black Seal, E 4.”
    • White Wolf: “Cabblen Hall, Red Seal, SW 15.”
    • White Falcon: “Blackhearth, Black Seal, SE 12.”
    • Black Knight: “Kronu Hall, White Seal, N 6.”
    • Black Griffon: “Redhearth, Red Seal, SE 7.”

    Taking this information and the map before them, Cligs tries to start at the white Cannith seal embedded in the white marble fireplace and walk 9 paces North East. This lands them in the middle of nowhere on the map. Thinking that this was likely intended for humans (actually, she was thinking that these racist humans were trying to hide information from the dwarves who lived here) and realizing that she is smol, she goes another 9 paces and see that a mine is marked. The location of Whitehearth is in a mine in the southerns hills of Cyre, roughly 30 miles east of where they are now. Using this information Cornelius works out the locations of the other locations as well:

    • Whitehearth: southern Cyre, southwest of the former town of Stillborough
    • Cabblen Hall - Southwest of Metrol, Cyre
    • Tallis - Near Sylbaran, southern Eldeen Reaches.
    • Blackhearth - near Baran's Keep, foothills of the Howling Peaks, Breland
    • Kronu Hall - King's Forest near the Brelish border with Zilargo
    • Redhearth - North of Shivairn, present day Valenar

    All this information gathered, S.A.M. insists that they destroy this map and the statues to prevent others from accessing it. Cornelius opposes this, because it is a Cannith historical site, but ultimately relents. However, he and Cassian decide not to help and to go investigate the church. S.A.M., Caelin, and Cligs work to remove the statues from their bases and pile them in the center and then S.A.M. uses their massive gong to shatter the the area - making a terrible ruckus but also destroying the statues and most of the map. Separately, Cornelius and Cassian discover and excavate a chalice dedicated to Olladra in the temple.

    Gimme that blooood!

    Deciding to head out of town quickly at this point, they head back north. Blocking the road at the edge of town they find a tall, emaciated man, wearing a beautiful hooded robe of black silk embroidered with the Emerald Claw and Blood of Vol insignia. He has deathly white skin, and a thick scar runs from the corner of one gleaming red eye back past a pointed ear. He smiles, flashing sharp fangs. “What have we here?” he hisses and eyes the group with crimson eyes. Caelin immediately throws holy water at him, which Garrow is able to dodge. In the conversation that ensues he makes it clear that they can't win, he commands both the soldiers and the zombies and they are already on their way here. He wants them to surrender but they refuse. Cligs casts Sleep and at first it works; however, Caelin tries the holy water again and while it doesn't immediately seem to have the effect they were going for, it does break Garrow out of the effects of the Sleep. Garrow waves a hand at them and says "enough" and suddenly everyone but Cassian feels like he isn't a threat and is ready to leave. They start to go, Garrow lets them, and Cassian decides not to push the issue and follows his friends.
