    Session 30

    25 Dravago

    Enough with your fake vampire ass

    Garrow reveals his true form to Cligs and S.A.M. as he decides it's time to end their interruptions to his plans. Insults are hurled, spells are slung, and swords are clashed. Garrow is able to down both S.A.M. and Cligs but, ultimately, he is destroyed by Caelin.

    Looting his corpse, hoping to find the creation pattern and schema before S.A.M. is fully recovered, Caelin is disappointed to see they are not there. They recover a glamoured breastplate, two flasks of holy water, a potion of invisibility, an ornate dagger, a feather token, 20 pp and 15 pieces of onyx worth 50 gp each. Additionally, they find a letter that seems like it was once sealed with the House Cannith seal - reading through it they aren't able to decipher it but can make out the signature: Z. d'Cannith.

    The group heads to the throne to review the items Garrow left there. They find the partially assembled creation pattern, with the three schemas inserted into it, the journal from Kedran d'Cannith, and all of Garrow's notes. They scoop all of this up and go to find a quiet place to rest. They descend one level down, Cligs sets an alarm on the stairs and Meerkat to keep watch. While resting, Cassian send the Tooth that he recovered (with Caelin's help) to his patron, who rewards this with three branches of a Thelanis tree. Tossing one into the fire, their Long Rest is reduced by half. Cligs also takes this time to review the notes that were taken from Garrow. They document his thoughts and the findings that the Emerald Claw had seen throughout the ruins. He posited that the fourth schema was hidden, accessed via the throne somehow. He also notes, at the end of his notes, that a voice has started calling to him...

    26 Dravago

    To the stars

    The group heads back up to the throne, now in the middle of the night. Muroni, Cligs, and Cornelius leading pairs as they have darkvision. At the throne, Cassian and Cornelius take up watch while Cligs inspects the disc that the throne is upon and S.A.M. and Caelin investigate the throne itself. S.A.M. climbs into the throne and as he does it's as if the stars in the sky descend around him and become crystal clear and just out of arms reach. He tries various methods, including using the creation pattern, but nothing seems to be working. Caelin jumps into the seat with him and is able to share in the vision. Eventually, a voice speaks to S.A.M. and says "Scorpion's Tail" and encourages him to speak it aloud. When he does, the stars in the sky seem to shift and one of them lights up. He progresses with the next 4 in the sequence, pausing before saying the last one and asking Caelin if they are sure he should say it while they are sitting there. Caelin responds by speaking the last star name out loud. Once they do both of them are encased in a column of light and then transported into a darkened chamber where they can hear a thrumming and see faint glowing columns in the darkness beyond.

    Good guy? Bad guy? Definitely evil.

    The rest of the group enters the portal and joins S.A.M. and Caelin in the vault. They start to explore and S.A.M. sees the first column seems to be covered on one side completely in various shapes and sizes of schema. The columns beyond glow with various colors of light and all seem to have both organic and metallic cables extending from them into something further in the vault.

    While viewing that first column, something seems to direct S.A.M.'s vision to a particular schema, which looks like it might be the right size and shape to fit the empty space in the creation pattern, and says in his mind "complete me." S.A.M. decides to comply as it fits with their mission and retrieves the fourth schema and inserts it into the creation pattern. As it snaps into place the creation pattern begins shedding a bright light and flies off into the darkness, illuminating the vault and showing that there is a giant warforged creation connected to the cables. A terrible voice fills their minds and the chamber at the same time, announcing “I . . . am . . . complete! Xulo is . . . whole! And now, Xulo has a body!”

    There seems to be some confusion about Xulo's intentions as he says all he wants to do it create, that is his purpose. But it also seems like he has a grudge against House Cannith and is willing to destroy life to get what it wants. S.A.M. tries to understand and sympathize with what he sees as the progenitor of the warforged but as the conversation continues it become clear that Xulo is a danger and the group decides to take him out.

    Destroying the cables that are attached to the giant warforged that Xulo attached himself to seems like to causing it some problems, so the group focuses on that at first. Eventually switching focus to attacking the body. Eventually, they bring the warforged giant down and the creation pattern falls to the ground. S.A.M. tries to pull the schemas out but they are stuck. Caelin strikes it several times with their scimitars and, eventually, the light shatters and the schema fall out. They quickly separate the pieces into the three bags of holding that they have and the voice of Xulo quiets.

    Epilogue

    Leaving the vault, the group cleans up the remaining Emerald Claw soldiers and undead roaming the ruins and returns to the Drow to check-in on their progress - giving them Garrow's head as proof of their work. They also return the air-skiff to the House Lyrandar air ship docked not far away and negotiate transport back to Stormreach and eventually Sharn. Along the way they tell the captains of both The Marlow and the Sea Dart about their alternate travel. They do not check in with Channa d'Cannith in Stormreach on their way through. On the journey back to Sharn, S.A.M. tells the group that once they check in with Elaydren he is going to go his own way as he needs to investigate the origins of his species and the information that Xulo shared with them. He also says that he is going to share his secret name with them: Secret Agent Man. Cligs and Caelin decide that they need a third part at the meeting with Elaydren and decide to invite Sergeant Villroy to this meeting, who agrees.

    They turn over the Kedran's journal, all 4 schemas, and the creation pattern to Elaydren. She rewards them with the rest of their fee, 2,500gp each. S.A.M. gives them each 500gp of his reward. Cligs gives S.A.M. a hair tie that belonged to her sister Suzie, as a token of good luck. Caelin gives S.A.M. a spy novel that they found for him that features a Firbolg as the antagonist. Cassian gives S.A.M. his personal ir'Bellona signet ring as a token. Cornelius gives him.....a high five? Then he goes off to meet up with Evine d'Sivis.
