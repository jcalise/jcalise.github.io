    • Steamy time

    7 Lharvion

    Seeing the canopic jar flying through the air, Cligs attempts to snatch it with her Telekinesis before it can hit the wall. However, her timing isn't quite right and the jar flies over the Mage Hand and smashes into the wall, unleashing the horrors contained within: a pair of Wraiths and their Specter minions. The group attempts to contain the fighting within the Captain's quarters to limit potential harm to the crew while also ensuring that Petrin cannot get away. Ultimately, the party manages to subdue Petrin without killing him and eliminate the 6 undead.

    J'Accuse...!

    During the fighting, several things were revealed that made the party take notice of Garrick, which leads to Cligs and Cornelius suspecting the man of being a "cultist". During the battle Garrick had wielded a holy symbol that was a shard of red glass shaped like a tear-drop, similar to the one that adorned the end of Petrin's dagger. He also showed strange signs while casting what Cornelius thought was a spell, with a red light emanating from his eyes. Upon deeper study Cornelius could tell that Garrick didn't cast a spell but did not press the issue with the magewright. Cligs, however, was outright with her accusation of him being in the same cult as Petrin. Garrick directs his answers towards Reverend Drake, seeming to do so to ensure the two would not have an issue, and explains that he is a priest of the Blood of Vol and that it isn't a cult and that the Emerald Claw have tarnished the faiths reputation but don't represent all Seekers. For his part, Ira smiles at Garrick but says nothing.

    Cligs tries to press the issue and Garrick again states that Petrin and the rest of the Emerald Claw are traitors. With that, the hostage, who had been bound by Cornelius' Rope of Climbing comes to and says that it is Garrick who is the traitor. The party attempt to question Petrin and even discuss how effective torture could be in getting information out of him. They ultimately don't think they can do enough to get him to talk and having already tried to read his mind (they don't yet know that he was wearing an Amulet to block the spell and his mind could have been read) decide it's time to kill him. They decide that the Captain should make that call and when summoned she agrees and suggests allowing Yz to fulfill her desire of throwing him overboard. The Gnoll happily goes to it and tosses the man overboard. Unfortunately, Petrin had a Feather Token in his possession still so his decent was slowed as he fell into Scion Sound. His fate is unknown, for now.

    A Flamite, a Seeker, and a Vassal walk into a Brass Wolf...

    The danger seemingly behind them for now and several hours of travel to go, the group decides to address some issues and investigate things that they recovered from Petrin. In no particular order:

    • Cornelius tries to question Garrick about the nature of the red light from his eyes but the Karrn is, as ever, deeply vague and unhelpful in giving information about themself.
    • Cligs examines the Emerald Claw signet pin and determines that while it is currently a mundane item, it previously was enchanted with Divination magic, likely some form of Sending, and so decides it's safest to throw it in the Sound.
    • Garrick and Ira discuss the difference between the Church of the Silver Flame and the Blood of Vol, particularly where it comes to their stance on undeath, but they don't directly address the issue of their differences.
    • Garrick warns the group about the dangers some of their behaviors could have in Korth, and reminds them that the Code of Kaius is strictly enforced with harsh penalties and limited judicial options should they run afoul of the authorities.

    In addition, the group discusses their options once they arrive in Korth for how to continue. They are debating between:

    • Pushing on with the original plan, which entailed going to the known secret Cannith base in the Nightwood, Red Hearth, and from there finding the location of Red Knight. An option that would provide them the exact location they need but would ultimately require them to infiltrate two secret bases.
    • Head to either Krona Peak or Irontown in the hopes of getting ahead of the shipment heading east that they learned about in Flamekeep, stakeout for Mr. Thul, and follow him to Red Knight.
    • Board the Lightning Rail when it arrives in Korth and take it until the cargo is offloaded and then follow Mr. Thul.

    Ultimately, it's decided that the first course of action will be to gather intel in Korth and then make a more informed decision, since they have time. They will split up tomorrow with Cornelius and Ira going to the Cannith East enclave in Korth for information and Cligs, Garrick, and Yz going to the House Orien enclave to look into the shipping routes. With that, they start to make their decent into Korth.

    Is it racism? Nationalism? Probably a bit of both.

    As promised, their arrival in Korth is nothing like previous journeys they have taken. The airship docking station leads down to a fortified pathway with one exit heading towards a border check. There are multiple armed guards with the White Lion of Korth emblazoned on their armor stationed here asking for papers. Though they are thorough, no one a head of the party seems to have an issue getting in. The party enters in the border crossing in the following order:

    • Yz presents her Droaam identification and a House Tharashk license to operate. The guard is cautious of the Gnoll but content with their paper work and lets her through.
    • Cornelius presents his Brelish documentation and when questioned about why a Cannith South man is here explains that he is retrieving something from the Cannith East library. The guard accepts this answer and allows him in.
    • Garrick presents Karrnathi papers, at which point Cligs begins reading the surface thoughts of the guards mind via Detect Thoughts. The guard becomes polite to Garrick based on what he sees in the papers but is curious why he is traveling with this group. He accepts Garrick's explanation and the surface thought Cligs gets is "deference." The guard ends the interaction by saying "Welcome home, sir."
    • Ira approaches and presents Brelish documents, which surprises most of the group though no one says anything, and does not mention anything about Thrane or the Church of the Silver Flame.
    • Cligs' interaction is the least positive. She opts to present her Darguul documents to which the guard sneers and says "Oh, more of your kind - great." Cligs get's the surface level thoughts of the guard, which are "trash". Cligs is questioned about her visit and it doesn't seem to be going well, until Garrick intervenes and says that the goblin is here with him. With that, the guard allows her through. Cligs resists the urge to probe his mind.

    Cligs is upset about the interaction and hoping to move past it, and get them out of the guards earshot, suggests they all go to get some traditional Vedbread and sausages and then head to a Gold Dragon Inn for the night. The do that and when they make their way to the GDI, Cligs uses her old House Ghallanda employee ID and asks Lindon, a halfling at the front desk, for the employee rate for two nights of fancy rooms. He agrees and they get 4 rooms for 25gp.

    Steamy time

    Ira invites Garrick for a conversation in the hotels sauna, which the priest accepts and Yz comes along though she stays outside to guard the door instead of entering. Ira explains that they don't have any issue with Garrick being Blood of Vol, despite what the Churh's official stances may be. They talk a bit more, with Ira making a comment about changing skins and Garrick discussing what it's like to be home again.
