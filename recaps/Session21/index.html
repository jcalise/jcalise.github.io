    Session 21

    Clang, clang, clang

    Caelin heads to the front of the train to pursue Garrow while the rest of the party, plus Grilsha, step off the train into what would be a pleasant Spring evening, were it not for the situation at hand. Directly ahead of them they can hear a loud rhythmic clanging noise. Quickly surveying the town they find a ruined sign that proclaims this to be the town of Caravan Crossing, which seems to have been a crossing of House Orien's caravan routes and the lightning rail, important for moving cargo. At some point in the recent past this outpost was destroyed and what remains is mostly the broken remnants of the stone structures of the town: the House Orien warehouse and the towns inn, both in states of collapse but semi-intact. In the distance they can also see a tower in the surrounding forest, it seems mostly whole.

    Discussing the options, they decide that Lucan would seek out somewhere enclosed, possibly with a basement, for protection from the daylight. They initially think that they will head for the tower and then work their way back this way if they can't find Lucan. As they are discussing the clanging, which was coming from the warehouse, stops and the group can see a skeletal minotaur charging at them. Though physically imposing, the creature is dumb and the party is able to eliminate him without any major issue. The group takes a quick look inside and finds that most of the roof has collapsed and there isn't a whole lot to see. Cornelius volunteers to search the warehouse for anything of importance, the rest of the group heads towards the tower. They quickly scan the ruined inn from the outside and don't see or hear anything that seems of importance and S.A.M. Locate Object isn't picking up Lucan's signet ring.

    To the Tower

    As they are moving through the woods, Cligs is sending off illusory Lucan's to try and distract anything that might be out in the woods. As they get close to the clearing where the tower is two things happen: first, they hear a loud explosion coming from the tower - but don't see any immediate damage. Second, Cligs noticed that one of her illusions has drawn the attention of something in the woods, that is going after it - she does not share this with the rest of the group.

    The explosion prompted S.A.M. to rush toward the tower. As he clears the woods, he takes in the sight of what appears to be a small camp outside the tower as well as a pair of skeletal gnolls that seems to be guarding the tower - these immediately get ready to attack when they see the group emerge. Grilsha calls down a Fireball on the gnolls, which sends flames up the sides of the tower and damages them. S.A.M. follows this up by Shattering the gnolls as well, which has the unintended but helpful consequence of blasting the door off of it's hinges and into the tower. When the door comes off the group can see inside the tower entryway, which seems to have a small troop of Emerald Claw soldiers attempting to enter a room - several of them are damaged by the door as it flies into the room. It takes some work, but the group is able to eliminate the soldiers and the gnolls.

    They decide to see what was going on with the door the soldiers were trying to get into, assuming that maybe Lucan had barricaded the door. They are surprised to see that the door is barricaded from the outside and that perhaps the soldiers were debating if they should open this door. Cligs uses her Mage Hand to open the door and finds the room beyond is completely obscured without any light. She tosses Lizzard McGuire into the room and in the second before she is obliterated Cligs is able to see, thanks to Darkvision, that there is another door beyond but, more importantly, there are several Gricks being kept in this room. She closes and re-locks the door. Examining the main room, this appears to be the source of the explosion they heard, the walls are singed and there are small fires around the room. Cligs can identify the remains of several Glyphs of Warding carved into the stone floor.

    There is another door on this floor, attempting to open it the door seems sealed closed. S.A.M. uses Knock and the door pops open, he enters the room and sees it has several occupants. There are several tables that have jars and containers as well as two with subjects on them, one a dead gnoll similar to the ones that were outside, and another that seems to have a grick with several of it's tentacles chopped off. There is also a seemingly twisted version of the same gnoll standing in the corner holding a scimitar, with what appears to be some fort of elongated neck. There also is a halfling woman standing in the room, apparently working on the two corpses. S.A.M. attempts to talk to her, trying to get her to give up without a fight. She seems to think on this for a moment....and then points a finger at him and fires a Ray of Sickness at him. Her creature joins the fight. She gets in several good shots, including a Shatter on Cligs and Grilsha, but the group is eventually able to defeat her and her her gnoll and prevent her from raising the other creatures. In here, S.A.M uncovers several papers of relevance including detailed notes of Destara's experiments, several letters that appear to relate to House Jorasco (that he conceals from the group) and a letter signed "Brother G" that appears to detail their ambush her. She was dispatched here with another Emerald Claw agent, named Nikovech, a contingent of soldiers and a number of undead to ambush "the target" when he fled here. It also details that she has a Familiar out in the woods that she is using to communicate with Garrow.

    In this room they also find a ceramic jar sealed with wax and contains 200 gp worth of diamond powder, a similar ceramic jar is unsealed and contains 50 gp worth of gold powder, a scroll of animate dead and a spell book containing the following spells: detect magic, unseen servant, and gentle repose.

    They ascend the spiral staircase up to the next level, which the main room appears to have been converted into a barracks for the guards they killed below. A second room on this floor looks like it might be the grim bedroom of a twisted mind - a dead bear with part of it's face missing seems to be magically suspended in it's current state and being used as a bed. They find a small chest at the foot of the bed that seems to again be magically sealed. S.A.M. doesn't want to spend another spell slot on it, so Cassian takes the chest and throws it down the stairs where it shatters on the floor and Cligs collects its contents: 80 gp, 100 sp, and Destara's spell book containing 1st level: Find familiar, jump, mage armor, protection from evil and good, ray of sickness, sleep, shield; 2nd level: darkvision, invisibility, knock, misty step, shatter, web; 3rd level: animate dead, counterspell, fear, sending

    They attempt to ascend the to the next level but find the top of the stairs are blocked by some type of wooden furniture. They try to push it out of the way but neither S.A.M. nor Cassian are strong enough to...so S.A.M. throws an Alchemist Fire at it and they descend the stairs to let it burn. Returning to the top of the stairs a few minutes later they find that the furniture has burned away but they are faced with a horde of Zombies waiting there...several of which have caught on fire. Cassian holds them at bay while S.A.M. uses his wand of web to spread the flames to all of the zombies. Cligs encourages the two men to let the zombies burn to death. Eventually they agree and keep the flames going and preventing the zombies from leaving and eventually are able to defeat them all. They head into the only door on this floor that they can see. It appears that someone had been in here not that long ago, there are some personal belonging that were left in a hurry. S.A.M. sees another door at the back of the room and goes to investigate. It's locked and when S.A.M. puts his hear to the door and he hears "Uuuurgh. Mmmgh." Deciding that there are more undead on the other side, they decide to go back down to Grilsha, who has been working on casting Scrying while the rest have been exploring the tower.

    A Final Showdown

    When they return to Grilsha she has finished casting the spell and begins communicating with her brother. They can only hear her side of the conversation but she is filling him in on what they have found in the tower, that they are here to help, and that they won't hurt him. It takes some convincing but but he eventually shows them where he is, having traveled through the darkened room with the Gricks and into the room beyond and down a trapdoor into the basement. They decide that the best course of action is to distract the grick with a food source and use bright light to shock them as they quickly traverse the room. To avoid having to toss a whole body into the room, they start hacking off limbs from the dead soldiers to use as bait. Cligs opens the door once again with her mage hand and the others toss their offerings into the room. S.A.M. fires up the Gem of Brightness and they rush through the room and into the second one. They quickly find the trapdoor and descend into the basement.

    They work on convincing Lucan to hand over the blade and come with them to safety. It takes some time but finally Lucan produces the blade and hands it to Cassian, seemingly struggling to do so. As soon as it is in his hands the blade begins whispering to Cassian, urging him to kill the vampire and that they can do great things. With great effort, he resists the pull of the blade and puts it into Clig's bag of holding. Once it is in the extradimensional space a sort of peace falls over Lucan's face. He thanks the group for their help and offer but thinks the best thing is for him and Grilsha to travel alone from here. He shows the group a passage he found that leads to the town and they travel it together. When they emerge on the other end it enters into the basement below the warehouse. The group say their goodbyes to the Stellos' and head back up to Cornelius, who ended up getting lost in reading a book.

    They return to the train and find most of the passengers hanging around outside. They find Caelin there as well, who tells them that they got to the Helm in search of Garrow but he was gone. Instead, he found that the conductor's head was exploded by Garrow after he got the man to stop the train. Caelin went with Jaralie to deal with the undead in the cargo. S.A.M. informs her of the infestation at the tower and she assures him that the Silver Flame will dispatch soldiers to deal with it. Several hours after they first stopped a continent of House Orien guards and a replacement conductor arrive via Teleportation Circle and get the train moving again. The party transfers at the next station to a train bound for Sharn and they make their way back home.

    Just Rewards

    Several days later the party return to Sharn and they arrange a meeting with Elaydren. When they meet with her she seems happy to see them but she seems exhausted. They do not question her about this at the time. She asks for a debrief on their adventure and if they have the blade. Cligs transfers the vampire blade into Elaydren's own bag of holding and the party fills her in on all that they have learned. She rewards them for completing the mission and information accordingly: - For preventing Garrow from getting the blade and returning it to her, she gives them each 600 gp - Revealing that Garrow is not actually a vampire she gives them 250 gp - Identifying Nikovech and Destarashe rewards them 400 gp, and - Identifying Neya ir'Krell and Meena ir'Edalis as members of the Royal Eyes of Aundair she rewards them 250 gp - She also allows them each to choose a potion of climbing, a potion of growth, a potion of resistance (fire), or a potion of water breathing.
