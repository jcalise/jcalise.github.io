    8 Lharvion

    Feeling that the rest of the crew can handle the situation with the Emerald Claw, Ira goes off to seek "The Midwife", an infamous forger who works out of the Low Ward. Ira off on their task, the rest of the party storms the safe house, with Yz kicking the door in. During the fighting they were able to work out that they were dealing with the commander, a knight, a priest, and a spy. They killed the priest during combat but managed to render the rest unconscious.

    Cligs took advantage of the death and incapacitation to inspect the safe house, she found a section of the floor had been excavated to reveal a sloping entrance into an underground area that eventually connected to the caverns. She also found a letter and supplies that could be used for arson, including a set of Molotov cocktails. The letter reads:

    Info

    LG,

    Suspicion of blood bag activity at Community Ward location verified with father. Proceed as planned.

    For the honor of Karrnath. For the glory of our Lady. AE

    Upon hearing this read out, Garrick's face darkens but when questioned about it he pushes on and suggests searching the Claw and then questioning them. Searching their belongings they don't have much of note beyond their gear but they are each caryring an identification:

    • The Captain's name they already knew, Lycia Gastor - the LG in the letter most likely. She also had an empty envelope in her possession with a return address for a location in the Waterside district of Lakeside, in Eastern Karrnath.
    • The knight is named Vedim
    • The spy is named Maril, and
    • The dead priest was named Uthar and had the same last name as Maril.

    A grim interrogation

    Cornelius ties Maril, the spy, to a chair positioned so she can see the corpse on the ground which Yz has started munching on. Garrick wakes her and questions her about what they were doing and about the missing 9th member of the cell from the caverns. Upon threat of being fed to Yz, Maril admits that there was a 9th soldier named Ebba Dorn who was sent off to alert their superior about the successful burning of the building and also the attack by the goblinoids. She either didn't know more or wouldn't say, Garrick had promised to give her to the White Lions in exchange for the information but when she was no longer useful he slit her throat.........and then immediately cast Spare the Dying on her to stabilize her.

    Next, they wake the knight and he sees Maril being carted off, blood all over, and the corpse of Uthar. Yz had stopped eating Uthar's arm when Maril shrieked in protest and Garrick asked her to stop. Faced with all of this before him and the choice of being fed to Yz or turned over to the White Lions Vedim gives them the information on the Community Ward building they burned down. They believe that Karrnath is being ruled by a vampire, not the real Kaius III, and that this building contained some of his "blood bags" - humans who volunteer their blood to feed a vampire. He also discusses the ultimate goal to put Lady Illmarrow on the throne of Karrnath. Once no longer cooperative, he received the same treatment as Maril.

    Finally, the Captain is roused. She is significantly more stoic than her compatriots and confirms most of what the others shared. She also tells them where Ebba went, to another safe house in the Commerce Ward, knowing that they would be too late to catch her or find any evidence. She also gets the same treatment as the other two.

    Interrogations done, they collect an Emerald Claw signet from Uthar. Cligs is unsure why the Emerald Claw would have an issue with a vampire being on the throne given that they know they have had other undead in their ranks as well as a high-ranking leader who pretended to be a vampire. Garrick gives her a brief explanation on the customs of Karrnath and how even though Kaius might be the legitimate heir in terms of birth, if they are compromised or weak that could be used to overthrow them. Cligs quickly looses interest and tells the group she is leaving and will meet them at the inn later.

    Cornelius heads out to both update Elaydren, only reporting on the high level details of what has happened, and also to summon the White Lions. He returns with a quartet of the warrior-police and Garrick takes the lead in speaking with them. They rouse the three Emerald Claw suspects and do their own, brief, interrogation to confirm the story that Cornelius and Garrick shared. Once confirmed, the three are immediately executed. Garrick asks for the letter back and the lead Lion agrees. When he puts it in his pack Cornelius notices a second letter in there but doesn't question it.

    Bookshops

    Cligs decides to return to the Commerce Ward and hunt down some book stores she had previously seen. In particular, she is looking for a volume on the Blood of Vol to try and better understand their beliefs as well as one on the Kech Nasaar. She is able to find one on Garrick's religion but isn't too discerning in what she finds and, though she doens't yet know it, has found one that is mostly anti-Blood of Vol propaganda. She has better luck with the one on the Kech, finding a relatively detailed history of the Dhakaani Empire - she takes this back to the tavern and spends her time while she waits for the others reading. She is in particular looking for why the Nasaar might be here, what their relationship with vampires are, and if they might be allies against the Emerald Claw. Her reading gives her some information:

    • Iraala and Muurat, the founders of the Nasaar, became vampires as a service to the empire despite it coming at great cost to them.
    • The Uul Dhakaan, a sort of collective dream, is a place that all the dar could be together in a vision of their ancient empire and share knowledge. However, the undead are cut off from this so while they don't necessarily have an issue with the undead it is not a desired state.
    • At the time of the fall of their empire, Iraala and Muurat hid many artifacts throughout the world to be reclaimed when their people emerged again. This is likely what Captain Brakk and her crew are doing and that their encounter with the Emerald Claw was entirely coincidental.

    Plan and Party

    When the others return they decide it's time to finally settle on a plan, now that they have more information. They discuss all of their options, the information they have learned so far, and what could be learned from different paths. Ultimately, what is decided is that:

    • Tomorrow, when the Lightning Rail arrives, they will sneak on and place something amongst the cargo they are interested in to later be used as part of a Locate Object spell to track the cargo.
    • They will take the airship near Lakeside, but to avoid drawing unnecessary attention they will paratroop on the outskirts of town by jumping off the airship and engaging Feather Tokens to land safely, since everyone has one.
    • The airship will head on to Irontown to wait for them, where they will arrive ahead of the Lightning Rail and then deal with tracking the cargo when it arrives.

    That settled, everyone, including Garrick, decides it's safe to get drunk and let loose a little. While a few drinks in, Cornelius finally confronts Garrick about his reaction to the letter they found and the second one he saw in his pouch. While he tries to be evasive once again, eventually he is more forthcoming and reveals that he believes he knows who "AE" is and that is someone from his past, a "friend" from his days at school. He isn't explicit but gives enough clues that everyone can discern he was once a student at Rekkenmark Academy and served in one of the Orders after graduating. He insists that it is safest if he doesn't share the actual name of the person his suspects but he will when he is more certain.
