    18-19 Eyre

    18 Eyre

    S.A.M. begins to talk to Garrow, loudly accusing him of being Emerald Claw and a murderer in the hopes of getting Jaralie, the Cleric of the Silver Flame, to side with them. In turn, Garrow is professing his innocence and trying to get her to think S.A.M. is a liar. They come to a point where S.A.M. challenges Garrow to allow a third-party to inspect his belongings and the cargo he had loaded - Garrow accepts on the condition that the Conductor of the train is the third-party and that S.A.M. and the Jaralie can participate - he also denies that he has any cargo loaded on the train.

    Meanwhile, Caelin goes to follow Lucan, still disguised as a sort-of-Lucan, and attempts to talk to him and try to stall until the others can catch up. Lucan does get distracted for long and quickly sets off again. Caelin attempts to pursue but loses Lucan in the Standard car, they are also unable to gain access to First Class. They decide to hop on the roof of the train and see what they can see from there.

    Eventually, they see S.A.M., Jaralie, Cornelius, Cassian, and Garrow make their way through the First Class car and into the Helm. In the Helm, Garrow calls for the Conductor and S.A.M. begins to explain the situation. As he is doing so, the Conductor seems to be willing to be impartial and do this search as agreed. However, Garrow pulls a letter out of his pocket and hands it to the man. S.A.M. is able to identify the seal on the envelope is that of House Orien. The Conductor reads the letter and it's almost as if a switch flips, he is immediately on Garrow's side. He informs the group that the cargo that was loaded is the property of the nation of Karrnath and not this passenger. He also says that he will conduct a search, as agreed, but that neither S.A.M. nor Jaralie will be allowed to search anything. She also seems to be losing interest in this investigation. They go to the First Class car and enter Garrow's cabin, the other man is there reading a book. The Conductor does a cursory examination of the room and Garrow's belongings and then says that the matters is closed and everyone should go back to their ticketed cars. S.A.M and the others agree and head out.

    Cligs was peeking out of the gnome-height peephole and spotted Cassian (his crotch) walking down the hallway, she grabbed him and S.A.M. and filled them in on her meeting with Grilsha and that she was willing to meet with Cligs in the morning to discuss getting him to turn over the blade in exchanged for helping to keep them safe. Everyone agreed she should take the meeting.

    The train arrived at Sterngate a couple of hours after the party's altercation with Garrow and Lucan. The train only lingered here briefly to take on Darguul cargo as well as several Brelish soldiers and a number of Hobgoblins. The rest of the evening passed without incident.

    19 Eyre

    Cligs meets with Grilsha once again, initially disguised as Philip Crinklebottom but drops that disguise and reveals her true identity. She explains, without giving all the details, that their mission was to retrieve the vampire blade and, above all else, ensure that it does not fall in the hands of Brother Garrow. Cligs emphasizes that they don't wish to harm Grilsha or Lucan if they don't have to. Grilsha, for her part, says that she is sorry about what happened at the Ball, but protecting her brother and ensuring his safety is the most important thing to her. She shares with Cligs that the blade has a power over her brother and is influencing his mind. She also says that given his past, Lucan would never side with the Emerald Claw. They thought they were simply meeting a representative of Karrnath that would help them settle in - not an envoy of the Emerald Claw. Grilsha promises to help the party with convincing her brother to give up the blade and asks Cligs to return after dark to speak with them again. Cligs shares that Garrow has brought some sort of secret cargo on board and asks for her help in dealing with it should it become a problem, Grilsha agrees as long as it doesn't put Lucan in danger.

    Back in Steerage, Cassian is continuing to attempt to become a "man of the people" and hanging out with DA BOYZ, his new posse of working class friends made up of Tommie, Robbie, and Four-Fingered Phil. S.A.M. is keeping his distance. Cligs, Caelin, and Cornelius make their way to Steerage to meet with everyone and discuss the meeting with Grilsha and their plan from here. Cligs gives the group the download of her meeting, leaving out the parts where she was hitting on Grilsha. They decide to go ahead with the meeting and to avoid any more confrontations with Garrow for now - but plan on taking him out as soon as possible. To that end, Cassian and Caelin go to make an attempt to scout out the cargo that was brought on. To support them, Cligs has summoned a new Familiar - a beaver, named Jennifer Beaver; she casts Invisibility on the creature so it can sneak in and help find out what is inside.

    Cassian and Caelin are not able to convince the guards to let them in to inspect again, seemingly they have received updated orders not to let anyone in. They do manage to get the door opened long enough for Jennifer to sneak in and get to work.

    The rest of the day passes by without much happening. Cornelius strikes up a conversation with one of the soldiers who boarded the night before. The man seems young, barely 19, and laments having only been able to join the military recently and not have had a chance to fight for the glory of Breland during the Last War. His name is Farnish Tells. Cornelius suggests to him that there might be trouble on the train and wonders if he could be relied on for support. The young man seems very eager but tells Cornelius he will only be on the train until Vathirond.

    Before heading to the meeting with Grilsha, Cligs goes to check in with Jennifer and see what the creature has discovered. She connects and gets the download. Jennifer shows her scouting out the cargo hold, which looks much as Cassian described it previously only with more cargo. In the center there are several large creates and a tall object covered in a tarp. She sneaks under the tarp and sees a coffin, without its lid on and no one inside. She looks at the creates and they do indeed have "Property of Karrnath" stamped on them. She gets to work beavering into the tops of them. While she is work, she sees Garrow come back and take a moment at each of the crates and then head out. Catching up to the present, Jennifer has finally made a hole big enough to see, and hear, what is inside - undead soldiers. Cligs dismisses Jennifer and informs the others that Garrow has at least 20 undead soldiers in the cargo hold and has recently reasserted his control over them. They need to make sure that dealing with this is part of their plan.

    The Deal

    The train pulls in to Starilaskur for an extended layover just as the sun is beginning to set. Evine leaves to catch her transfer to Sharn, promising to reconnect with Cornelius when he is back in Sharn. When darkness has set in the whole party heads out to meet with Grilsha and Lucan. Knocking at their door, she lets them in and takes up a position to keep them all in her line of sight. S.A.M. and Lucan do most of the talking. Lucan fills in more details about the situation, saying that he can't stay in Breland or travel through the nation, the King's Dark Lanterns will be hunting for him all across the nation. He reaffirms that he refuses to work with the Emerald Claw but still thinks Karrnath is the safest place for him to go.

    Cligs shares the information she learned about the cargo, particularly about the empty coffin and the likelihood that it is intended as a way to trap and transport Lucan to Karrnath. As she is going over this information, a note is slid under the door, magically altered to grab attention. It's an invitation for another conversation with Garrow.

    S.A.M. has an offer. Give up the blade and they can set him and Grilsha up with Cassian's family to keep them hidden. Elaydren can help with this and eventually he can move on with a new life. S.A.M. even says that he can keep on to the sword until they arrive at the estate. Lucan is initially hesitant, since this would still be in Breland, but is encouraged by the fact that House ir'Bellona is not a well known one and that his sister might be able to return to her life. Lucan's only modification to S.A.M. plan is that he would like to stay on the train until it crosses into Thrane as it will be safer and easier to avoid the Dark Lanterns there.

    Lucan and Grilsha agree to the plan. It is decided that Lucan will go to the meeting with Garrow to keep him from feeling like he will be forced to act. Once the train crosses into Thrane, Cornelius we decouple the last cargo car and the party will confront Garrow, with Lucan and Grilsha's help, and then they will escape the train and make their way to Cassian's home.
