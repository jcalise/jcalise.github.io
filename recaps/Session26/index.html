    Session 26: 22-25 Dravago

    22 Dravago

    Following their encounter with the ettin, the crew continue upriver until sunset and Chinxero calls a halt for the day, making camp anchored in the river. After settling in for the night, Cligs begins having conversations with the rest of the group about Muroni. She first talks to S.A.M. who is on board with either having her removed from the boat or just outright killing her if necessary. She then speaks with Cassian and Cornelius about not trusting the elf, they seem less convinced that she is a threat to their mission but are also fine with leaving her behind if it becomes necessary. Cornelius is interested in the two books she seems to have with her. They tell her she needs to speak to Caelin, who seems to be getting close to Muroni.

    While this is happening, Caelin continues their work of converting the crew to the good word of Aureon. Through their ministering they are able to convert three more of the crew, Jefford, Kirkwell, and Bulah. Finally, Cligs reaches out to Caelin to discuss their concerns about Muroni. Unsurprisingly, Caelin is not quite as on board with abandoning Muroni along the river believing that through their conversations with the elf she is being honest about her intentions and information.

    In a, perhaps misguided, effort to calm the situation, Caelin casts charm person on Cligs but the spell fails. Cligs is enraged by this mental intrusion and responds by casting phantasmal force on Caelin, making them believe that a swarm of bees is attacking them. Caelin runs out of the room and jumps into the water, at which point Cligs drops the spell and yells at Caelin.

    Muroni reaches over the side of the boat to help pull Caelin back onboard. When she asks what is happening, Caelin is honest about the conversation and Cligs concern and potential plan to leave her behind. Muroni is confused and hands Caelin the books to review, unfortunately they are written in Draconic script which Caelin doesn't read. Caelin then escorts Muroni to talk to Cligs.

    Muroni does her best to reassure Cligs, offering her the books she has as well to read but Cligs also doesn't read Draconic. Muroni tries to walk her through the sections of the Draconic Prophesy that led her to the group and reaffirms that her goal is to observe what happens around the group and to help them as needed, she has no intention of betraying the group or helping the Emerald Claw. For now, Cligs seems content.

    To round out the evening, Caelin tells Chinxero that Cligs may try to steal the boat.

    23 Dravago

    Daytime

    The following morning, the crew pulls anchor and they set back to traveling up the river. During the day there are no major incidents, but they each get involved in various activities. Cornelius is back to fishing and manages to reel in 3 eels, prized for their taste and quality in cooked dishes. Cligs tries to spend most of the day observing Chinxero but he seems less inclined to let the goblin observe him, but doesn't send her away. S.A.M. and Cassian work on practicing their music. S.A.M. also notices that the drums they heard on the first day still seem to be happening.

    Caelin attempts to convert both Wyck, the final crew member, and Quire, the first mate, but fails with both. They notice that Wyck appears to bear a tattoo of The Traveler, one of the Dark Six. After failing with Quire, the first mate tells them about Vulkoor, the drow scorpion god who he worships. After listening to Quire, Caelin notices a number of similarities to The Mockery, another of the Dark Six.

    Evening

    After traveling the whole day, the Marlow makes camp again for the evening when the sun sets. Cornelius cooks up the eels that he caught earlier in the day for the crew and the rest of the party. After dinner, the crew retires below deck for the night except for two who remain on watch, Wyck and Bulah. S.A.M. remains on the deck but not on watch, and Caelin also takes up a watch.

    When everyone is asleep, Caelin believes that they hear a noise coming from the western banks of the river. S.A.M. ignites on of the Everbright Lanterns and sweeps it over the area that Caelin pointed out. As he does so, he sees a large six-legged cat jumping out of the jungle towards the boat. A second cat also emerges from the jungle. Caelin uses Sanctuary to protect Bulah and sends her to go wake the rest of the party and get to safety below deck. The rest of the group, and Muroni, awaken and emerge to help with the situation and the apparent leader of this pack, a Displacer Beast, also emerges from the jungle to join the fray.

    Eventually, the party is able to overcome and defeat the cats. Sensing their defeat, they do try to flee but the party is adamant about eliminating the threat and all three cats are killed. Caelin attempts to skin the Displacer Beast and botches it, ruining the hide. Cornelius does the same with the jungle cats and is able to harvest two mediocre hides and a significant amount of tiger meat.

    24 Dravago

    Daytime

    Once again, in the morning the crew pulls anchor and they continue on the journey. Cligs again notices the captain being distant and asks him about what is going on. Chinxero tells Cligs about what Caelin said but she is able to ensure him that she is not trying to steal his boat, only learn how such vessels work.

    Cornelius fishes again and manages to pull out of the river a silver brooch inlaid with a wave pattern. He then sells this to Cassian for the former to use in a ritual later that day.

    S.A.M., Cligs, and Quire talk about the proximity of the drums coming from the west. Quire tells them about how most drow tribes uses drums as signals for hunting and war parties and to communicate in and across the jungle.

    Caelin notices a large mass of what appears to be jungle detritus float past the boat and then seem to get stuck to the back of the vessel. Caelin, Cassian, and Cornelius go investigate the situation. Carefully investigating the mass they are able to determine that it isn't what it appears and is in fact a shambling mound. After a few failed attempts they are able to dislodge it but it continues to follow them. Cligs steps in an uses Phantasmal Force to make it believe that a hippopotamus is coming to eat it and it flees.

    Evening

    After making camp, Cornelius grills up some tiger steaks for everyone while Cassian regales the crew with stories about Thelanis and the Forgotten Prince, all the while performing the ritual he was taught by his patron on the brooch that Cornelius recovered. At the conclusion of his story, ice swirls about the brooch and then it vanishes, along with a message for the Prince.

    Caelin, with the help of one of his converts, has a conversation with the captain about the glory and grace of Aureon and, finally, is able to convert the captain as well.

    Quire seeks out S.A.M. to let him know that he had been listening to the drums and consulting his notes and is able to confirm that the drums are war drums. He also tells them that he believes they have entered the territory of the chief Amoxtli and shows them the drow script that might be placed to denote an area as under Amoxtli's domain.

    Finally, Cligs summons a new familiar, Oscar Owl, and gives it the instruction to fly out for 5 hours to scout and report back in the morning on everything it sees.

    25 Dravago

    Daytime

    As the crew is breaking camp and pulling anchor to head out, Oscar returns and relays everything they saw to Cligs who passes it along to the rest of the group. Oscar shows flying over the jungle and heading towards the drums in the southwest, he eventually shows a number of groups of drow that appear to be making their way south and a large gathering of them several miles inland. He continues south and eventually, just at the end of the 5 hours Oscar comes upon a massive stone structure in the jungle that seems to be several hundred feet tall. The owl turns to head back and heads toward the river to fly back. As he approaches the river bank he shows that he saw a pair of giant stone hands rising up from the ground and, anchored just nearby, a small air-skiff. He isn't able to make out how many people are there but he does see a small camp and a fire burning nearby.

    Reviewing the information and determining the best course of action, the group decides to have Chinxero drop them off about a mile downriver of the place that Oscar showed them air-skiff. From there they will approach on foot.
