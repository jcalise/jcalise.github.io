    8 Lharvion

    When morning arrives, the group convenes in the private dining room that the GDI staff has setup for them. A Sivis Telegram has also been delivered for Cornelius, it is from Elaydren and she lets him know she is aware of what happened on the airship and is looking for an update.

    After breakfast, the group decides to split up with Cligs and Cornelius going to the nearby Cannith Enclave and Yz, Garrick, and Ira heading to the Korth Station and Orien Enclave. On the way out, Cligs stops to talk to the halfling at the front desk and inquire about the goblins in town. She learns that they have made camp in the haunted caverns in the Community Ward bluffs where that are adjacent to the Commerce Ward. She also learns that there has recently been what has been classified as a seditious act by the White Lions and that goblins have been blamed, several were executed as a result.

    The Gorgon

    Cligs and Cornelius arrive at the Cannith East Enclave, known as the College of Flame, where they immediately split up. Cligs, disguised as Philip Crinklebottom, approaches a woman in her mid-fifties who is inspecting a ledger at what appears to be a central information desk. Cligs learns the woman's name is Varya Harn d'Cannith and she is the seneschal of the enclave, Zorlan's second in command. Cligs makes a scene to allow her to have Lizzard McGuire sneak into the Forgehold, which she is able to do, but also gets thrown out.

    Cornelius speaks with the Forgemaster Brannoc Harn d'Cannith and a binder name Ilsa Harn d'Cannith for information on the enclave and what they are working on. He also learns about Varya's role and that she is the person who could approve him to gain access to the library here. He is able to convince her to give him 2 hours of access to the library.

    In the library, Cornelius uses Alter Self to appear like Varya and tries to gain access to the restricted section. He can't convince the librarian to let him in but he is able to pick the lock and gain entry. He reviews a number of records and is able to find a few pieces of information:

    1. A Kallus d'Cannith has recently been transferred to run a special project in eastern Karrnath - the location you believe Red Knight is located.
    2. Redhearth, the secret research base you know is located in the Nightwood, specializes in creating weapons of mass destruction.
    3. The project that Kallus is working on requires a Mabaran Manifest Zone to function.

    Using the last of their aloted time in the library, Cornelius researches Mabar and the properties of it's manifest zones. He find the following entry on the subject:

    Mabar, The Endless Night and it's Manifest Zones

    Mabaran manifest zones are infamous and almost universally shunned, for nearly all are harmful to the flora and fauna of the region. In some zones, life withers and dies. In others, it’s twisted in strange ways; plants may seek the blood of living creatures, or grow unnaturally pale and cold. Rot and decay are often accelerated, and disease can thrive. However, such regions are also often powerful sources of negative energy. Mabaran zones often possess the Necrotic Power universal property, and there are epic rituals and Eldritch Machines that require a Mabaran manifest zone. The Odakyr Rites that create Karrnathi undead are an example of this, and the Karrnathi city of Atur is built on a Mabaran zone. This often has a curious synergy: if the negative energy of a Mabaran manifest zone is regularly channeled into rituals or spells, it prevents that energy from spreading disease or killing vegetation. In his War and Death: A History of Karrnathi Necromancy , Jolan Hass Holan asserts that the plagues and famines Karrnath suffered early in the war were due to the Seekers who normally tended those zones being pushed out of them due to strategic concerns—which, in turn, forced Karrnath to embrace necromancy to counter the effects of those famines.

    The Unicorn

    Meanwhile, the other group goes to the Korth Lightning Rail station where Ira diguises as a random tourist and strikes up a conversation with a patrolling guard named Ari. During the conversation he manages to steal the guard's keys. While that is happening, Garrick and Yz go to speak with the station master, Jurian Bayne d’Orien. Garrick presents the manifest they took in Flamekeep and asks for information about their shipment, since they heard there were problems and wanted to make sure things were okay. The station master, a generally friendly and helpful individual, is happy to assist and takes them to his desk to look up the information. He gives Garrick the full route of the shipment:

    • Left Flamekeep via ship on Lharvion 7, arrived in Rekkenmark end of day
    • Loaded on Lightning Rail, cargo inspection, and will depart Rekkenmark on Lharvion 9
    • Will arrive in Korth midday on Lharvion 9, 1 hour layover
    • Will arrive in Atur in the late afternoon of Lharvion 9
    • Will arrive in Vedykar in the evening of Lharvion 9, cargo transfer to Orien Caravan
    • Depart Vedykar morning of Lharvion 10, arrive in Vulyar Lharvion 14 and transfer to Lightning Rail
    • Depart Vulyar morning of Lharvion 15, arrive in Irontown midday, and Krona Peak in the evening.

    He also learns that the shipment is scheduled to be picked up from an Orien Warehouse on 17 Lharvion from Irontown. Mission accomplished, Garrick and Yz head to the gift shop where Yz purchases a genuine Karrnathi wool knit cap with ear holes.

    Ira approaches the station master and says they are looking for a job. He says they could use janitors and sends him to speak with an HR representative. Ira passes the interview but when asked for their work papers says they don't have them and will return tomorrow. He is given a pass to return the next day and show out. On the way out, Ira sneaks into a nearby records room and is able to find a record of the contract for the shipments they are tracking. He learns that the contract is in the name of Vatar Thul, of Irontown, and that in the past year there have been 12 shipments, roughly once a month.

    Chaat’oor gath’atcha

    Having been kicked out of the Cannith Enclave, Cligs decides to go look into the goblin encampment. She secures a coach to the area. Upon arrival, she sees that three goblins have been hung by the White Lions at the edge of their camp, a sign in Common reads: SEDITION WILL BE PUNISHED. Beneath that a phrase has been scrawled in goblin that reads chaat’oor gath’atcha that she knows translates to "defilers are without honor" but it is a strange phrase she is unfamiliar with. She speaks with the hobgoblin who seems to be leading the group, who goes by Captain Brakk. Several things seem off to Cligs, their accent is unfamiliar to her, the hobgoblin treats the goblins with them as equals, and they seem to moving almost completely silently even though they are wearing armor. They tell her that it was the Emerald Claw, who they have run into deep in the caverns, that burned down the building and that they fought and killed 6 of them and believes they have drove them off. They invite her to lunch, lizard skewers and noon grain. While eating, she sends a Sending to Yz with information about where she is and to come immediately.

    A goblin in need

    The rest of the group reconvenes at the GDI and begins sharing information that they learned while eating lunch. Yz receives the Sending from Cligs and shares with the group. They decide to head out right away to go help her out and make their way to the encampment. Brakk is unhappy to see the humans approach but when Cligs vouches for them she also invites them to their campfire for lunch. During this conversation, Ira is able to notice that Brakk is using her ears to silently send signals to the goblins around her.

    Brakk tells them where they encountered the Emerald Claw and sends them on their way to investigate. It takes some time, but they make their way and find the remains of the Emerald Claw camp as Brakk described it. There are 6 Emerald Claw corpses, now partially consumed by cavern critters, as well as the remains of the camp. It seems as if at sometime since the fight, some of the Claw returned and threw some records into the fire. They do manage to recover one, which when decoded shows that the cells mission was the burn down the building but there is no explanation of why.

    The group decides that Garrick will use Speak with Dead to reanimate one of the soldiers and Ira will question them. They disguise as Claw soldiers and begin their conversation. They ask their 5 questions and get their answers:

    1. Why did you burn down the building? "Orders."
    2. Who gave you the order? "Cell commander."
    3. Who is your cell commander? "Captain Lycia Ghastor."
    4. Where is your cell safe house? "123 Sesame Street, Low Ward."
    5. What is the passcode for the safe house? "At All Costs."

    Sketchville

    The party makes their way back to the goblinoid camp. Mok, the quartermaster and cook, offers them a takeaway snack, which Yz and Cligs accept. Cligs also leaves Lizzard McGuire behind to keep watch on the camp. They make their way to the Low Ward and find the address of the safe house. It is a nondescript storefront that has it's windows boarded up in a rough neighborhood. There is a bar across the way where they setup to keep watch. Cornelius has Snailor Swift go listen at the windows and she can hear several voices. She then peaks in the gaps in the boards and is able to identify 2 wounded Emerald Claw soldiers in their armor and two that appear to be in normal clothes. Knowing that the White Lions don't patrol the Low Ward, the party decides it's safest for everyone if they eliminate this cell. They get ready to storm the safe house.
