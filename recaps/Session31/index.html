    9 Nymm

    About a week after returning from Xen'drik, Cassian returns to the inn he has been staying at and the innkeeper waves him over - a letter has arrived for S.A.M. but since he hasn't seen him in some time, he is passing it to Cassian. Reading the letter, Cassian sees that it is from his parents telling S.A.M. that they have arrived in Sharn to attempt to change the houses fortunes and that they need his assistance to succeed. Cassian takes this to meet up with the rest of the party at their usual weekly meet-up and asks them if they would come to meet with his parents to help out - they agree.

    The group makes their way to Gallanda Hall, in Middle Dura, to meet with the ir'Bellonas. The Hall is a massive complex of rooms, meeting halls, dinning halls, and caters to a wide variety of constituents. The interior great hall is fabulously decorated and filled with many people. Approaching the reception desk, a tall halfling greets them and sells them a fancy bottle of wine that Cassian is hoping to smooth over his reunion with his parents.

    Awkward...

    When they are escorted to the dinning hall Cassian's parents are using, the two nobles are sitting there with a strange Elf. His father looks strikingly similar to Cassian, but balding, and his mother has sharp features and a drink in hand. They don't seem immediately happy to Cassian and question where S.A.M. is. Cassian fills them in on the highlights of what happened with the Bard. His parents don't seem happy with the news but press on.

    They present the party with a job: a rival house has been given a prototype weapon to test and get into production, they want the party to disrupt their progress and destroy or steal the prototype. Anything else they can retrieve will also be rewarded. Their target is the ir'Faldren Forgeworks in the Ashblack district of the Upper Cogs. They have hired the Elf to aid in the mission, his name is Draconus.

    After discussing the details a bit further, they agree to the job and head to Middle Tavick's Landing to try and question some former guards of their target. As part of this project the Faldren's have changed their guards so there are some former guards who may have some information.

    Guard Change

    The group heads to Middle Tacvick's and seek out the House Deneith enclave, knowing that they deal is guards and bounty hunters. There they see a pair of message boards with jobs available and guards looking for work. A name stands out to them, Kavton Bouldersight, as one that was given to them by the ir'Bellonas as a former guard. They hunt him down at a nearby tavern and buy him, and his two friends, a round of drinks. Find him already deep in his cups, they don't have much trouble getting him to give them some information for more booze and some coin. He draws them a rough map of the Forgeworks, identifies a secret entrance, a trap that they can disarm, and finally the guard rotation. He also describes the guard uniform to them.

    The group decides not to wait and heads out to the Cogs. They find the Forgeworks and spend some time scoping it out, with Draconus tailing the guards on patrol and getting a lay of the building. He hears the distinct sounds of dogs barking coming from one window and hammers hitting metal from another. Once he gives the all clear, the crew uses the secret entrance to get into the facility. Through a combination of having Lizzard McGuire sneak under the doors and listening for the guards they carefully make their way through the facility. The find a lot of valuable items but due to time and not wanting to tip their hand they decide to leave the items until their exit (they will come to regret this). They only have one run in with a guard and Cligs is able to put them to Sleep and then the others are able to tie them up and stash them out of the way.

    Smarter than the average bear

    Heading into the room that had been marked as "Testing Grounds" they find many types of animals kept in cages, that immediately shy back into their cages when they see people. Cassian uses a potion of animal speaking to gain the ability to talk to the animals. He finds a brown bear and asks what has been done to them. The bear, Yogi, tells them that they have been testing weapons on them, most recently a metal rod that seems to shoot ice out of it. Cassian tells the animals that he wants to free them, he is able to get most of them out through the window but Yogi is too big, so he travels with the group.

    The party heads up to the second floor of the facility. They decide to ignore the room that was indicated as the Faldren's Offices and head to the two unknown rooms. They enter the room on the left of the hallway first and find 4 large safes. They are able to determine that these are trapped in some way and so decide to leave them for now. They also find a secret entrance in one of the walls that takes them across to the other unknown room. They decide to take this.

    Smash and Grab

    In this room they fin 5 metal pedestals, each with a glass case covering them and a weapon inside of them. They also see a blacksmiths workstation setup in the center of the room and two foreboding statues in the far corners. Snailor Swift scouts the 5 pedestals and lets them know that they each have a notecard which describes what they are:

    1. Rapier of Wounding. When you hit a creature with an attack using this magic weapon, the target takes an extra 2d6 Necrotic damage and must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be unable to regain Hit Points for 1 hour. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
    2. Mithral Chain Shirt. Mithral is a light, flexible metal. A mithral chain shirt or breastplate can be worn under normal clothes.If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.
    3. Glaive Sharpness. When you attack an object with this magic weapon and hit, maximize your weapon damage dice against the target. When you attack a creature with this weapon and roll a 20 on the d20 for the attack roll, that target takes an extra 14 Slashing damage and gains 1 Exhaustion level.
    4. Flail of Warning. As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you and allies within 30 feet of you gain the following benefits. Alarm. The weapon magically awakens each subject who is sleeping naturally when combat begins. This benefit doesn't wake a subject from magically induced sleep. Supernatural Readiness. Each subject has Advantage on its Initiative rolls.
    5. Metal Rod. The final pedestal is labeled as currently being refined.

    The group decides to have Cligs' mage hand lift the glass case covering the metal rod and take that. If they don't die, they can quickly grab the other 4 items and make a break for it - so they each position near one of the pedestals. As soon as Cligs lifts the glass case an earsplitting alarm sounds throughout the building and, at the same time, the two statues begin to wake up. Cassian grabs the glass case covering the rapier of wounding and grabs the blade and they all start to make a run for it.

    The two Iron Sentries, now fully active, begin attacking the group and using their Magnetic Pull to try and prevent them from leaving. They can also hear the sounds of guards closing in on them. When Cassian and Yogi make it to the hallway they can see a trio of guards coming from one direction and know that others wait for them on the balcony. Yogi decides to attack the guards in the hallway to distract them.

    An offer

    One by one the party makes their escape from the Iron Sentries and on to the balcony, only to be confronted by a pair of guards and Kelvin ir'Faldren, the patriarch of the family. He congratulates them on making into the vault and stealing the rod, not just anyone could have done so. He makes them a deal, if they return the rod and provide evidence that gets whomever sent them thrown in jail, he will double the fee they were promised.

    The group discusses it and decides they can't win an outright fight here but they also don't want to implicate Cassian's parents. They decide to pin the heist on the Boromar Clan. Cassian is able to trick Kelvin in believing that Boromar is behind this, but seeing as that wouldn't lead to an arrest he lets them go in exchange for the rod - which they return.

    Dissapointment

    Returning to his parents, Cassian relays a version of what happened to his parents but leaves out the deal they made. He simply says it wasn't possible to steal it. Luckily, his parents believe his story but they are thoroughly disappointed in this failure. They tell him they are leaving Sharn to return home and do not give the group their reward. Cassian does not give them any of the items they took from the Forgeworks.
